<h2 id="classpass">ClassPass: cancel any time</h2>

With ClassPass you get credits every month for classes and gym visits at studios across Lisbon. You are not tied down: you cancel whenever you want and keep training until the end of your month.

- **Plans in Lisbon:** the standard plan is 37 credits for €59 a month. There are smaller and bigger plans in the app. See [ClassPass credits explained](/guides/classpass-credits/).
- **Try it first:** 14 days free with 10 workouts. Read [Trying ClassPass for free](/guides/classpass-free-trial/).
- **Pausing:** when you cancel, ClassPass often offers a free pause. Read [Cancelling or pausing ClassPass](/guides/cancel-or-pause-classpass/).
- **Travelling a lot?** Your plan also works in other cities. Read [ClassPass while travelling](/guides/classpass-while-travelling/).

<h2 id="gyms">Gyms without a contract</h2>

Many gym chains in Portugal offer plans with a minimum term (often 12 months) and plans without one ("sem fidelização"), which cost a bit more per month. Element Gyms has 56 gyms in Portugal, several of them in Lisbon (for example Parque das Nações), and works without a contract. With the Classic plan you train at 24 gyms for €5.99 a week.

<h2 id="drop-in">Drop-in classes and class packs</h2>

Many yoga, pilates and boxing studios sell single classes or a pack of classes. You only pay when you go, which is handy if you have one favourite studio. Check the studio's website; prices and validity differ.

<h2 id="tips">Tips if you are new in Portugal</h2>

- **"Sem fidelização" is what you're looking for.** It means no minimum term. "Com fidelização" means you sign up for a fixed period.
- **Websites in Portuguese.** Many gym sign-up pages are only in Portuguese. Your browser can translate them.
- **Other fees.** Check for a joining fee, insurance, a yearly fee or an access card.
- **Cancelling.** Stopping going or cancelling the direct debit does not end the contract. Follow the cancellation steps in your contract and keep proof.
- **Signing up online.** If you sign up online, you usually have 14 days to withdraw without giving a reason.
- **How often you go.** If you train three times a week or more at the same place, a plan with a minimum term is often cheaper. Read [Gym pass or membership?](/guides/gym-pass-or-membership/)

## Frequently asked questions

<details open>
<summary>Can I join a gym in Lisbon without a contract?</summary>

Yes. With ClassPass you cancel any time, there are gyms without a minimum term, and many studios sell drop-in classes.

</details>

<details>
<summary>What is the cheapest way to work out without a contract?</summary>

That depends on how often you go. For a few classes a month, a small ClassPass plan or a drop-in class is cheapest. If you often train at one place, a gym without a contract is usually cheaper per visit.

</details>

<details>
<summary>I am only in Lisbon for a few months. What should I choose?</summary>

Pick something you can cancel quickly. ClassPass has no notice period, so you can stop at the end of any month. With a gym, check how to cancel and do it in time before you leave.

</details>
