Don't want to be tied to one gym for a year? In Luxembourg several gym chains offer memberships you pay per month or per four weeks. Here's an overview of the flexible options, with what they cost and where you'll find them.

<p class="klein">ClassPass doesn't have partner studios in Luxembourg yet. As soon as it does, you'll find it here too.</p>

### Basic-Fit

The biggest budget chain in Luxembourg, with clubs in and around the city: Luxembourg City, Gasperich, Strassen, Windhof, Bereldange, Sandweiler, Foetz, Bettembourg, Belvaux and Ettelbruck. Most clubs open early and close late.

| Membership (Flex) | Per four weeks | What you get |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Comfort | €34.98 | All clubs in Luxembourg |
| Premium | €39.98 | All clubs in Europe, bring a friend once a week, massage chairs |
| Ultimate | €44.98 | All clubs in Europe, bring a friend anytime, massage chairs, sports water, free freeze |

On top of that you pay a one-off €19.99 subscription fee. With a yearly contract each membership is €9.99 cheaper per four weeks, but you're tied for a year.

### JIMS Fitness

A Belgian gym chain, also in Luxembourg, with group classes and separate zones for boxing and functional training. You choose between a membership per four weeks or a cheaper yearly plan, and you can pause your membership.

### Fitness Zone (Howald)

A single gym in Howald with a monthly membership and group classes. Students pay a little less.

<p class="klein">Prices can differ per club and change during promotions. Always check the current price on the gym's own website.</p>

<h2 id="tips">Before you sign up</h2>

1. **Check the notice period.** "Monthly" often means you cancel at least one month or four weeks in advance.
2. **Look at the joining fee.** Some gyms charge a one-off fee, others drop it during promotions.
3. **Try it first.** Many gyms offer a trial session or a first week at a lower price.
4. **Live across the border?** If you live in Belgium, France or Germany and work in Luxembourg, ClassPass does work in your home country. See [Belgium](/be/), [France](/fr/) or [Germany](/de/).

## Frequently asked questions

<details open>
<summary>Is ClassPass available in Luxembourg?</summary>

Not yet. ClassPass has no partner studios in Luxembourg at the moment. It does work in neighbouring Belgium, France and Germany.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Which gym in Luxembourg has no long contract?</summary>

Basic-Fit and JIMS Fitness offer memberships per four weeks, and Fitness Zone in Howald has a monthly membership. Check the notice period before you sign up.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Can I use my Basic-Fit membership from another country in Luxembourg?</summary>

With a membership that includes access to all clubs, you can also train at Basic-Fit clubs abroad. Check on the Basic-Fit website which membership includes this.

</details>
