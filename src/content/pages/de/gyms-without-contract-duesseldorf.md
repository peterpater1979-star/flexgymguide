<h2 id="classpass">ClassPass: cancel any time</h2>

With ClassPass you get credits every month for classes and gym visits at studios all over Düsseldorf. You are not tied down: you cancel whenever you want and keep training until the end of your month.

Most studios are in the city centre, Pempelfort, Unterbilk, Flingern and Oberkassel, with lots of reformer pilates and barre, and outdoor bootcamps in the Rheinpark. The same plan works in Neuss, Meerbusch and Wuppertal. A selection per neighbourhood is on [ClassPass in Düsseldorf](/de/classpass-duesseldorf/) (in German).

- **Plans in Düsseldorf:** the standard plan is 42 credits for €59 a month. There are smaller and bigger plans in the app. See [ClassPass credits explained](/guides/classpass-credits/).
- **Try it first:** one month free with 10 workouts. Read [Trying ClassPass for free](/guides/classpass-free-trial/).
- **Travelling a lot?** Your plan also works in other cities. Read [ClassPass while travelling](/guides/classpass-while-travelling/).

<h2 id="fitness-first">Fitness First: membership without a minimum term</h2>

Fitness First has 5 clubs in Düsseldorf, and you can join without a minimum term and cancel monthly. Prices are per week and differ per club. This is what they looked like in October 2026 at two clubs in Düsseldorf:

| Term | RED BASIC (Kö-Galerie) | RED ALL-IN (Kö-Galerie) | BLACK (Derendorf) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 month | €14.90 | €16.90 | €26.90 |
| 12 months | €11.90 | €13.90 | €20.90 |
| 24 months | €9.90 | €11.90 | €18.90 |

Students, pupils and trainees pay less: at the BLACK club Derendorf im Lighthouse €23.90 per week without a minimum term, €17.90 with 12 months and €15.90 with 24 months. At the RED club In der Kö-Galerie it was €14.90 without a minimum term, €11.90 with 12 months and €9.90 with 24 months.

Without a minimum term you also pay a startup fee and a training package; how RED, BLACK, BASIC and ALL-IN work is explained in [Gyms without a contract in Germany](/de/gyms-without-contract/#fitness-first). Not sure? Book a free trial session first.

<h2 id="mcfit">McFit Flex: cancel monthly</h2>

McFit has several gyms in Düsseldorf. With Flex you pay €49.90 a month and cancel monthly; with a 12 or 24-month contract it is cheaper. Prices and the first-month fee are explained in [Gyms without a contract in Germany](/de/gyms-without-contract/#mcfit).

<h2 id="other">Other gyms in Düsseldorf with a flexible plan</h2>

- **FitX:** 4 gyms (Derendorf, Flingern, Holthausen and Lörick), open 24 hours, €29 a month with a 12-month minimum term.
- **JOHN REED:** 3 clubs, Flex from €60 to €80 a month, cancel monthly.
- **Holmes Place:** 3 premium clubs; ask about the terms during a visit.

## Frequently asked questions

<details open>
<summary>Can I join a gym in Düsseldorf without a contract?</summary>

Yes. With ClassPass you cancel any time, Fitness First and McFit have memberships without a long term, and many studios sell drop-in classes.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Where in Düsseldorf can I do reformer pilates without a contract?</summary>

With ClassPass, at studios in the city centre, Pempelfort, Unterbilk and Oberkassel, among others. A selection is on [ClassPass in Düsseldorf](/de/classpass-duesseldorf/) (in German).

</details>

<details>
<summary>I am only in Düsseldorf for a few months. What should I choose?</summary>

Pick something you can cancel quickly. ClassPass has no notice period, so you can stop at the end of any month. With a monthly gym membership, check the notice period and any startup fee, and cancel in time before you leave.

</details>
