Cologne has a lively ClassPass scene with lots of barre, yoga, martial arts and dance from salsa to pole, Fitness First has 14 clubs with a monthly membership, and McFit and Basic-Fit are cheaper options. <h2 id="classpass">ClassPass: cancel any time</h2>

With ClassPass you get credits every month for classes and gym visits at studios all over Cologne. You are not tied down: you cancel whenever you want and keep training until the end of your month.

Most studios are in the Belgian Quarter, the city centre, the Südstadt and Sülz. Cologne has a lot of barre, outdoor classes in the Stadtwald and Mediapark, and dance classes from salsa and bachata to hip hop and pole dance. A selection per neighbourhood is on [ClassPass in Cologne](/de/classpass-koeln/) (in German).

- **Plans in Cologne:** the standard plan is 42 credits for €59 a month. There are smaller and bigger plans in the app. See [ClassPass credits explained](/guides/classpass-credits/).
- **Try it first:** one month free with 10 workouts. Read [Trying ClassPass for free](/guides/classpass-free-trial/).
- **Travelling a lot?** Your plan also works in other cities. Read [ClassPass while travelling](/guides/classpass-while-travelling/).

<h2 id="fitness-first">Fitness First: membership without a minimum term</h2>

Fitness First has 14 clubs in Cologne, and you can join without a minimum term and cancel monthly. Prices are per week and differ per club. This is what they looked like in October 2026 at two clubs in Cologne:

| Term | RED BASIC (Breite Straße) | RED ALL-IN (Breite Straße) | BLACK (Mediapark) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 month | €14.90 | €16.90 | €25.90 |
| 12 months | €11.90 | €13.90 | €19.90 |
| 24 months | €9.90 | €11.90 | €17.90 |

Students, pupils and trainees pay less: at the BLACK club Mediapark €22.90 per week without a minimum term, €16.90 with 12 months and €14.90 with 24 months. At the RED club Breite Straße it was €14.90 without a minimum term, €11.90 with 12 months and €9.90 with 24 months.

Without a minimum term you also pay a startup fee and a training package; how RED, BLACK, BASIC and ALL-IN work is explained in [Gyms without a contract in Germany](/de/gyms-without-contract/#fitness-first). Not sure? Book a free trial session first.

<h2 id="mcfit">McFit Flex: cancel monthly</h2>

McFit has several gyms in Cologne. With Flex you pay €49.90 a month and cancel monthly; with a 12 or 24-month contract it is cheaper. Prices and the first-month fee are explained in [Gyms without a contract in Germany](/de/gyms-without-contract/#mcfit).

<h2 id="other">Other gyms in Cologne with a flexible plan</h2>

- **Basic-Fit:** clubs in Spichernstraße and Peter-Huppertz-Straße, Flex €9.99 more per 4 weeks than the yearly contract.
- **Holmes Place:** 1 premium club; ask about the terms during a visit.
- **Mrs.Sporty:** several women-only clubs; prices are set per club.

## Frequently asked questions

<details open>
<summary>Can I join a gym in Cologne without a contract?</summary>

Yes. With ClassPass you cancel any time, Fitness First and McFit have memberships without a long term, and many studios sell drop-in classes.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Can I take dance classes in Cologne without signing up at a dance school?</summary>

Yes. On ClassPass you can book single dance classes, for example salsa and bachata, hip hop, ballet, zumba or pole dance, without a membership at the school itself.

</details>

<details>
<summary>I am only in Cologne for a few months. What should I choose?</summary>

Pick something you can cancel quickly. ClassPass has no notice period, so you can stop at the end of any month. With a monthly gym membership, check the notice period and any startup fee, and cancel in time before you leave.

</details>
