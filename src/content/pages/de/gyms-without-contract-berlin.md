Just moved to Berlin, here for a semester, or not sure how long you will stay? Berlin is one of the easiest cities in Germany to train without a long contract: there are studios on ClassPass in almost every neighbourhood, Fitness First has 15 clubs with a membership you cancel monthly, and chains like McFit, FitX and JOHN REED have flexible plans too. New in Germany? Read [Gyms without a contract in Germany](/de/gyms-without-contract/) for the German rules on notice periods and fees.

<h2 id="classpass">ClassPass: cancel any time</h2>

With ClassPass you get credits every month for classes and gym visits at studios all over Berlin, from Kreuzberg to Charlottenburg. You are not tied down: you cancel whenever you want and keep training until the end of your month.

Most studios are in Mitte, Prenzlauer Berg, Kreuzberg, Friedrichshain and Charlottenburg, from Lagree and reformer pilates to boxing, rowing and indoor cycling. A selection per district is on [ClassPass in Berlin](/de/classpass-berlin/) (in German).

- **Plans in Berlin:** the standard plan is 42 credits for €59 a month. There are smaller and bigger plans in the app. See [ClassPass credits explained](/guides/classpass-credits/).
- **Try it first:** one month free with 10 workouts. Read [Trying ClassPass for free](/guides/classpass-free-trial/).
- **Travelling a lot?** Your plan also works in other cities. Read [ClassPass while travelling](/guides/classpass-while-travelling/).

<h2 id="fitness-first">Fitness First: membership without a minimum term</h2>

Fitness First has 15 clubs in Berlin, and you can join without a minimum term and cancel monthly. Prices are per week and differ per club. This is what they looked like in October 2026 at two clubs in Berlin:

| Term | RED BASIC (Schöneberg) | RED ALL-IN (Schöneberg) | BLACK (Gendarmenmarkt) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 month | €12.90 | €14.90 | €26.90 |
| 12 months | €9.90 | €11.90 | €20.90 |
| 24 months | €7.90 | €9.90 | €18.90 |

Students, pupils and trainees pay less: €12.90 per week without a minimum term at the RED club in Schöneberg, €23.90 at the BLACK club at Gendarmenmarkt.

Without a minimum term you also pay a startup fee and a training package; how RED, BLACK, BASIC and ALL-IN work is explained in [Gyms without a contract in Germany](/de/gyms-without-contract/#fitness-first). Not sure? Book a free trial session first.

<h2 id="mcfit">McFit Flex: cancel monthly</h2>

McFit has several gyms in Berlin. With Flex you pay €49.90 a month and cancel monthly; with a 12 or 24-month contract it is cheaper. Prices and the first-month fee are explained in [Gyms without a contract in Germany](/de/gyms-without-contract/#mcfit).

<h2 id="other">Other gyms in Berlin with a flexible plan</h2>

- **FitX:** more than ten gyms, €29 a month with a 12-month minimum term (then monthly), plus a €29 joining fee.
- **JOHN REED:** several clubs, Flex from €60 to €80 a month, cancel monthly.
- **Basic-Fit:** several clubs, Flex €9.99 more per 4 weeks than the yearly contract.
- **clever fit:** several gyms, most with a monthly plan.
- **Holmes Place:** 6 premium clubs in Berlin; ask about the terms during a visit.

## Frequently asked questions

<details open>
<summary>Can I join a gym in Berlin without a contract?</summary>

Yes. With ClassPass you cancel any time, Fitness First and McFit have memberships without a long term, and many studios sell drop-in classes.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Which part of Berlin is best for ClassPass?</summary>

If you live or work in Mitte, Prenzlauer Berg, Kreuzberg or Friedrichshain, you have many studios within walking or cycling distance. In the outer districts the choice is smaller, so check the map in the app before you sign up.

</details>

<details>
<summary>I am only in Berlin for a few months. What should I choose?</summary>

Pick something you can cancel quickly. ClassPass has no notice period, so you can stop at the end of any month. With a monthly gym membership, check the notice period and any startup fee, and cancel in time before you leave.

</details>
