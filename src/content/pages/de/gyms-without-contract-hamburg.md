Just moved to Hamburg, here for a semester, or not sure how long you will stay? You don't have to sign a long gym contract. In Hamburg you can use ClassPass at studios in Eppendorf, Eimsbüttel and Altona, join one of 17 Fitness First clubs on a monthly membership, or pick a flexible plan at FitX, EASYFITNESS or McFit. New in Germany? Read [Gyms without a contract in Germany](/de/gyms-without-contract/) for the German rules on notice periods and fees.

<h2 id="classpass">ClassPass: cancel any time</h2>

With ClassPass you get credits every month for classes and gym visits at studios all over Hamburg. You are not tied down: you cancel whenever you want and keep training until the end of your month.

Most studios are in Eppendorf, Eimsbüttel, Winterhude, Altona and the city centre, with a lot of pilates (Lagree, reformer and hot pilates), barre, yoga and bootcamps. A selection per neighbourhood is on [ClassPass in Hamburg](/de/classpass-hamburg/) (in German).

- **Plans in Hamburg:** the standard plan is 42 credits for €59 a month. There are smaller and bigger plans in the app. See [ClassPass credits explained](/guides/classpass-credits/).
- **Try it first:** one month free with 10 workouts. Read [Trying ClassPass for free](/guides/classpass-free-trial/).
- **Travelling a lot?** Your plan also works in other cities. Read [ClassPass while travelling](/guides/classpass-while-travelling/).

<h2 id="fitness-first">Fitness First: membership without a minimum term</h2>

Fitness First has 17 clubs in Hamburg, and you can join without a minimum term and cancel monthly. Prices are per week and differ per club. This is what they looked like in October 2026 at two clubs in Hamburg:

| Term | RED BASIC (Harburg) | RED ALL-IN (Harburg) | BLACK (Alsterufer) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 month | €12.90 | €14.90 | €25.90 |
| 12 months | €9.90 | €11.90 | €19.90 |
| 24 months | €7.90 | €9.90 | €17.90 |

Students, pupils and trainees pay less: €12.90 per week without a minimum term at the RED club in Harburg, €22.90 at the BLACK club at Alsterufer.

Without a minimum term you also pay a startup fee and a training package; how RED, BLACK, BASIC and ALL-IN work is explained in [Gyms without a contract in Germany](/de/gyms-without-contract/#fitness-first). Not sure? Book a free trial session first.

<h2 id="mcfit">McFit Flex: cancel monthly</h2>

McFit has several gyms in Hamburg. With Flex you pay €49.90 a month and cancel monthly; with a 12 or 24-month contract it is cheaper. Prices and the first-month fee are explained in [Gyms without a contract in Germany](/de/gyms-without-contract/#mcfit).

<h2 id="other">Other gyms in Hamburg with a flexible plan</h2>

- **FitX:** 4 gyms (Altona, Bahrenfeld, Steilshoop and the airport), open 24 hours, €29 a month with a 12-month minimum term.
- **EASYFITNESS:** 4 gyms, from €27.99 a month, with a Flex plan.
- **JOHN REED:** 2 clubs (Harburg and Wandsbek), Flex from €60 to €80 a month.
- **Basic-Fit:** 1 club in Barmbek, Flex €9.99 more per 4 weeks than the yearly contract.
- **Holmes Place:** 2 premium clubs; ask about the terms during a visit.

## Frequently asked questions

<details open>
<summary>Can I join a gym in Hamburg without a contract?</summary>

Yes. With ClassPass you cancel any time, Fitness First and McFit have memberships without a long term, and many studios sell drop-in classes.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Is there a 24-hour gym in Hamburg without a long contract?</summary>

FitX gyms in Hamburg are open 24 hours, but you start with a 12-month term. If you want to cancel monthly from the start, look at McFit Flex or EASYFITNESS Flex, and check the opening hours of the gym near you.

</details>

<details>
<summary>I am only in Hamburg for a few months. What should I choose?</summary>

Pick something you can cancel quickly. ClassPass has no notice period, so you can stop at the end of any month. With a monthly gym membership, check the notice period and any startup fee, and cancel in time before you leave.

</details>
