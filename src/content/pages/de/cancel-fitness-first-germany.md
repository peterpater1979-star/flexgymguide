You cancel Fitness First in Germany online, with the cancellation form on fitnessfirst.de. Fitness First confirms your cancellation by email, for the earliest possible date. When that is depends on the term and the notice period in your contract. The form is in German; your browser can translate it.

<h2 id="short">In short</h2>

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Where** | Online, with the form at fitnessfirst.de/kuendigung/antrag |
| **Form** | In writing ("Textform"): online, by email or by letter |
| **Notice period** | In your contract; what counts is when Fitness First receives your cancellation |
| **After the minimum term** | For contracts since March 2022: cancel monthly, with one month's notice (German law) |
| **Pause** | 4, 8 or 12 weeks, no fees |
| **Signed up online?** | 14-day right of withdrawal |

<h2 id="steps">How to cancel</h2>

1. **Open the cancellation form** at fitnessfirst.de/kuendigung/antrag. Before that, Fitness First first offers you a pause.
2. **Fill in the form:** your club, your membership number, first and last name and your email address. Then choose the type of cancellation: "Ordentliche Kündigung" (regular) or "Außerordentliche Kündigung" (extraordinary, for an important reason). Giving a reason is optional.
3. **Cancelling for an important reason,** such as health? You can upload proof as a photo (jpg or png, at most 2 MB).
4. **Click "Jetzt kündigen"** and keep the confirmation email. Check the date your membership ends.

According to Fitness First, you can withdraw your cancellation until the end of your membership. Watch out for cancellation services from other companies: cancelling directly with Fitness First is free.

<h2 id="when">When can you stop?</h2>

- **Regular cancellation.** You cancel with the notice period in your contract. Fitness First offers terms of 1, 12 and 24 months; the shorter the term, the higher the weekly price.
- **After the minimum term.** For contracts since March 2022, a contract no longer renews for a whole year. You can then cancel at any time with one month's notice.
- **Extraordinary cancellation.** According to the terms and conditions, you keep the right to cancel for an important reason. They don't say which reasons count. Moving to another city alone is usually not enough.
- **Withdrawal.** You can withdraw from a contract you signed online within 14 days, without a reason: with "Vertrag hier widerrufen" on the website, by email to service@fitnessfirst.de or by letter. Fitness First then refunds everything within 14 days. If you have already trained, you pay a pro-rata amount.

<h2 id="pause">Pause instead of cancelling?</h2>

Fitness First offers a pause of 4, 8 or 12 weeks. During the pause you pay no fees, and you can keep training with the Fitness First app. Note: your contract is extended by the length of the pause. You request the pause at fitnessfirst.de/kuendigung.

With a 24-month term, the plan description includes a free pause of up to 12 weeks per calendar year, which you can take at any time.

## Frequently asked questions

<details open>
<summary>What is the notice period at Fitness First in Germany?</summary>

It is in your contract; the terms and conditions don't give a fixed period. After the minimum term, for contracts since March 2022, you can cancel at any time with one month's notice.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Can I cancel Fitness First online?</summary>

Yes, with the cancellation form at fitnessfirst.de/kuendigung/antrag. You get a confirmation by email.

</details>

<details>
<summary>I am leaving Germany. Can I cancel early?</summary>

The terms only say you can cancel for an important reason; they don't list which reasons count. Ask Fitness First, choose "Außerordentliche Kündigung" in the form and upload proof, such as your deregistration certificate (Abmeldebestätigung).

</details>

<details>
<summary>Can I pause my membership?</summary>

Yes, for 4, 8 or 12 weeks, without fees. Your contract is extended by the pause. With a 24-month term, up to 12 weeks of pause per calendar year are included.

</details>
