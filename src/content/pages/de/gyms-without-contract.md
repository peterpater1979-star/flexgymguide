New in Germany, here for a semester, or simply not keen on a two-year contract? German gyms are known for long minimum terms, but there are good alternatives that you cancel monthly, or you only pay when you go. This guide explains the options and the German rules that are useful to know before you sign up.

<h2 id="urban-sports-club">Urban Sports Club: cancel monthly</h2>

With Urban Sports Club you pay a fixed price per month and check in at partner venues, for the gym, classes or other sports depending on your membership. The monthly membership can be cancelled monthly.

<h2 id="classpass">ClassPass: cancel any time</h2>

With ClassPass you get credits every month for classes and gym visits at studios in your city. You cancel any time before your renewal date, and in Germany you can try it first with one month free and 10 workouts. Your plan also works in other cities and countries. <h2 id="fitness-first">Fitness First: membership without a minimum term</h2>

Fitness First has 220 clubs in Germany, with equipment, a large class schedule and a wellness area, and a pool in some clubs. Besides a 24-month membership there is one for 12 months and one without a minimum term, which you cancel monthly. Fitness First charges per week, and the price depends on the club: there are RED and BLACK clubs. At a RED club you choose between BASIC and ALL-IN; ALL-IN includes classes, drinks and the wellness area, among other things.

Without a minimum term you pay a one-off startup fee of €39. On top of that comes a training package of €39 or €49, charged every six months. With 24 months there is no startup fee, and you can pause for free for up to 12 weeks. You can switch to the 24-month membership at any time. Students, pupils and trainees pay less. Prices per club are on our city pages, and you can book a free trial session first.

<h2 id="mcfit">McFit Flex: no fixed term</h2>

McFit has more than 230 gyms in Germany, Austria and Italy. With Flex you have no fixed term. This is what prices looked like in October 2026, per month:

| Plan | Price | Term |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Flex | €49.90 | No fixed term |
| 12 months Standard | €29.90 | 12 months |
| 24 months Standard | €24.90 | 24 months |

In the first month you pay €88.90 in total with Flex (total price according to McFit). So Flex costs almost twice as much as a 24-month contract, but you are not tied down. With every plan you can train at all McFit gyms.

<h2 id="drop-in">Drop-in classes and 10-class cards</h2>

Many yoga, pilates and boxing studios sell single classes or a card for a number of classes. You only pay when you go, which is handy if you have one favourite studio. Check the studio's website; prices and validity differ.

<h2 id="tips">Tips if you are new in Germany</h2>

- **Websites in German.** Many gym websites and sign-up pages are only in German. Your browser can translate them.
- **Per week, not per month.** Fitness First shows prices per week. Multiply by about 4.3 for a monthly amount.
- **One-off and recurring fees.** Many gyms charge a joining fee, and some charge a training or service package every few months.
- **Direct debit.** Gyms usually collect payment by SEPA direct debit, so have your IBAN ready.
- **Notice period.** "Monthly" often means one month's notice. Read the terms before you leave the country.
- **Cancelling online.** If you signed up online, the gym has to offer a cancel button on its website.
- **After the minimum term.** For contracts since March 2022, a yearly contract no longer renews for a whole year after the minimum term. You can then cancel with one month's notice.
- **How often you go.** If you train three times a week or more at the same place, a fixed contract is often cheaper. Read [Gym pass or membership?](/guides/gym-pass-or-membership/)

## Frequently asked questions

<details open>
<summary>Are there gyms in Germany without a contract?</summary>

Yes. Urban Sports Club, ClassPass, McFit Flex, Basic-Fit Flex and JOHN REED Flex all have options without a long term, and many studios sell drop-in classes or 10-class cards.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Can I cancel a German gym contract when I move abroad?</summary>

Not always straight away. Moving is not automatically a reason to end a contract early, so check the terms and the notice period before you sign. With ClassPass or a monthly plan you avoid the problem: you simply stop at the end of your month.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Can I combine ClassPass with a gym membership?</summary>

Yes. Many people train at a cheap gym and book a class through ClassPass now and then, for something their gym does not offer.

</details>
