<h2 id="classpass">ClassPass: cancel any time</h2>

With ClassPass you get credits every month for classes and gym visits at studios across Dublin. You are not tied down: you cancel whenever you want and keep training until the end of your month.

- **Plans in Dublin:** the standard plan is 33 credits for €49 a month. There are smaller and bigger plans in the app. See [ClassPass credits explained](/guides/classpass-credits/).
- **Try it first:** 14 days free with 10 workouts. Read [Trying ClassPass for free](/guides/classpass-free-trial/).
- **Pausing:** when you cancel, ClassPass often offers a free pause. Read [Cancelling or pausing ClassPass](/guides/cancel-or-pause-classpass/).
- **Travelling a lot?** Your plan also works in other cities. Read [ClassPass while travelling](/guides/classpass-while-travelling/).

<h2 id="flyefit">Flyefit: no contract</h2>

Flyefit is an Irish gym chain with 20 gyms in Dublin, one in Cork and one in Galway. Membership is contract-free: you are not tied to a minimum term. You choose between one gym and Flye Roaming, which gives access to all gyms.

| Membership | Monthly | Every 3 months | Per year |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Membership** (1 gym) | €38 | €114 | €355 |
| **Flye Roaming** (all gyms) | €41 | €123 | €395 |
| **Student, 1 gym** | €29 for the first 3 months, then €38 | €114 | €355 |
| **Student, Flye Roaming** | €41 | €123 | €395 |

- **Joining fee:** normally €25 (students €12.50). In October 2026 it was crossed out on the sign-up page, so you paid none.
- **Students:** show a valid student ID the first time you enter the gym, or your membership is cancelled. The €29 offer is for one gym and can't be moved to another location.
- **Pay as you go:** a day pass costs €14, two days €22, three days €29. Handy if you are only in Dublin for a short while.

<p class="klein">Source: Flyefit sign-up page (October 2026). Always check the current price with Flyefit.</p>

<h2 id="drop-in">Drop-in classes and class packs</h2>

Many yoga, pilates and boxing studios sell single classes or a pack of classes. You only pay when you go, which is handy if you have one favourite studio. Check the studio's website; prices and validity differ.

<h2 id="tips">What to watch for</h2>

- **Joining fees.** Many gyms charge a one-off joining fee, also without a contract. Sometimes there are sales with no joining fee.
- **How you cancel.** Check how much notice you need to give and how to cancel, so you don't pay an extra month.
- **Off-peak memberships.** Some gyms have cheaper memberships if you only train at quiet times.
- **How often you go.** If you train three times a week or more at the same place, a gym membership is often cheaper per visit. Read [Gym pass or membership?](/guides/gym-pass-or-membership/)

## Frequently asked questions

<details open>
<summary>Can I join a gym in Dublin without a contract?</summary>

Yes. With ClassPass you cancel any time, Flyefit works without a contract, and many studios sell drop-in classes.

</details>

<details>
<summary>What is the cheapest way to work out without a contract?</summary>

That depends on how often you go. For a few classes a month, a small ClassPass plan or a drop-in class is cheapest. If you often train at one place, a monthly gym membership is usually cheaper per visit.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Can I combine ClassPass and a gym?</summary>

Yes. Many people train at their gym and now and then book a class through ClassPass that their gym doesn't offer.

</details>
