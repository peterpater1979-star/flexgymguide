<h2 id="areas">By area</h2>

A few examples per area. There are more studios than the ones named here: click a workout type further down to see the full list on ClassPass.

### City centre (Dublin 2)

HIIT at Barry's on Dawson Street and Perpetua Fitness on Windmill Lane, cycling and HIIT at SAINT Studios and yoga with Yoga by Tata on South William Street, Pilates at Mouve on Fade Street and at Active Form Pilates, yoga and Pilates at Luuma on Fitzwilliam Square, The Space Between on Fenian Street, yogahub at Camden Court and Yoga Dublin on Richmond Street South, boxing fitness at BeatBox on Mount Street Upper, and karate and dance classes at the YMCA on Aungier Street.

### Portobello, Rathmines and Ranelagh

### Ballsbridge, Sandymount and Ringsend

### Stoneybatter, Smithfield and the north inner city

Yoga, Pilates and barre at The elbowroom and training at Stoneybatter Cross Training, F45 Training in Grangegorman, Bodyfirm Pilates on Prussia Street, yogahub on the North Circular Road and Brunswick Street North, jiu-jitsu at JS BJJ Academy on Parnell Street, martial arts at Vieira Martial Arts on North Lotts and HIIT at KoCoon Fitness in the Docklands.

### Clontarf and the northside

Training at Element Health Fitness Performance and The Edge Gym on Clontarf Road, Irish Fit Coach in Killester, yoga at The Yoga Lounge on Howth Road and boxing and cycling at FightR.

### Sandyford, Stillorgan and Dún Laoghaire

TRIB3 in Beacon South Quarter and Cherrywood, hot yoga at 360 Hot Yoga, HIIT at UN1T Dublin in Stillorgan, Cycle Studio on Merville Road, Yoga Lab Dublin on Corrig Road, PILAT3S in Cherrywood, barre at TheBarre.ie and yoga with Joanne King Yoga in Dún Laoghaire, and open-air yoga with James Yoga Flow in Sandycove.

<h2 id="tips">Tips</h2>

- Popular classes early in the morning and after work fill up fast. Book a few days ahead.
- Credits per class depend on the studio and the time of day. Off-peak classes are often cheaper.
- For reformer Pilates and barre you usually need grip socks. Arrive a bit early the first time.
- Several studios, like yogahub, Yoga Dublin and Perpetua Fitness, have more than one location: check you've picked the right one when you book.
- Some wellness and beauty places ask you to confirm your appointment by phone or through their own booking system first. Read the studio's notes before you book.
- Your plan also works in other Irish cities and abroad, wherever ClassPass is available.
