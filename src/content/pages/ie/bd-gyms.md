<h2 id="memberships">The memberships</h2>

| Membership | Price | How you pay |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Monthly** | €36 a month | Rolling, no contract |
| **6 months** | €199 | One payment upfront |
| **12 months** | €299 | One payment upfront |

- **Joining fee:** none, for every membership.
- **Monthly:** you pay the first month (€36) when you join; after that, €36 is collected every month.
- **Included:** full gym access, classes and free parking for up to 2 hours.
- **Not included:** personal training, Pilates, padel, physio and the barber and coffee shop at some gyms.
- **One gym:** your membership is for the gym where you join. You can't use other BD Gyms locations or move your membership.

<h2 id="classes">Classes</h2>

Classes are free with every membership. Examples are BD PowerCycle, BD HIIT Cycle, BD Strong, BD Circuit, BD Lift, BD Kettlebell, BD Bootcamp and HYROX. Each gym has its own timetable.

<h2 id="opening-hours">Opening hours</h2>

BD Gyms are open Monday to Friday from 6.00 to 22.00, and on Saturday, Sunday and bank holidays from 8.30 to 17.30. Last showers are 30 minutes before closing.

<h2 id="guest-pass">Guest pass</h2>

Only training once? A guest pass costs €10 a day at reception. You need to be 16 or older and bring ID.

<h2 id="cancel">Pausing and cancelling</h2>

- **Monthly:** cancel by email to BD Gyms' membership team at least two weeks before your next payment, and also tell your bank if you pay by direct debit. You can't cancel at reception. A missed payment cancels your membership automatically.
- **6 and 12 months:** paid upfront and not refundable, except within 14 days if you haven't used the gym.
- **Pausing:** only a 12-month membership can be paused, on medical grounds with a doctor's note, for up to 6 months. Monthly memberships can't be paused.

<h2 id="age">Minimum age</h2>

You need to be 16 and show ID, such as a passport, driving licence or Garda Age Card.

If you go to the same gym three times a week or more, BD Gyms is usually cheaper. If you want to switch between yoga, Pilates, boxing and spinning at different studios, or you train one to three times a week, a fitness pass like ClassPass often suits you better.
