Flyefit is an Irish gym chain with 22 gyms: most in Dublin, plus Cork and Galway. Membership is contract-free. You choose one gym or Flye Roaming for all gyms, and pay monthly, every three months or once a year. Classes are included.

<h2 id="memberships">The memberships</h2>

| | Monthly | Every 3 months | Per year |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Membership** (1 gym) | €38 | €114 | €355 |
| **Flye Roaming** (all gyms) | €41 | €123 | €395 |

- **Joining fee:** normally €25 for every option. Flyefit regularly runs an offer with no joining fee; in November 2026 the home page showed one.
- **Students:** a lower joining fee, and sometimes a lower price for the first months. Show a valid student ID the first time you go, or your membership is cancelled.
- **Switching gym:** moving a single-gym membership to another gym costs €20. You can upgrade to Flye Roaming in the MYFLYE app.

<h2 id="day-pass">Day passes</h2>

Only in Ireland for a short while? A day pass costs €14, two days €22 and three days €29, to use within 30 days. The sign-up page sometimes shows other prices, so check before you buy.

<h2 id="classes">Classes</h2>

All classes are free with a Flyefit membership. You book them in the MYFLYE app, up to 24 hours ahead. Cancel at least 30 minutes before the start, or you may not be able to book for a while. Flyefit also has online classes in FLYEPLUS. Personal trainers work independently and charge their own fees.

<h2 id="opening-hours">Opening hours</h2>

Flyefit gyms are open 364 days a year. Most open at 5.30 on weekdays and close between 22.00 and 23.30; at weekends they are open from 7.30 to 19.30. George's Street and Swords are open around the clock from Monday morning to Saturday evening. Check your gym's page for its exact hours.

<h2 id="cancel">Pausing and cancelling</h2>

- **Cancelling:** do it yourself in MYFLYE under My Account, at least 72 hours before your next payment. You keep access until your next renewal date. There is no other notice period. If you rejoin later, you pay the joining fee again.
- **Yearly membership:** you can cancel within 14 days for a refund if you haven't used the gym, by emailing Flyefit.
- **Pausing:** a monthly membership costs €5 a month while paused, and you can't use the gym. A yearly membership can be paused for up to 90 days in total.

<h2 id="age">Minimum age</h2>

You need to be 16. Between 16 and 18 you need consent from a parent.

<h2 id="classpass">Flyefit or ClassPass?</h2>

If you go to the same gym three times a week or more, Flyefit is usually cheaper. If you want to switch between yoga, pilates, boxing and spinning at different studios, or you train one to three times a week, a fitness pass like ClassPass often suits you better. Read [All about ClassPass](/ie/classpass/).

## Frequently asked questions

<details open>
<summary>Does Flyefit have a contract?</summary>

No. All memberships are contract-free. You cancel in the MYFLYE app at least 72 hours before your next payment.

</details>

<details>
<summary>How much is Flyefit?</summary>

€38 a month for one gym or €41 for all gyms with Flye Roaming, plus a joining fee of €25 unless there is an offer (November 2026).

</details>

<details>
<summary>Are classes included at Flyefit?</summary>

Yes. Classes in the gym and online classes in FLYEPLUS are included in every membership.

</details>
