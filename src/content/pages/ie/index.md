Don't want a long contract at a single gym? With a fitness pass like ClassPass, one membership gets you into different studios and gyms. Here's how it works in Ireland, and in which cities.

<h2 id="opties">Two ways to train flexibly</h2>

<div class="kolommen">

<div class="kolom kaart aanbiederkaart">

<div class="fotokop foto-classpass"><span>Fitness pass</span></div>

### ClassPass

A membership with credits for yoga, Pilates, boxing, spinning and gym visits at studios across the city, and abroad too.

- Good if you like variety
- Good if you train one to three times a week
- Also works in 30 other countries

### Flyefit

An Irish gym chain without a contract: 20 gyms in Dublin, plus Cork and Galway. From €38 a month for one gym.

- Good if you train three times a week or more
- Good if you want one regular place
- No minimum term

Four gyms in Dublin and one in Portlaoise. €36 a month, no contract and no joining fee.

- Good if you want a low-cost gym
- No joining fee

### Gym Plus

Seven clubs in Dublin, Cork, Ashbourne, Drogheda and Naas, most with a pool. €69 a month, or €59 in Cork.

- Good if you want a pool
- Good if you train three times a week or more

## Frequently asked questions

<details open>
<summary>How much does ClassPass cost in Ireland?</summary>

It depends on how many credits you get each month. You'll find the current prices at ClassPass.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Is ClassPass cheaper than a gym membership?</summary>

If you go to the same gym three times a week or more, a regular membership is usually cheaper. If you like switching between studios, a fitness pass suits you better.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Can I use ClassPass on holiday?</summary>

Yes. Your credits work in every country where ClassPass is available, at that country's rates.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Is there a PureGym in Ireland?</summary>

Not yet. PureGym plans its first Irish gym in Dublin city centre in early 2027. Read [PureGym Ireland](/ie/puregym/).

</details>
