You cancel a Snap Fitness monthly membership with 30 days' notice, in writing or at your gym. Your membership ends on the last day of the month that is at least 30 days after your notice.

<h2 id="summary">In short</h2>

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Where** | In writing or at your gym |
| **Notice** | 30 days; ends on the last day of the month after that |
| **Fixed term** | Stopping early costs an early cancellation fee |
| **Freeze** | Usually 1 to 3 months a year, for a small fee |

<h2 id="steps">How to cancel</h2>

1. **Check your membership:** monthly or a fixed term?
2. **Give notice in writing** or at your gym.
3. **Keep the confirmation** and check the end date.

<h2 id="when">Good to know</h2>

- Your end date is the last day of the month that is at least 30 days after your notice, so give notice early in the month.
- Cancelling your direct debit with your bank is not the same as cancelling your membership. Cancel with the gym first, then stop the payment.
