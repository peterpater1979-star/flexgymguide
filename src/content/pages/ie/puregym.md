PureGym is coming to Ireland. The chain has more than 700 gyms, among them in the UK, Denmark and Switzerland. Its first Irish gym is planned for Dublin city centre in early 2027. Until then, there is no PureGym open in Ireland.

<h2 id="short">What we know so far</h2>

| | PureGym Ireland |
| --- | --- |
| **Status** | Coming soon, no gym open yet |
| **First gym** | Dublin city centre, early 2027 |
| **Address** | Not announced yet |
| **Price** | Not confirmed yet |
| **Contract** | PureGym says its Irish memberships will be without a contract |
| **Opening hours** | Not confirmed for Ireland |

<p class="klein">Source: PureGym Ireland website and The Irish Times (July 2026). We update this page as soon as PureGym announces the address and prices.</p>

<h2 id="until-then">Where to train until then</h2>

- **One gym, no contract:** [Flyefit](/ie/flyefit/) is an Irish chain with gyms across Dublin, plus Cork and Galway. Read more in [Gyms without a contract in Dublin](/ie/gyms-without-contract-dublin/).
- **Variety:** with [ClassPass](/ie/classpass/) you book yoga, Pilates, boxing and gym visits at studios across [Dublin](/ie/dublin/) and [Cork](/ie/cork/).

<h2 id="abroad">PureGym in other countries</h2>

Read how PureGym works in the [United Kingdom](/uk/puregym/), [Denmark](/dk/puregym/) and [Switzerland](/ch/puregym/). Plans and prices in Ireland may be different.
