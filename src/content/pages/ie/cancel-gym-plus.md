You cancel Gym Plus with the cancellation form on the Gym Plus website. A monthly membership has a minimum term of 3 months. After that, you cancel at any time with 30 days' notice.

<h2 id="summary">In short</h2>

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Where** | Cancellation form on the Gym Plus website |
| **Minimum term** | 3 months |
| **Notice** | 30 days |
| **Freeze** | 1 to 3 months in a row, €10 a month, with 15 days' written notice |

<h2 id="steps">How to cancel</h2>

1. **Check that your 3 months are up.**
2. **Fill in the cancellation form** on the Gym Plus website.
3. **Count 30 days' notice** and keep the confirmation.

<h2 id="when">Good to know</h2>

- You can keep using the club until your membership ends.
- Just need a break? You can freeze for 1 to 3 months in a row, for €10 a month.
