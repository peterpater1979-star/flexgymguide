You cancel a BD Gyms monthly membership by email to the membership team, at least two weeks before your next payment. If you pay by direct debit, also tell your bank. You can't cancel at reception.

<h2 id="summary">In short</h2>

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Where** | By email to BD Gyms' membership team, not at reception |
| **Deadline** | At least two weeks before your next payment |
| **Monthly** | Rolling, no contract |
| **6 and 12 months** | Paid upfront, not refundable after 14 days or once used |
| **Pause** | Only 12-month memberships, on medical grounds, up to 6 months |

<h2 id="steps">How to cancel</h2>

1. **Email the membership team** at least two weeks before your next payment.
2. **Tell your bank** if you pay by direct debit.
3. **Keep the confirmation.**

<h2 id="when">Good to know</h2>

- A missed payment cancels your membership automatically, but cancelling by email is the clean way to do it.
- 6 and 12-month memberships are paid upfront. They're only refundable within 14 days, if you haven't used the gym.
