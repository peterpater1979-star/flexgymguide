## ClassPass in Cork

With ClassPass you get monthly credits to book classes and gym visits at studios across the city. Ideal if you like variety and don't want to commit to one place.

### Where

A small choice, in the city centre, Shandon, Ballintemple and Douglas. Mostly strength training and gyms, also boxing.

### What you get

- Monthly credits for classes and gym visits
- Standard plan: 33 credits for €49 a month
- 14 days free with 10 workouts to try it
- Use it in more than 2,500 other cities in 31 countries

### Watch out for

- Credits per class vary by studio and time of day
- Unused credits roll over for one month, then they expire

## Flyefit in Cork

Flyefit has one gym in Cork, plus 20 in Dublin and one in Galway. With Flye Roaming you can use all of them. Membership is contract-free: €38 a month for one gym, or €41 for all gyms with Flye Roaming. Students pay €29 for the first 3 months at one gym.

### Good for

- Training three times a week or more
- Students and anyone who wants no contract

### Watch out for

- Students show a valid student ID on their first visit
- The joining fee is normally €25 (students €12.50); in October 2026 it was waived

## Frequently asked questions

<details open>
<summary>Is ClassPass cheaper than a gym in Cork?</summary>

It depends on how often you train and what you want to do. If you mostly use the gym floor, a gym membership is usually cheaper. If you want to mix studios and classes, a fitness pass is usually the better fit.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Can I use ClassPass outside Cork?</summary>

Yes. You can use your credits in other Irish cities and abroad, wherever ClassPass is available.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Does Flyefit have a contract?</summary>

No. Flyefit memberships are contract-free. You can also pay monthly, every 3 months or per year.

</details>
