You cancel West Wood Club at your club's reception. After your minimum contract period, you give 60 days' notice.

<h2 id="summary">In short</h2>

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Where** | At your club's reception |
| **Minimum term** | Minimum contract period, set in your contract |
| **Notice** | 60 days |
| **Freeze** | For medical or special reasons, with a small admin fee |

<h2 id="steps">How to cancel</h2>

1. **Check when your minimum contract period ends.**
2. **Cancel at reception** of your club, and ask for a written confirmation.
3. **Count 60 days' notice.**

<h2 id="when">Good to know</h2>

- 60 days is longer than at most budget gyms. Give notice in good time.
- Cancelling in writing, by email or letter, always gives you proof of the date. Keep a copy and the confirmation.
