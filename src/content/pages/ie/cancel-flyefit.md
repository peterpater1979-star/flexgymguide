You cancel Flyefit yourself in the MYFLYE app, under My Account, at least 72 hours before your next payment. There's no other notice period, and you keep access until your next renewal date.

<h2 id="summary">In short</h2>

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Where** | MYFLYE app, under My Account |
| **Deadline** | At least 72 hours before your next payment |
| **Contract** | None: all memberships are contract-free |
| **Access** | Until your next renewal date |
| **Pause** | €5 a month on a monthly membership; up to 90 days in total on a yearly one |
| **Rejoining** | You pay the joining fee again |

<h2 id="steps">How to cancel</h2>

1. **Open the MYFLYE app** and go to My Account.
2. **Cancel your membership**, at least 72 hours before your next payment.
3. **Keep the confirmation.** You can keep training until your next renewal date.

<h2 id="good-to-know">Good to know</h2>

- **Yearly membership.** You can cancel within 14 days for a refund if you haven't used the gym, by emailing Flyefit.
- **Pause instead?** A monthly membership costs €5 a month while paused, and you can't use the gym. A yearly membership can be paused for up to 90 days in total.
- **Rejoining later?** You pay the joining fee again (€25, students €12.50).
