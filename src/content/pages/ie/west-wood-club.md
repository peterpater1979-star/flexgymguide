West Wood Club is a group of six health clubs in Dublin. Besides a gym, the clubs have swimming pools, group classes and spa facilities, and several have tennis, padel or squash. Group classes are included in every membership. Unlike a budget gym, West Wood Club has a minimum contract period.

<h2 id="memberships">Memberships</h2>

West Wood Club has memberships for adults, couples, families, juniors, teens, students and over-60s, plus Off-peak and Corporate options. You join at one club. With a **Global** membership you can use all six clubs.

- **Students:** a lower rate for full-time students under 25. Bring your student ID and passport.
- **Over 60s:** a lower rate with photo ID.
- **Paying:** most members pay by monthly direct debit; you can also pay for a year upfront.
- **Changing:** you can upgrade or downgrade your membership at reception.

West Wood Club doesn't list its prices online. Ask your club for the current rate and the joining fee before you sign up.

<h2 id="classes">Classes and facilities</h2>

Group classes such as Les Mills, yoga, Pilates, spinning, aquafit and BoxFit are free with every membership. Depending on the club you also get swimming pools, a spa with sauna, steam room and jacuzzi, tennis, padel or squash, a ladies-only gym and a teen gym. There is childcare and swimming lessons for children, sometimes at an extra cost.

<h2 id="opening-hours">Opening hours</h2>

All clubs are open Monday to Friday from 6.00 to 23.00. At weekends and on bank holidays they open from 8.00 to 21.00, Aston Quay from 8.00 to 20.00. Facilities close half an hour earlier.

<h2 id="trial">Free trial</h2>

West Wood Club offers a free 4-day trial for adults aged 18 and over. You choose a club when you sign up. Conditions apply, so read them before you start.

<h2 id="cancel">Freezing and cancelling</h2>

- **Cancelling:** after your minimum contract period, you need to give 60 days' notice. You cancel at your club's reception.
- **Freezing:** possible for medical or special reasons. You pay a small admin fee while your membership is frozen.
- **Rejoining:** you pay a new joining fee, although former members sometimes get a rejoin offer.

<h2 id="age">Minimum age</h2>

You can join from age 13. Members under 18 have to do an induction first. There are junior memberships for families with children.
