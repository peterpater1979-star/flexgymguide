Just moved to Vienna, here for a semester, or not sure where you will live next year? You don't have to sign a one-year gym contract. These are the options to work out month by month, or only when you go.

<h2 id="short">In short</h2>

| | Cancelling | Price | Good for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **ClassPass** | Any time, no notice period | Standard plan 35 credits for €49 a month; smaller plans in the app | Variety at studios all over the city |
| **Fitness First** | Monthly, or cheaper with 12 months | BASIS €11.90, ALL-IN €13.90 a week without a minimum term | Gym with classes and wellness |
| **Drop-in class or class pack** | Not needed | Varies per studio | Now and then at a favourite studio |

<p class="klein">Prices from October 2026. ClassPass: location Vienna. Fitness First: club Wien Landstraße; all clubs in Vienna have the same prices. Always check the current price with the provider.</p>

<h2 id="classpass">ClassPass: cancel any time</h2>

With ClassPass you get credits every month for classes and gym visits at studios across Vienna, from Mariahilf to the Innere Stadt. You are not tied down: you cancel whenever you want and keep training until the end of your month.

- **Plans in Vienna:** the standard plan is 35 credits for €49 a month. There are smaller and bigger plans in the app. See [ClassPass credits explained](/guides/classpass-credits/).
- **Try it first:** 14 days free with 10 workouts. The workouts expire at the end of the trial. Read [Trying ClassPass for free](/guides/classpass-free-trial/).
- **Pausing:** when you cancel, ClassPass often offers a free pause. Read [Cancelling or pausing ClassPass](/guides/cancel-or-pause-classpass/).
- **Travelling a lot?** Your plan also works in other cities. Read [ClassPass while travelling](/guides/classpass-while-travelling/).

<h2 id="fitness-first">Fitness First</h2>

Fitness First has 4 clubs in and around Vienna, including Vösendorf, with a gym floor, classes and a wellness area. All clubs in Vienna are RED clubs, so the prices are the same everywhere. Fitness First charges per week. You choose between BASIS and ALL-IN; ALL-IN includes classes, unlimited drinks, massage at a fixed price and the wellness area. These were the prices in October 2026, per week:

| Term | BASIS | ALL-IN | Students, pupils, trainees |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 month | €11.90 | €13.90 | €11.90 |
| 12 months | €8.90 | €10.90, first 12 weeks free | €8.90, first 12 weeks free |

The 12 free weeks were an offer until 27 October 2026. The student plan includes what ALL-IN offers. Not sure yet? Book a free trial session first.

<p class="klein">Source: the Wien Landstraße club page on fitnessfirst.at (October 2026). Always check the current price with the club.</p>

<h2 id="drop-in">Drop-in classes and class packs</h2>

Many yoga, pilates and boxing studios in Vienna sell single classes or packs of classes. You only pay when you go, which is handy if you have one favourite studio. Check the studio's website; prices and validity differ.

<h2 id="tips">Tips</h2>

- **Joining fees.** Some gyms charge a one-off joining fee, also on monthly plans.
- **Notice period.** "Monthly" often means one month's notice. Read the terms.
- **Minimum terms.** A 12-month membership is cheaper per week, but you usually can't leave before the end of the term.
- **How often you go.** If you train three times a week or more at the same place, a gym membership is often cheaper per visit. Read [Gym pass or membership?](/guides/gym-pass-or-membership/)

## Frequently asked questions

<details open>
<summary>Can I join a gym in Vienna without a contract?</summary>

Yes. With ClassPass you cancel any time, Fitness First has a membership with a one-month term, and many studios sell drop-in classes.

</details>

<details>
<summary>What is the cheapest way to work out without a contract?</summary>

That depends on how often you go. For a few classes a month, a small ClassPass plan or a drop-in class is cheapest. If you often train at one place, a membership is usually cheaper per visit.

</details>

<details>
<summary>I am in Vienna for one semester. What should I choose?</summary>

Pick something you can cancel quickly. ClassPass has no notice period, and Fitness First has a one-month membership with a lower student price.

</details>
