New members can try ClassPass first. You visit a number of studios and gyms, each one once. How long the trial lasts and exactly what you get differs per country and per offer. You see it when you sign up.

<h2 id="summary">In short</h2>

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Who for** | New members only, once per person |
| **What you get** | A first visit at participating studios, or a set number of credits or bookings |
| **How long** | Differs per offer; you see it when you sign up |
| **Free?** | Often, but in some areas it's a discount on your first period |
| **Afterwards** | You move automatically to the monthly plan you saw when you signed up |

<h2 id="rules">The rules</h2>

- **Once per location.** You can visit each studio or gym once. If you book a class and cancel it, you can't book there again during the trial.
- **Cancellation fees still apply.** If you cancel late or don't show up, you pay the usual fee, also during the trial.
- **Extra credits.** Some trial offers let you buy extra credits, for example to visit a studio again or for a spa or salon appointment.
- **Upgrading early.** Some offers let you switch to a monthly plan straight away. Then you can also go back to the same studio.

<h2 id="end">When the trial ends</h2>

You get a reminder email just before the end. If you don't cancel, your account moves automatically to the monthly plan you saw when you signed up. Want a smaller or bigger plan? Change it in your account settings before your next billing date.

Credits you bought yourself carry over to your plan, up to the number of credits in that plan. Your visit history starts again, so after the trial you can go back to the same studio.

<h2 id="cancel">Cancelling during the trial</h2>

Cancelling during the trial takes effect immediately. You lose your trial credits and all your booked classes. Want to use the whole trial? Then only cancel on the last day. Read how in [Cancel or pause ClassPass](/guides/cancel-or-pause-classpass/).

<h2 id="tips">How to get the most out of it</h2>

1. **Set a reminder** a few days before the end, so you can decide in time.
2. **Book ahead.** Popular classes fill up fast, especially in the evening and at weekends.
3. **Try different kinds of classes**, such as yoga, cycling, strength and dance. That way you find out what suits you.
4. **Use your one visit per studio well.** Pick the class you're most curious about.
5. **Look beyond your own area.** In the app you can search by type of class, rating and time.

<h2 id="checklist">Checklist</h2>

- [ ] When you sign up, check how long the trial lasts and which plan starts afterwards
- [ ] Set a reminder for the last day
- [ ] Book your first classes straight away
- [ ] Decide before the end: carry on, choose another plan or cancel

## Frequently asked questions

<details open>
<summary>Is the trial really free?</summary>

Often, yes. In some areas it's a discount on your first period instead of a free trial. You see what you get when you sign up.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Can I try ClassPass for free more than once?</summary>

No. The trial is for new members only, and everyone can use it once.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Does ClassPass have a student discount?</summary>

No. ClassPass has no student discount. Some employers do offer ClassPass through a corporate plan.

</details>
