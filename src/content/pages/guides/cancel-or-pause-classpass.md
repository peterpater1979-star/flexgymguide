You can cancel ClassPass at any time, with no fee and no notice period. Do it at least 48 hours before your renewal date, and use your credits first: they are gone once your membership ends.

<h2 id="short">In short</h2>

| | Cancel | Pause | Smaller plan |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **How** | Settings in the app or on the website | When you cancel, ClassPass often offers a pause; otherwise ask via chat | Account, then Manage plan |
| **When** | At least 48 hours before your renewal date | Two months, from your next renewal date | From your next renewal date |
| **Cost** | Free | Free: you switch to ClassPass Lite at 0 a month | The lower monthly price |
| **Your credits** | Lost when your membership ends, rollover credits too | Not usable during the pause, available again afterwards | The usual rollover rules |

<h2 id="cancel">How to cancel</h2>

**On the website**

1. Click your initial at the top right and open **Settings**.
2. Click the red cancel link.
3. Follow the steps until you see the confirmation.

**In the app**

1. Go to **Profile**, then **Settings**.
2. Under **Billing**, tap the cancel option.
3. Follow the steps until you see the confirmation.

Along the way, ClassPass first shows you offers, such as a free two-month pause or a discount on the next few months (for example 20% off for three months). If you really want to stop, keep choosing to continue cancelling. If you accept an offer, you stay a member and don't cancel. After a discount, the full price applies again automatically. After cancelling, you can still use your credits and bookings until the end of your current cycle.

You then get a confirmation email from ClassPass. No email means your request did not go through, so contact ClassPass via chat.

<h2 id="trial">Cancelling during the free trial</h2>

If you cancel during the free trial, it takes effect straight away: your credits are gone and your booked classes are cancelled. So if you want to use the whole trial, cancel on its last day, at least 48 hours before it turns into a paid plan.

<h2 id="pause">Can you pause ClassPass?</h2>

Yes, in many cases. When you start cancelling, ClassPass often first offers a free two-month pause. You switch to ClassPass Lite, at 0 a month.

- You pay nothing during the pause.
- You can't use your credits during the pause. Unused credits are available again afterwards.
- The pause starts on your next renewal date.
- After two months, your current plan resumes automatically.

Don't see this offer? Ask for a pause via chat, for example after an injury. ClassPass decides case by case.

Just less time for a while? A smaller plan is often easier: you change it yourself, and it starts on your next renewal date.

<h2 id="before">Before you cancel</h2>

- [ ] Use your remaining credits, including rollover credits
- [ ] Consider a smaller plan instead of stopping completely
- [ ] Cancel at least 48 hours before your renewal date
- [ ] Check that the confirmation email has arrived

## Frequently asked questions

<details open>
<summary>Does it cost anything to cancel ClassPass?</summary>

No. There is no cancellation fee and no notice period.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Do you get an offer when you cancel?</summary>

Often, yes, but only if you change your mind and stay. Before your cancellation goes through, ClassPass often offers you something to keep your membership: a free two-month pause or a discount on the next few months, for example 20% off for three months. If you accept, you don't cancel.

The discount comes with conditions:

- It starts at your next cycle.
- After the discount period you automatically pay the full price again.
- If you cancel within a few months (the app said 1 to 5), you lose the discount.
- It can't be combined with other offers and can be changed or withdrawn.
- It's only for eligible members, so not everyone gets the same offer.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Do I get money back for the rest of the month?</summary>

No. ClassPass does not refund months of membership or extra credits you bought. That is why it pays to use your credits before your membership ends.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Can I come back later?</summary>

Yes. You can reactivate your membership in your account later. You then start again with the credits of your new plan.

</details>
