You can cancel ClassPass at any time, with no fee and no notice period. Do it at least 48 hours before your renewal date, and use your credits first: they are gone once your membership ends.

<h2 id="short">In short</h2>

| | Cancel | Pause | Smaller plan |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **How** | Settings in the app or on the website | Ask ClassPass via chat, with a reason | Account, then Manage plan |
| **When** | At least 48 hours before your renewal date | Only for unexpected circumstances, not guaranteed | From your next renewal date |
| **Cost** | Free | You are not charged during the pause | The lower monthly price |
| **Your credits** | Lost when your membership ends, rollover credits too | Frozen, then carried over | The usual rollover rules |

<h2 id="cancel">How to cancel</h2>

**On the website**

1. Click your initial at the top right and open **Settings**.
2. Click the red cancel link.
3. Follow the steps until you see the confirmation.

**In the app**

1. Go to **Profile**, then **Settings**.
2. Under **Billing**, tap the cancel option.
3. Follow the steps until you see the confirmation.

You then get a confirmation email from ClassPass. No email means your request did not go through, so contact ClassPass via chat.

<h2 id="trial">Cancelling during the free trial</h2>

If you cancel during the free trial, it takes effect straight away: your credits are gone and your booked classes are cancelled. So if you want to use the whole trial, cancel on its last day, at least 48 hours before it turns into a paid plan.

<h2 id="pause">Can you pause ClassPass?</h2>

Sometimes. ClassPass offers a pause for unexpected circumstances, such as an injury, but it is not guaranteed. You ask for it via chat and give a reason.

- You are not charged during the pause.
- Your credits are frozen and unused credits carry over.
- After the pause, your previous plan starts again with new credits.

Just less time for a while? A smaller plan is often easier: you change it yourself, and it starts on your next renewal date.

<h2 id="before">Before you cancel</h2>

- [ ] Use your remaining credits, including rollover credits
- [ ] Consider a smaller plan instead of stopping completely
- [ ] Cancel at least 48 hours before your renewal date
- [ ] Check that the confirmation email has arrived

## Frequently asked questions

<details open>
<summary>Does it cost anything to cancel ClassPass?</summary>

No. There is no cancellation fee and no notice period.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Do I get money back for the rest of the month?</summary>

No. ClassPass does not refund months of membership or extra credits you bought. That is why it pays to use your credits before your membership ends.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Can I come back later?</summary>

Yes. You can reactivate your membership in your account later. You then start again with the credits of your new plan.

</details>
