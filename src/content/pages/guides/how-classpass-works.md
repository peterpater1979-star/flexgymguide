## What is ClassPass?

ClassPass is a monthly membership that gives you access to fitness studios, gyms, spas and salons. Instead of joining one gym, you book classes and visits at different places in your city, all from one app.

You don't need a separate account or payment details at each studio. You simply book in the ClassPass app and pay with credits.

## How credits work

<div class="kolommen">

<div class="kolom">

### A monthly balance

Your plan gives you a set number of credits every month.

</div>

<div class="kolom">

### Prices vary

Each class costs credits. The amount depends on the studio, the time and the city.

</div>

<div class="kolom">

### Limited rollover

Unused credits roll over, up to the number of credits in your next month's plan.
