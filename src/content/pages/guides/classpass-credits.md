With ClassPass you don't pay per class but with credits. That makes it flexible, but it can also be confusing at first: the same yoga class can cost a different number of credits on Monday morning than on Thursday evening. Here's how it works.

<h2 id="basics">The basics</h2>

<div class="kolommen">

<div class="kolom">

### A monthly balance

Your plan gives you a set number of credits every month. A bigger plan means more credits.

</div>

<div class="kolom">

### Every booking costs credits

Before you book, the app shows how many credits a class or gym visit costs.

</div>

<div class="kolom">

### Limited rollover

Unused credits roll over, up to the number of credits in your next month's plan.

</div>

</div>

<h2 id="price">Why one class costs more than another</h2>

The number of credits for a class is not fixed. It depends on:

- **The studio:** a boutique studio usually costs more credits than a regular gym
- **The time:** classes at popular times, such as weekday evenings and weekend mornings, often cost more
- **Demand:** a class that fills up quickly can cost more than one with plenty of spots
- **The city:** credit values differ per city and per country

So it's worth looking at a few options before you book.

<h2 id="tips">Seven tips to get more out of your credits</h2>

1. **Train off-peak.** A class in the middle of the day or early in the morning often costs fewer credits than the same class in the evening.
2. **Look at similar classes.** Search by activity, such as yoga or spinning, and look at the credits per class across a few studios near you.
3. **Mix in gym visits.** A gym visit between classes is often a cheaper way to keep training.
4. **Cancel on time.** Late cancellations and no-shows can cost you credits or a fee. Check the cancellation window before you book.
5. **Use your rollover.** Had a quiet month? Your unused credits move to the next month, within the limit of your plan.
6. **Pick a plan that fits.** If you often run out of credits, a bigger plan can be better value. If you always have credits left, a smaller plan saves money.
7. **Check before you travel.** Credit values abroad can be different from home, so look at a few classes before you plan your week.

<h2 id="plan">How many credits do you need?</h2>

That depends on how often you train and which studios you like. Start with the free trial and note how many credits your favourite classes cost. Multiply that by the number of times you want to train per month, and you'll see which plan fits.

Training three times a week or more at the same gym? Then a regular membership may suit you better. Read [Gym pass or gym membership?](/guides/gym-pass-or-membership/)

## Frequently asked questions

<details open>
<summary>Do ClassPass credits expire?</summary>

Unused credits roll over to the next month, up to the number of credits in your next month's plan. Credits above that limit expire.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Can I use my credits abroad?</summary>

Yes. Your credits work in every country where ClassPass is available, at the local credit values.

</details>

<details>
<summary>What happens if I cancel a class late?</summary>

A late cancellation or a no-show can cost you the credits or a fee. The rules can differ per studio and city, so check them in the app before you book.

</details>
