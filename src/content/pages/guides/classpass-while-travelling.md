Your ClassPass plan doesn't only work in your own city. On holiday, on a work trip or visiting family, you can use the same credits in any country where ClassPass operates. There's nothing to switch: you simply search where you are.

<h2 id="kort">In short</h2>

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Where** | In more than 30 countries, 14 of them in Europe |
| **Extra cost?** | No, you book with the credits from your own plan |
| **Credits** | A class can cost more or fewer credits than at home |
| **Cancelling** | Free up to 12 hours before; the fee after that differs by country |
| **How** | Search in the app by city, neighbourhood or your current location |

<h2 id="hoe">How it works</h2>

1. **Open the app** and tap Search at the bottom.
2. **Choose your place:** your current location, or a city, neighbourhood or postcode. You can also do this on the website, on the search page.
3. **Check the credits.** For each class you see how many credits it costs there.
4. **Book as usual.** The credits come off your normal balance.

<h2 id="waar">Where does ClassPass work?</h2>

In Europe, ClassPass works in 14 countries:

[Netherlands](/nl/classpass/) · [Belgium](/be/classpass/) · [Germany](/de/classpass/) · [Austria](/at/classpass/) · [Switzerland](/ch/classpass/) · [United Kingdom](/uk/classpass/) · [Ireland](/ie/classpass/) · [France](/fr/classpass/) · [Spain](/es/classpass/) · [Portugal](/pt/classpass/) · [Denmark](/dk/classpass/) · [Sweden](/se/classpass/) · [Norway](/no/classpass/) · [Finland](/fi/classpass/)

Outside Europe it works in the United States, Canada, Australia, New Zealand, Japan, Singapore, Hong Kong, Thailand, Malaysia, the Philippines, Indonesia, India, the United Arab Emirates, Brazil and Chile, among others. ClassPass keeps growing; you'll find the latest list in the app.

<h2 id="letop">What to watch out for</h2>

- **Different credit values.** Every city is different: the same kind of class can cost more credits in one city than in another. So take a look before you book.
- **Cancelling.** You can cancel for free up to 12 hours before the start, unless the studio has an earlier deadline. After that you pay a fee, and the amount differs by country. Japan has its own rules.
- **Time difference.** If you book a class at your destination from home, check the local start time carefully.
- **Trial.** Different rules may apply during the trial. You'll see what applies to you when you sign up.

<h2 id="tips">Tips for the road</h2>

1. **Save a few credits.** Unused credits roll over to the next month, within the limit of your plan. A quiet month before your trip gives you a little extra.
2. **Look around in advance.** Search your destination from home, so you know which studios there are and what they cost.
3. **Book early.** Popular classes fill up fast, on holiday too.
4. **Think of a gym as well.** A gym visit near your hotel is an easy way to fit in a workout.
5. **Relax after your trip.** You can also use credits for a massage or the spa.

## Frequently asked questions

<details open>
<summary>Does ClassPass cost extra abroad?</summary>

No. There's no surcharge, and you book with the credits from your own plan. A class can cost more or fewer credits than at home, though.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Do I need to change my plan when I travel?</summary>

No. You search in the app where you are and book there. Your plan stays the same.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Does ClassPass work outside Europe?</summary>

Yes, including the United States, Japan, Australia and Singapore. The full list is above.

</details>
