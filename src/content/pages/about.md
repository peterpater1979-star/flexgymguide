FlexGymGuide helps you work out without being tied to one gym. We compare fitness passes such as ClassPass and gym chains with flexible memberships, country by country, in the language of that country.

## Who we are

FlexGymGuide is made by Peter Pater, based in Amsterdam. I love mixing up my workouts: variety is what keeps me moving. So I went looking for the best option: a gym that offers everything, or a handy fitness pass that lets you train anywhere? That's how FlexGymGuide was born.

## How we compare

- We look at how a pass or gym works, what it costs per month, how you cancel, and where you can use it.
- Prices and terms change often. We use placeholders until we have checked a figure, and we date each page.
- Providers cannot pay to be included or to get a better review.
