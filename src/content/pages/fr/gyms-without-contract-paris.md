Just moved to Paris, here for a semester, or not sure how long you will stay? You don't have to sign up for a year ("avec engagement"). These are the options to work out month by month, or only when you go.

<h2 id="short">In short</h2>

| | Cancelling | Price | Good for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **ClassPass** | Any time, no notice period | Standard plan €75 a month (47 credits); smaller plans in the app | Variety at studios and gyms all over Paris |
| **Basic-Fit Flex** | 4 weeks' notice, no minimum term | From €34.98 per 4 weeks, plus €19.99 joining fee | Cheap and frequent training, often open 24/7 |
| **Drop-in class or class card** | Not needed | Varies per studio | Now and then at a favourite studio |

<p class="klein">Prices from October 2026. ClassPass: location Paris. Basic-Fit: national prices in France. Always check the current price with the provider.</p>

<h2 id="classpass">ClassPass: cancel any time</h2>

With ClassPass you get credits every month for classes and gym visits at studios across Paris. You are not tied down: you cancel whenever you want and keep training until the end of your month.

- **Plans in Paris:** the standard plan is 47 credits for €75 a month. There are smaller and bigger plans in the app. See [ClassPass credits explained](/guides/classpass-credits/).
- **Try it first:** 14 days free with 10 workouts. Read [Trying ClassPass for free](/guides/classpass-free-trial/).
- **Pausing:** when you cancel, ClassPass often offers a free pause. Read [Cancelling or pausing ClassPass](/guides/cancel-or-pause-classpass/).
- **Travelling a lot?** Your plan also works in other cities. Read [ClassPass while travelling](/guides/classpass-while-travelling/).

<h2 id="basic-fit">Basic-Fit Flex: no minimum term</h2>

Basic-Fit is a budget gym chain with more than 300 clubs open 24/7 in France. Besides the one-year membership (52 weeks, then cancel with 4 weeks' notice) there is the Flex option, without commitment. Flex costs €9.99 more per 4 weeks, and the welcome offers for the yearly membership don't apply.

| | One-year commitment | Flex | What you get |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Comfort | €24.99 | €34.98 | All clubs in France |
| Premium | €29.99 | €39.98 | All clubs in Europe, one guest a week, massage chairs |
| Ultimate | €34.99 | €44.98 | All clubs in Europe, unlimited guests, Yanga Sports Water, freezing included |

Prices per 4 weeks. With Flex you pay a €19.99 joining fee on every plan. Group classes are included at clubs that offer them.

**Taking a break?** With the freeze option (€2.99 per 4 weeks, added when you sign up, included in Ultimate) you can freeze your membership twice a year, for up to 4 weeks each time. While frozen, you don't pay the membership fee.

<h2 id="drop-in">Drop-in classes and class cards</h2>

Many yoga, pilates and boxing studios sell single classes or a card for a number of classes. You only pay when you go, which is handy if you have one favourite studio. Check the studio's website; prices and validity differ.

<h2 id="tips">Tips if you are new in France</h2>

- **"Sans engagement" is what you're looking for.** It means no minimum term. "Avec engagement" means you sign up for a fixed period, often a year.
- **Websites in French.** Many gym sign-up pages are only in French. Your browser can translate them.
- **Per 4 weeks, not per month.** Basic-Fit charges every 4 weeks, so you pay 13 times a year.
- **Direct debit.** Gyms usually collect payment by SEPA direct debit, so have your IBAN ready.
- **Notice period.** "No commitment" doesn't mean "no notice": at Basic-Fit you pay 4 more weeks after cancelling. Cancel in time before you leave the country.
- **How often you go.** If you train three times a week or more at the same place, a fixed contract is often cheaper. Read [Gym pass or membership?](/guides/gym-pass-or-membership/)

## Frequently asked questions

<details open>
<summary>Can I join a gym in Paris without a contract?</summary>

Yes. With ClassPass you cancel any time, Basic-Fit has a Flex membership without a minimum term, and many studios sell drop-in classes.

</details>

<details>
<summary>What is the cheapest way to work out without a contract?</summary>

That depends on how often you go. For a few classes a month, a small ClassPass plan or a drop-in class is cheapest. If you often train at one place, a monthly gym membership is usually cheaper per visit.

</details>

<details>
<summary>I am only in Paris for a few months. What should I choose?</summary>

Pick something you can cancel quickly. ClassPass has no notice period, so you can stop at the end of any month. With Basic-Fit Flex, count on 4 weeks' notice and cancel in time before you leave.

</details>
