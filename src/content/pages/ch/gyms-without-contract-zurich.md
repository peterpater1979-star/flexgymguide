<h2 id="classpass">ClassPass: cancel any time</h2>

With ClassPass you get credits every month for classes and gym visits at studios all over Zurich. You are not tied down: you cancel whenever you want and keep training until the end of your month.

- **Plans in Zurich:** the standard plan is 45 credits for CHF 79 a month. There are smaller and bigger plans in the app. See [ClassPass credits explained](/guides/classpass-credits/).
- **Try it first:** 14 days free with 10 workouts. Read [Trying ClassPass for free](/guides/classpass-free-trial/).
- **Pausing:** when you cancel, ClassPass often offers a free pause. Read [Cancelling or pausing ClassPass](/guides/cancel-or-pause-classpass/).
- **Travelling a lot?** Your plan also works in other cities. Read [ClassPass while travelling](/guides/classpass-while-travelling/).

<h2 id="gyms">Activ Fitness: also for one month</h2>

Most Swiss gym chains mainly sell yearly memberships. Activ Fitness has 16 gyms in Zurich and, besides the yearly membership, also sells a one-month membership. Which gyms you can use depends on the category:

- **Category 2** (over 90 gyms in Switzerland): in Zurich Altstetten, Bellevue, Central, Forum, Kreuzplatz, Langstrasse, Oerlikon, Stauffacher, Tiefenbrunnen, Wiedikon and Wipkingen.
- **Category 3** (over 120 gyms): also Glattpark, Puls 5, Sihlcity, Stadelhofen and Stockerhof.

| Activ Fitness | Category 2 | Category 3 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **1 month**, paid in advance | CHF 150 | CHF 150 |
| **12 months**, paid in advance | CHF 799 | CHF 899 |
| **12 months**, students, paid in advance | CHF 699 | CHF 799 |
| **12 months**, paid monthly | CHF 70 a month | CHF 80 a month |
| **12 months**, students, paid monthly | CHF 62 a month | CHF 70 a month |

- **Paying monthly:** this is still a yearly membership. There is a one-off administration fee of CHF 70, and the membership renews automatically.
- **Loyalty bonus:** if you renew a prepaid yearly membership without a break, you get CHF 50 credit on your chip bracelet.

<h2 id="drop-in">Drop-in classes and class cards</h2>

Many yoga, pilates and boxing studios sell single classes or a card for a number of classes. You only pay when you go, which is handy if you have one favourite studio. Check the studio's website; prices and validity differ.

<h2 id="tips">Tips if you are new in Switzerland</h2>

- **Websites in German.** Many gym websites in Zurich are mainly in German. Your browser can translate them.
- **Automatic renewal.** Many memberships renew automatically unless you cancel in time. Check the notice period in the terms.
- **Cancelling by letter.** Some gyms want a written cancellation. Send it by registered mail.
- **One-off fees.** Watch for joining fees and fees for a membership card.
- **How often you go.** If you train three times a week or more at the same place, a yearly membership is often cheaper. Read [Gym pass or membership?](/guides/gym-pass-or-membership/)

## Frequently asked questions

<details open>
<summary>Can I join a gym in Zurich without a yearly contract?</summary>

Yes. With ClassPass you cancel any time, Activ Fitness has a one-month membership, and many studios sell drop-in classes.

</details>

<details>
<summary>What is the cheapest way to work out without a contract?</summary>

That depends on how often you go. For a few classes a month, a small ClassPass plan or a drop-in class is cheapest. If you often train at one place, a gym membership is usually cheaper per visit.

</details>

<details>
<summary>I am only in Zurich for a few months. What should I choose?</summary>

Pick something you can cancel quickly. ClassPass has no notice period, so you can stop at the end of any month. With a gym, check the notice period and cancel in time before you leave.

</details>
