F45 Training is a chain of studios for 45-minute group workouts that mix high-intensity interval training, circuits and functional movement. The UK has 46 studios, most of them in London. Each studio is a franchise and sets its own prices and terms.

<h2 id="workouts">The workouts</h2>

Every class is a 45-minute circuit with a trainer, in a small group. The week alternates cardio days and resistance days, with a hybrid session in between. The timetable differs a little per studio.

<h2 id="cancel">Cancelling</h2>

- **Cancel:** through your local studio, or with the cancel form on the F45 website, which your studio checks. Your membership isn't cancelled straight away: you get a confirmation once the studio has processed it.
- **Notice and fees:** the notice period and any early termination fee depend on your studio and your membership.
- **Studio closes:** your membership is cancelled automatically.

## Frequently asked questions

<details open>
<summary>How do I cancel F45?</summary>

Contact your local studio, or use the cancel form on the F45 website. The studio processes your request with its own notice period.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Can I train at other F45 studios?</summary>

Usually you train at the studio where you signed up. With an unlimited membership you can visit other studios a set number of times a month at no extra cost.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Why do F45 prices differ per studio?</summary>

Every F45 studio is owned and run independently, so prices and memberships differ per location.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Can I try F45 through ClassPass?</summary>

Yes, some F45 studios are on ClassPass, such as Blackwall, Bromley and Peckham in London, Brighton Central and Chelmsford. With ClassPass you can book a class there without a membership. Read [all about ClassPass](/uk/classpass/).

</details>
