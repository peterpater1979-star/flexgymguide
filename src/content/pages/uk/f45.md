F45 Training is a group workout of 45 minutes: a circuit of functional exercises with strength and cardio, led by a coach. The chain started in Australia and has around 2,000 studios in more than 50 countries. Every studio is a franchise with its own owner, so prices, memberships and terms differ per studio.

<h2 id="memberships">Memberships and prices</h2>

F45 has no national price list. Each studio sells its own memberships and class packs, and the price differs per studio. Ask your studio about:

- the price of a membership and of a class pack
- the minimum term and the notice period
- whether you can freeze your membership

<p class="klein">We don't list temporary offers or trials.</p>

<h2 id="other-studios">Training at other studios</h2>

A membership is usually only valid at the studio where you signed up. With a paid-in-full or autopay unlimited membership, F45 lets you train at other studios for a set number of visits a month. Check with your own studio whether this applies to you.

<h2 id="cancel">Cancelling</h2>

- **How:** contact your own studio, or use the cancellation form on the F45 website. Your studio handles the request.
- **When it ends:** not straight away. Your studio's membership terms apply, including any notice period or early termination fee.
- **Tip:** ask about the notice period before you sign up.

<p class="klein">Source: F45 Training FAQ and cancellation form (November 2026).</p>
