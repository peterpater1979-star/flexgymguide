<h2 id="classpass">ClassPass: cancel anytime</h2>

With ClassPass you get credits every month for classes and gym time at studios across Leeds. You’re not tied to anything: cancel before your renewal date and you keep training until the end of your month. Read [how ClassPass credits work](/uk/classpass/).

<h2 id="puregym">PureGym: no contract</h2>

PureGym has 8 gyms in Leeds, most of them open 24/7. Every plan is without a contract: Off-Peak, Core or Plus. The price differs per gym. Cancelling works online in the Members’ Area; read [how to cancel PureGym](/uk/cancel-puregym/).

<h2 id="village-gym">Village Gym: a Flexible term</h2>

Village Gym has two health clubs in Leeds, Leeds North in Headingley and Leeds South, with a gym floor, an indoor pool, a sauna and more than 100 classes a week. With a Flexible term you’re tied for at least 3 months instead of 12. You choose Peak or Off Peak.

<h2 id="letop">What to watch out for</h2>

- **Joining fees.** Many gyms charge a one-off fee, also without a contract.
- **Notice.** “No contract” doesn’t always mean you can stop the same day: check how far ahead you need to cancel.
- **How often you go.** If you train three times a week or more at the same place, a cheap gym usually works out cheaper per visit than a pass. If you want variety, a pass suits you better.
