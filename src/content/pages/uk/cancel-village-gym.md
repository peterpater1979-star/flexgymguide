You cancel Village Gym by email to Member Services. With a 12-month Results agreement, you give one full month's notice to stop when your 12 months are up. With Flexible, you commit to at least 3 months and then give 90 days' notice.

<h2 id="summary">In short</h2>

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Where** | By email to Member Services |
| **Results (12 months)** | One full month's notice to stop at the end of the 12 months |
| **Flexible** | At least 3 months, then 90 days' notice |
| **Freeze** | 1 to 12 months at £10 a month; ask before the 20th of the month before |

<h2 id="steps">How to cancel</h2>

1. **Check your membership:** Results or Flexible, and when your term ends.
2. **Email Member Services** with your name, membership number and club.
3. **Count the notice period** and keep the confirmation.

<h2 id="when">Good to know</h2>

- Cancelling your direct debit early does not end the agreement: the remaining months are still due.
- The joining fee is not refunded, even if you cancel in the first days.
- A freeze extends your 12-month term.

<p class="klein">Source: Village Gym membership terms (November 2026). Always check the terms of your own membership.</p>

<h2 id="alternatives">What next? Flexible alternatives</h2>

## Frequently asked questions

<details open>
<summary>What is the notice period at Village Gym?</summary>

One full month at the end of a 12-month Results agreement. With Flexible, 90 days after the first 3 months.

</details>

<details>
<summary>How do I cancel Village Gym?</summary>

By email to Member Services. Cancelling your direct debit is not the same as cancelling.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Can I freeze instead?</summary>

Yes, from 1 to 12 months at £10 a month. Ask before the 20th of the month before.

</details>
