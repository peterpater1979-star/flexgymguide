Virgin Active is a chain of health clubs with 31 clubs in the UK, according to the chain. Most are in and around London; outside London there are clubs in Salford Quays, Nottingham, Sheffield, Solihull, Northampton and Essex. Every club has a gym floor, group classes and a spa, and most clubs have a pool. Each club sets its own prices.

<h2 id="memberships">Memberships</h2>

| Membership | Term |
| --- | --- |
| **12-month membership** | You commit for 12 months |
| **Monthly membership** | Rolling, with a minimum of one month |
| **Corporate membership** | Through your employer, at participating companies |

At some clubs there are also off-peak, club-only, family and age-related memberships. Ask your club which ones it offers.

<h2 id="prices">Prices</h2>

Virgin Active has no national price list: each club sets its own monthly price, and the website shows the price once you choose a club. According to Virgin Active, a 12-month membership gives you a lower monthly rate than a rolling monthly one.

At **Virgin Active Chelmsford** in Essex, these were the prices in November 2026:

| Membership | Price |
| --- | --- |
| **Any time, 12 months** | £100 a month, plus a £20 joining fee |
| **Any time, monthly** | £135 a month, plus a £30 joining fee |

This includes unlimited access to the Chelmsford club, group classes and the app, plus access to some other Virgin Active clubs. The club is open from 5:30am to 10pm on weekdays and from 7am to 7pm at weekends.

Ask your own club about:

- the monthly price for a 12-month or monthly membership
- the joining fee
- off-peak, family or age-related options

<p class="klein">Prices without temporary offers.</p>

<h2 id="included">What's included</h2>

- Gym floor with Technogym and Eleiko equipment at many clubs
- Unlimited group classes, such as Reformer Pilates, yoga, boxing, cycle and strength
- Spa with sauna, steam room and hydrotherapy pool
- Pool at every London club except Cannon Street, and for example in Salford Quays, Nottingham, Sheffield and Solihull
- Childcare at many clubs
- Most clubs open from 6am to 10pm on weekdays and from 8am to 8pm at weekends

<h2 id="other-clubs">Training at other clubs</h2>

Every club belongs to a tier, from Tier 1 (the most exclusive, such as Mayfair and Chiswick Riverside) to Tier 7. With your membership you can visit any club in your own tier or a lower one. The clubs outside London are mostly Tier 5. Access may depend on your membership type: check it in the app.

According to Virgin Active's multi-club access guide, a membership with multi-club access also lets you use Virgin Active clubs abroad, in Italy, South Africa, Australia, Singapore and Thailand. The guide gives no fees or limits for this, so ask your club team before you travel.

<h2 id="cancel">Cancelling and freezing</h2>

Virgin Active doesn't publish its membership contract on the website. Check the notice period, the rules for freezing and what it costs in your contract or with your club before you join. A 12-month membership runs for the full 12 months.

## Frequently asked questions

<details open>
<summary>How much is Virgin Active?</summary>

It depends on the club and the membership. In Chelmsford, an any-time membership costs £100 a month with 12 months and £135 a month rolling monthly, plus a joining fee of £20 or £30 (November 2026). Each club sets its own price, and the website shows it once you choose a club.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Does Virgin Active have a contract?</summary>

You choose a 12-month membership or a rolling monthly one with a minimum of one month. Check the notice period in your contract.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Can I use other Virgin Active clubs?</summary>

Yes, every club in your own tier or a lower one. Tier 1 clubs such as Mayfair are only open to Tier 1 members.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Can I use Virgin Active abroad?</summary>

With multi-club access, Virgin Active says you can also use its clubs in Italy, South Africa, Australia, Singapore and Thailand. Ask your club about the conditions before you travel.

</details>
