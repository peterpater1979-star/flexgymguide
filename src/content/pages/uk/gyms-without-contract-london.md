<h2 id="classpass">ClassPass: cancel any time</h2>

With ClassPass you get credits every month for classes and gym visits at studios across London. You are not tied down: you cancel whenever you want and keep training until the end of your month.

- **Plans in London:** the standard plan is 52 credits for £69 a month. There are smaller and bigger plans in the app. See [ClassPass credits explained](/guides/classpass-credits/).
- **Try it first:** 14 days free with 10 workouts. Read [Trying ClassPass for free](/guides/classpass-free-trial/).
- **Pausing:** when you cancel, ClassPass often offers a free pause. Read [Cancelling or pausing ClassPass](/guides/cancel-or-pause-classpass/).
- **Travelling a lot?** Your plan also works in other cities. Read [ClassPass while travelling](/guides/classpass-while-travelling/).

<h2 id="the-gym-group">The Gym Group: no contract</h2>

The Gym Group is a low-cost chain with 87 gyms in London, most of them open 24/7. Monthly memberships have no contract and no minimum term, and classes are included. You choose between Off-peak, Standard and Ultimate; with Ultimate you can also use other gyms.

[PRICES THE GYM GROUP]

Cancel at least four working days before your next payment date. In the first 14 days you can cancel and get a refund, minus the cost of any visits. Saver plans (9 or 12 months paid up front) are cheaper per month, but you can't cancel them early. Not sure yet? Try a gym with a day pass.

<h2 id="drop-in">Drop-in classes and class packs</h2>

Many yoga, pilates, boxing and spin studios in London sell single classes or packs of classes. You only pay when you go, which is handy if you have one favourite studio. Check the studio's website; prices and validity differ.

<h2 id="tips">Tips</h2>

- **Joining fees.** Some gyms charge a one-off joining fee, also on monthly plans.
- **Notice period.** "No contract" can still mean you need to cancel a few days before your next payment. Read the terms.
- **Saver or upfront plans.** Paying for months up front is cheaper, but you usually can't get your money back if you stop.
- **How often you go.** If you train three times a week or more at the same place, a gym membership is often cheaper per visit. Read [Gym pass or membership?](/guides/gym-pass-or-membership/)

## Frequently asked questions

<details open>
<summary>Can I join a gym in London without a contract?</summary>

Yes. With ClassPass you cancel any time, The Gym Group's monthly plans have no contract, and many studios sell drop-in classes.

</details>

<details>
<summary>What is the cheapest way to work out without a contract?</summary>

That depends on how often you go. For a few classes a month, a small ClassPass plan or a drop-in class is cheapest. If you often train at one place, a monthly gym membership is usually cheaper per visit.

</details>

<details>
<summary>I am only in London for a few months. What should I choose?</summary>

Pick something you can cancel quickly. ClassPass has no notice period, so you can stop at the end of any month. With a monthly gym membership, cancel in time before your next payment.

</details>
