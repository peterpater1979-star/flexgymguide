<h2 id="areas">By area</h2>

A few examples per area. There are more studios than the ones listed here: you'll find the full list on ClassPass.

### City centre and North Laine

Reformer Collective on Old Steine, Pilates@BN1 on Marlborough Place and Humankind Hot X TheBox on Castle Street, with TheBox in the same building for strength and boxing. F45 is on Fleet Street, The Vault 164 on North Street, FMG Project on Kensington Street and Rebalance on Old Steine. Kings Gym on Queens Road is the place for gym visits, and Yoga in the Lanes is on Middle Street.

### Kemp Town and the seafront

Humankind has a yoga studio, a reformer studio and a hot studio in Kemp Town. On the seafront at Sea Lanes there are Luna Wave Yoga, SEAGYM for outdoor training on the beach and Powerwatts for indoor cycling, and Ascend has a strength studio on Edward Street.

### Hove

Reformer Collective at Palmeira Square, Barefoot Pilates, Reform Rituals and Align Studio for reformer Pilates, and Flow State, Ekah and Studio Sensi for yoga. For cycling and strength there are Rox Life on Western Road, OneCity and Adapt Fitness, plus The Shredquarters, impact fitness hove and Snap Fitness for gym visits.

### North and east Brighton

RogueCycle and Boxe on Stroudley Road, SPACE Yoga Studio at St Augustine's Centre, FITBOX near Preston Park, Fitness Bar on Dyke Road and LifeFit. Freedom Leisure runs leisure centres in Withdean, Moulsecoomb and Portslade, and the Prince Regent Swimming Complex in the centre.

### Around Brighton

OP3 Studios in Rottingdean for reformer Pilates, Three Sixty Fitness in Shoreham, Rep & Reform in Lancing and Go Fitness in Lewes.

<h2 id="types">By type of workout</h2>

A selection of studios for each type of workout. You'll find all studios on ClassPass.

- **Reformer Pilates:** among others, Reformer Collective, Humankind, Pilates@BN1, Barefoot Pilates, Reform Rituals, Align Studio, Iona Studio and OP3 Studios
- **Yoga and hot yoga:** among others, Flow State, Ekah, SPACE Yoga Studio, Luna Wave Yoga, Studio Sensi, Dynamic Hot Yoga and Humankind
- **Indoor cycling:** among others, RogueCycle, Rox Life, OneCity and Powerwatts
- **Boxing and martial arts:** among others, TheBox, Boxe, Gorilla Boxing, Kenshiro Gym and Miah Bros MMA in Portslade
- **Strength and HIIT:** among others, F45, Ascend, FITBOX, The Vault 164, FMG Project, The Shredquarters and Brighton Kettlebells
- **Outdoor:** among others, SEAGYM on the beach and Be Outdoor Fit in Preston Park, Hove Park and at Hove Lagoon
- **Gyms:** among others, Kings Gym, Fitness Bar, Snap Fitness and the Freedom Leisure centres

<h2 id="tips">Tips</h2>

- Popular studios in Hove and the city centre book up fast, especially early mornings and after work. Book a few days ahead.
- Credits per class vary by studio and time of day. Off-peak classes are often cheaper.
- Your plan also works in London, the rest of the UK and abroad, wherever ClassPass is available.
