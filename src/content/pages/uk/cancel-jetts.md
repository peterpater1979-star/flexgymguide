You cancel Jetts by email to your home gym, not by phone or social media. Your email must arrive by the 1st of the month. Depending on the date, you pay one or two more monthly payments.

<h2 id="summary">In short</h2>

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Where** | By email to your home gym |
| **Deadline** | Your email must arrive by the 1st of the month |
| **Cooling-off** | 7 days: cancel by email and get your fees back |
| **Paid In Advance** | Runs until the end of the period you paid for |
| **Freeze** | Possible under the Jetts hold policy; ask your gym |

<h2 id="steps">How to cancel</h2>

1. **Email your home gym** with your name, membership details and the request to cancel.
2. **Send it before the 1st** of the month to keep the number of payments down.
3. **Keep the confirmation** with your end date.
4. **Stop your direct debit** only after your last payment.

<h2 id="when">Good to know</h2>

- Messages by phone or on social media don't count as a cancellation.
- You can't cancel while your membership is on hold.
