Virgin Active doesn't publish its membership contract on the website. Your notice period and the rules for leaving are in your own contract. Cancel in writing to your club, so you have proof of the date.

<h2 id="summary">In short</h2>

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Where** | In writing to your club, or the way your contract describes |
| **12-month membership** | Runs for the full 12 months |
| **Monthly membership** | Rolling, with a minimum of one month |
| **Notice period** | In your contract |

<h2 id="steps">How to cancel</h2>

1. **Check your contract** for the notice period and how to cancel.
2. **Cancel in writing** with your club, by email or letter, with your name and membership number.
3. **Keep the confirmation** and check the date your membership ends.

<h2 id="when">Good to know</h2>

- Cancelling your direct debit with your bank is not the same as cancelling your membership. Cancel with the gym first, then stop the payment.
- Cancelling in writing, by email or letter, always gives you proof of the date. Keep a copy and the confirmation.

<p class="klein">Virgin Active doesn't publish its contract online (November 2026). Always check your own contract.</p>
