## ClassPass in Nottingham

With ClassPass you get monthly credits to book classes and gym visits at studios. Ideal if you like variety and don't want to commit to one place.

### Where

At the moment there are no studios in Nottingham on ClassPass. Your plan does work in other UK cities, such as [Birmingham](/uk/birmingham/), [Sheffield](/uk/sheffield/), [Leeds](/uk/leeds/) and [London](/uk/london/). If you mainly train in Nottingham, a low-cost gym is the better choice for now.

### What you get

- Monthly credits for classes and gym visits
- Standard plan in Nottingham: 36 credits for £49 a month
- 14 days free with 10 workouts to try it out
- No studios in Nottingham yet, but studios in Birmingham, Sheffield, Leeds and London
- Use it in more than 2,500 other cities in 31 countries

### Good for

- People who like to try different workouts
- Training one to three times a week
- People who often travel to other cities

### Watch out for

- Credits per class vary by studio and time of day
- Unused credits roll over for one month. If you still don't use them, they expire

## The Gym Group in Nottingham

The Gym Group has 5 gyms in Nottingham: Nottingham City, Radford, Sherwood, Lady Bay and Chilwell. Monthly memberships have no contract, and most gyms are open 24/7.

### What you get

- A gym floor with classes included
- 24/7 access at most gyms with Standard or Ultimate
- Access to other gyms with Ultimate

### Good for

- Training three times a week or more on a budget
- Students and anyone who wants no contract

### Watch out for

- Off-peak only gives access at quieter hours
- 24/7 access is not available at every gym

## PureGym in Nottingham

PureGym is a budget gym chain with over 450 gyms in the UK, including 6 gyms in Nottingham. Most are open 24/7. Every plan is without a contract, prices start at £18.99 a month for Off-Peak, and classes are included at most gyms.

### Good for

- Training three times a week or more for little money
- Not being tied to a contract

### Keep in mind

- The price differs per gym
- Off-Peak only gives access outside peak hours
- Joining fee from £10

<summary>Is ClassPass cheaper than a gym membership in Nottingham?</summary>

It depends on how often you train and what you want to do. If you mostly use the gym floor, a low-cost gym is usually cheaper. If you want to mix studios and classes, a fitness pass is usually the better fit, but ClassPass has no studios in Nottingham at the moment.

Yes. You can use your credits in other UK cities and abroad, wherever ClassPass is available.
