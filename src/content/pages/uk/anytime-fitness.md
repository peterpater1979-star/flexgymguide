Anytime Fitness is a chain of small gyms that are open 24 hours a day, with more than 5,500 clubs worldwide according to the chain. With your key fob or the app you can get in at any time, at your own club and at clubs abroad. Every club is a franchise and sets its own prices. You can choose a fixed term or a rolling monthly contract.

<h2 id="prices">Prices: two examples</h2>

Anytime Fitness has no national price list: each club sets its own. At Anytime Fitness Aldgate in London, these were the prices in November 2026:

| Membership | Price |
| --- | --- |
| **12-month agreement** | £44.99 a month |
| **6-month agreement** | £49.99 a month |
| **3-month agreement** | £54.99 a month |
| **Rolling monthly** | £59.99 a month |
| **6 months paid up front** | £289.99 |

All memberships include 24/7 access to every club, and private bathrooms and showers. On top of that you pay £40 once for your digital key. Students get 10% off at Aldgate.

At **Anytime Fitness Manchester Central**, these were the prices in November 2026:

| Membership | Price |
| --- | --- |
| **12-month agreement** | £39.95 a month |
| **6-month agreement** | £43.95 a month |
| **Rolling monthly** | £47.95 a month, one calendar month's notice |
| **Student** | £35.95 a month |

You pay a one-off joining fee of £30, or £25 for students. Classes and a fitness consultation are included at this club.

Ask your own club about:

- the monthly price for each term
- the key fee or joining fee
- a student discount

<p class="klein">Prices from November 2026, without temporary offers.</p>

<h2 id="cancel">Cancelling and freezing</h2>

- **Cancel:** with the online cancellation form on your club's page, or at the club. A 3, 6 or 12-month agreement runs until the end of its term.
- **Rolling monthly:** no fixed term. In Manchester Central you give one calendar month's notice; check the notice period with your own club.
- **Freeze:** ask your club; whether you can and what it costs depends on the club.

## Frequently asked questions

<details open>
<summary>How much is Anytime Fitness?</summary>

It depends on the club. In Manchester Central, a 12-month agreement costs £39.95 a month and a rolling monthly contract £47.95, plus a £30 joining fee. At Aldgate in London you pay £44.99 and £59.99, plus £40 for your key (November 2026).

</details>

<details>
<summary>Is Anytime Fitness open 24/7?</summary>

Yes. With your key fob or the app you can get in at any time, every day. Staff are there at set hours only.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Can I use Anytime Fitness abroad?</summary>

Yes. Your membership gives you access to all Anytime Fitness clubs worldwide, more than 5,500 according to the chain.

</details>
