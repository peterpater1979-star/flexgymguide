You cancel Fitness First online, in the "My Membership" section of the website. You give one calendar month's notice. During your minimum term your membership ends at the end of that term; once it has renewed, you can leave at any time with a month's notice.

<h2 id="summary">In short</h2>

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Where** | Online, under "My Membership" on fitnessfirst.co.uk |
| **Notice** | One calendar month |
| **Minimum term** | 1, 3, 6, 9 or 12 months; ends at the end of the term |
| **After renewal** | Cancel any time with a month's notice |

<h2 id="steps">How to cancel</h2>

1. **Log in to fitnessfirst.co.uk** and go to "My Membership".
2. **Fill in the cancellation request.** This is also your notice.
3. **Keep the confirmation email.** Fitness First emails you once it has processed your cancellation. No email? Contact them, because without it your cancellation may not have gone through.

Cancelling within your first 7 days, or for medical reasons or a move, works differently: you contact Membership Services directly (see below).

<h2 id="when">When can you stop?</h2>

- **At the end of your minimum term.** Give one calendar month's notice. If you don't cancel, your membership renews into a new contract with the same minimum term.
- **After renewal.** As a loyalty discount, you can cancel at any time with a month's notice. Depending on your direct debit date, you may pay for the days between your last payment and your cancellation date.
- **Before the end of your minimum term.** You have two options: pay the rest of the term (your membership then ends on the last day of the term), or pay a settlement fee and leave straight away. The settlement fee is the discount you had compared with the 1-month flexible rate. Fitness First's own example: you leave after 3 months of a 12-month term and the difference is £15 a month, so you pay £15 × 3 = £45.
- **Within 7 days of joining.** Cancel for any reason at no cost. If you've already visited the club 3 or more times, you won't get a refund.
- **Paid upfront?** Your membership simply ends at the end of the term, unless you renew.

<h2 id="medical">Cancelling for medical reasons, job loss or a move</h2>

If you can no longer use your home club, for example for medical reasons, because you lost your job or because you moved more than 5 miles away, you can cancel without extra fees. Contact Member Services and send evidence, such as a doctor's note or your notice of termination. Your membership is only cancelled once they have the evidence.

<h2 id="freeze">Freeze instead of cancelling?</h2>

After your first month, you can freeze your membership for 1 to 3 months in the Member Area. It costs £8 a month and you need to ask at least 5 working days in advance. With evidence of a medical reason, a move or another acceptable reason, the freeze can be free. Frozen months don't count towards your minimum term, and cancelling while frozen still costs the usual cancellation fees.
