<h2 id="how-it-works">How ClassPass works</h2>

1. You choose a plan with a set number of credits per month.
2. In the app or on the website you find a class or gym near you.
3. You book with your credits. You see how many credits a class costs before you book.
4. At the end of the month you get new credits.

<div class="kolommen">

<div class="kolom">

### Credits

- Credits per class vary by studio and time of day. A class at a popular time can cost more credits
- Unused credits roll over for one month. If you still don't use them, they expire

</div>

<div class="kolom">

- You can change, pause or cancel your plan any time

## Frequently asked questions

<details open>
<summary>How much does ClassPass cost in the UK?</summary>

It depends on the number of credits per month. More credits let you train more often. Check the current plans with ClassPass.

</details>

<details>
<summary>How many classes can I do with my credits?</summary>

That depends on the studio and the time of day. Classes cost a varying number of credits; you always see the number before you book.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Can I cancel ClassPass any time?</summary>

Yes, you can change, pause or cancel your plan any time. Check the exact terms with ClassPass.

</details>

<details>
<summary>What happens to credits I don't use?</summary>

Unused credits roll over for one month. If you don't use them that month, they expire.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Can I use ClassPass abroad?</summary>

Yes, wherever ClassPass is active: in more than 2,500 cities in 31 countries, at that country's prices.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Is ClassPass cheaper than a gym?</summary>

That depends on how often you train. If you train one to three times a week and like variety, ClassPass is often a good choice. If you go to the gym more often, a regular gym is usually cheaper.

</details>
