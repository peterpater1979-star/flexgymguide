London has a huge choice of studios and gyms on ClassPass, spread across the whole city. Below is a small selection by area and by type of workout, so you can see what is near you. The full, up-to-date list is always on ClassPass itself.

<h2 id="summary">In short</h2>

| | ClassPass in London |
| --- | --- |
| **Price per month** | Standard plan £69 a month (52 credits) |
| **Try it first** | 14 days free, 10 workouts |
| **Where** | All over London, from the West End and the City to Clapham, Hackney and the suburbs |
| **What you can do** | HIIT, reformer Pilates, Lagree, indoor cycling, boxing, yoga, dance, outdoor classes and gym visits |

<p class="klein">Prices and terms change regularly. Always check the latest details with ClassPass.</p>

<h2 id="areas">By area</h2>

### West End and central London

Barry's in Soho and on Euston Road, 1Rebel at Oxford Circus and in Holborn, Psycle on Mortimer Street and Sweat by BXR in Marylebone. For reformer Pilates and Lagree there are JETSET Pilates in Fitzrovia, MAD in Mayfair and Enmei in Marylebone. Gymbox has gyms in Holborn and Farringdon, and Solo60 in Fitzrovia gives you a private gym to yourself.

### The City and East London

Barry's in Shoreditch, St Paul's and Canary Wharf, Sweat by BXR at Bishopsgate, Tribe near Liverpool Street and Gymbox at Bank. Further east there are Mission E1 in Spitalfields for yoga, Pilates, calisthenics and boxing, Victus Soul in Aldgate, BLOK in Clapton, RUMBLE boxing in Dalston, Momentum for CrossFit in Haggerston, Yogahome in Stoke Newington, Waking Dreams in Hackney, KO Combat Academy in Mile End and Gymbox at Westfield Stratford. Solo60 also has a private gym near Moorgate.

### North London

1Rebel in Angel and St John's Wood, FRAME in Angel, ĀVĀ Movement in St John's Wood, Club Fiit in West Hampstead, 3Tribes in Crouch End for hot yoga and Pilates, LIM Studio in Highbury, Ceci Studio for reformer Pilates and Gymbox in Finsbury Park.

### South London

1Rebel and Psycle in Clapham, Goji in Clapham Old Town, Mind & Motion on Northcote Road and Avalon Pilates in Earlsfield. In Peckham there are Milo and the Bull, F45, CRANK for indoor cycling and Yogarise, with CRANK also in Lewisham. You will also find Oru Space in East Dulwich, High Vibe in Brixton, Regen London in Streatham, Forma Pilates in Catford, Tribe at Waterloo, Tempo 301 near Elephant & Castle and Gymbox at Elephant & Castle.

### West London

1Rebel in Chelsea and on High Street Kensington, KXU in Chelsea, Blanc Space in Fulham and Notting Hill, Studio Fix and BoxcleverLDN on Kensington High Street and El Estudio in Olympia. There are also FRAME in Hammersmith, Yoke in Shepherd's Bush, Move in Fulham, The Hot Spot Yoga in Fulham, Barry's in Bayswater and Victoria, JAB Boxing Club near Victoria, and Gymbox at Victoria, Westfield London and in Ealing.

### Outer London

Outside the centre there are bhuti in Richmond and Oru Space in Sutton, plus gyms and studios in towns around London such as Loughton, Grays and Epsom.

<h2 id="types">By type of workout</h2>

- **HIIT and treadmill classes:** Barry's, 1Rebel, Sweat by BXR, Homerun, Milo and the Bull and F45
- **Reformer Pilates and Lagree:** JETSET Pilates, ĀVĀ Movement, Avalon Pilates, Ceci Studio, Blanc Space, MAD and Enmei
- **Indoor cycling:** Psycle, 1Rebel and CRANK
- **Rowing:** ROWBOTS and FRAME x ROWBOTS
- **Boxing and martial arts:** JAB Boxing Club, RUMBLE, BoxcleverLDN, KO Combat Academy, Undisputed Fitness & Martial Arts and outdoor boxing with BoxFit in Hyde Park
- **Yoga:** Yogahome, Yogarise, 3Tribes, The Hot Spot Yoga, Mission E1 and bhuti
- **CrossFit and strength:** Momentum Training and Gymbox
- **Dance:** FRAME, LoveDance, Salsateca and MB Studios
- **Recovery:** saunas and cold plunges at 1Rebel Reset in Kensington and Clapham
- **Gyms:** Gymbox, Nuffield Health and Fitness4Less, plus private sessions at Solo60

<h2 id="tips">Tips</h2>

- Popular classes in central London book up fast, especially early mornings and after work. Book a few days ahead.
- Credits per class vary by studio and time of day. Off-peak classes are often cheaper.
- Your London plan also works in other UK cities and abroad, wherever ClassPass is available.
