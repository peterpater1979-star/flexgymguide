London has a huge choice of studios and gyms on ClassPass, spread across the whole city. Below is a small selection by area and by type of workout, so you can see what is near you. The full, up-to-date list is always on ClassPass itself.

<h2 id="summary">In short</h2>

| | ClassPass in London |
| --- | --- |
| **Price per month** | Standard plan £69 a month (52 credits) |
| **Try it first** | 14 days free, 10 workouts |
| **Where** | All over London, from the West End and the City to Clapham, Hackney and the suburbs |
| **What you can do** | HIIT, reformer Pilates, Lagree, indoor cycling, boxing, yoga, dance, outdoor classes and gym visits |

<p class="klein">Prices and terms change regularly. Always check the latest details with ClassPass.</p>

<h2 id="areas">By area</h2>

### West End and central London

Barry's in Soho and on Euston Road, 1Rebel at Oxford Circus, Psycle on Mortimer Street, JETSET Pilates in Fitzrovia, MAD in Mayfair for Lagree and Enmei in Marylebone. For a gym to yourself there is Solo60 in Fitzrovia.

### The City and East London

Barry's in Shoreditch, St Paul's and Canary Wharf, Mission E1 in Spitalfields for yoga, Pilates, calisthenics and boxing, Victus Soul in Aldgate, BLOK in Clapton and Yogahome in Stoke Newington. Solo60 also has a private gym near Moorgate.

### North London

1Rebel in Angel and St John's Wood, ĀVĀ Movement in St John's Wood, 3Tribes in Crouch End for hot yoga and Pilates, LIM Studio in Highbury and Ceci Studio for reformer Pilates.

### South London

1Rebel and Psycle in Clapham, Goji in Clapham Old Town, Mind & Motion on Northcote Road, Avalon Pilates in Earlsfield, Milo and the Bull and F45 in Peckham, High Vibe in Brixton and Forma Pilates in Catford.

### West London

1Rebel in Chelsea and on High Street Kensington, Studio Fix on Kensington High Street, El Estudio in Olympia, Barry's in Bayswater and Victoria, JAB Boxing Club near Victoria and The Hot Spot Yoga in Fulham.

<h2 id="types">By type of workout</h2>

- **HIIT and treadmill classes:** Barry's, 1Rebel, Homerun and F45
- **Reformer Pilates and Lagree:** JETSET Pilates, ĀVĀ Movement, Avalon Pilates, Ceci Studio, MAD and Enmei
- **Indoor cycling:** Psycle and 1Rebel
- **Boxing and martial arts:** JAB Boxing Club, Undisputed Fitness & Martial Arts and outdoor boxing with BoxFit in Hyde Park
- **Yoga:** Yogahome, 3Tribes, The Hot Spot Yoga and Mission E1
- **Dance:** LoveDance, Salsateca and MB Studios
- **Gyms:** private sessions at Solo60, plus leisure centres such as Tottenham Green

<h2 id="tips">Tips</h2>

- Popular classes in central London book up fast, especially early mornings and after work. Book a few days ahead.
- Credits per class vary by studio and time of day. Off-peak classes are often cheaper.
- Your London plan also works in other UK cities and abroad, wherever ClassPass is available.
