Freedom Leisure is a not-for-profit charitable trust that runs more than 130 leisure and cultural venues for local councils across England and Wales. Most centres have a gym, a pool and group classes. Memberships and prices are set per area, and a membership usually covers all centres in that area.

<h2 id="memberships">Memberships</h2>

What a membership includes depends on the area. Two examples:

- **Brighton and Hove, Unlimited Membership:** gym at all 7 sites, the 3 city pools, steam rooms and saunas, more than 100 classes a week and 5 one-to-one coaching sessions. Pay monthly or yearly. There is also a Student Membership at a discounted rate.
- **Wealden (Crowborough, Hailsham, Uckfield):** Premier, Family, Swim, Six0 for over-60s and an 11-18 membership, each with access to the three Wealden centres.

Contract, notice period and freezing aren't listed on the centre pages. Ask your centre before you join.

How to cancel: [Cancel Freedom Leisure](/uk/cancel-freedom-leisure/).

## Frequently asked questions

<details open>
<summary>Is Freedom Leisure a council gym?</summary>

Freedom Leisure is a charitable trust that runs leisure centres for local councils. Any surplus goes back into the centres.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Can I use all Freedom Leisure centres?</summary>

Usually only the centres in your own area. In Brighton and Hove the Unlimited Membership covers all 7 sites.
