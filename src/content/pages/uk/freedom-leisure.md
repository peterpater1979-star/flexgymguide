Freedom Leisure is not a gym chain in the usual sense. It is a not-for-profit leisure trust that runs public leisure centres for local councils: gyms, swimming pools, sports halls and classes. Any surplus goes back into the facilities. Because every centre is run for its own council, prices and memberships differ per centre and per area.

<h2 id="memberships">Memberships</h2>

Freedom Leisure designs its memberships per centre, so the options and prices differ. Common types are:

- **Monthly:** paid by Direct Debit, usually around the 1st of the month, without a long contract.
- **Paid in full:** usually 12 months up front. Normally not refundable or transferable.
- **Student and senior:** lower rates at many centres, with proof that you qualify.
- **Family and corporate:** at some centres.

In Brighton & Hove, the council's Leisure Card gives people on certain benefits a discount on selected activities at these centres.

<p class="klein">Prices differ per centre, so we don't list them here. We don't list temporary offers either.</p>

<h2 id="cancel">Cancelling and freezing</h2>

- **Cancel:** give at least one calendar month's notice before your next Direct Debit, with the online cancellation form or at your centre.
- **Direct Debit:** stopping the Direct Debit at your bank does not cancel your membership by itself.
- **Freeze:** only for monthly fitness memberships, in full calendar months, at least one month and normally up to three months in any 12 months. You can freeze after your first Direct Debit. There is a fee, which your centre can tell you.
- **Rejoining:** if your membership lapsed for more than a month, a joining fee may apply.

<p class="klein">Source: Freedom Leisure membership terms (November 2026).</p>

## Frequently asked questions

<details open>
<summary>Is Freedom Leisure a gym chain?</summary>

Not quite. Freedom Leisure is a not-for-profit trust that runs public leisure centres for councils. That's why each centre has its own memberships and prices.

</details>

<details>
<summary>How do I cancel Freedom Leisure?</summary>

Give at least one calendar month's notice before your next Direct Debit, with the online form or at your centre. Only stopping the Direct Debit is not enough.

</details>
