You cancel F45 through your own studio, or with the cancel form on the F45 website, which your studio then checks. Every studio is independently owned, so the notice period and any early termination fee depend on your studio.

<h2 id="summary">In short</h2>

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Where** | Your own studio, or the cancel form on the F45 website |
| **Notice** | Set by your studio; see your membership agreement |
| **Early exit** | An early termination fee may apply, set by your studio |
| **Studio closes** | Your membership is cancelled automatically |

<h2 id="steps">How to cancel</h2>

1. **Check your agreement** for the notice period and any minimum term.
2. **Contact your studio** or fill in the cancel form on the F45 website.
3. **Wait for the confirmation:** your membership isn't cancelled until the studio has processed it.
4. **Stop your payment** only after your last payment.

<h2 id="when">Good to know</h2>

- Billing and refunds go through your own studio, not through F45 head office.
- Cancelling in writing, by email, always gives you proof of the date. Keep a copy and the confirmation.

## Frequently asked questions

<details open>
<summary>How do I cancel F45?</summary>

Through your local studio, or with the cancel form on the F45 website. The studio processes your request.

</details>

<details>
<summary>How much notice do I need to give?</summary>

That depends on your studio and membership. Check your agreement or ask your studio.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Is my membership cancelled straight away?</summary>

No. You get a confirmation once the studio has processed your request.

</details>
