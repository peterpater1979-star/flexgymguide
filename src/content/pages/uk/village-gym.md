Village Gym is a chain of 34 health clubs at Village Hotels across the UK. Every club has a gym floor, a heated indoor pool, a sauna and a steam room, and more than 100 group classes a week. You choose a Peak or Off Peak membership, with a 12-month agreement or a shorter flexible term. Each club sets its own prices.

<h2 id="memberships">Memberships</h2>

| Membership | Access | Term |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Peak Results** | Any time the club is open | Initial 12-month agreement, pay monthly or a year up front |
| **Peak Flexible** | Any time the club is open | At least 3 months, then 90 days' notice |
| **Off Peak Results** | Off-peak hours on weekdays, any time at weekends | Initial 12-month agreement |
| **Off Peak Flexible** | Off-peak hours on weekdays, any time at weekends | At least 3 months, then 90 days' notice |

Off-peak hours differ per club, for example 8am to 5pm on weekdays in Newcastle, Nottingham and Cheadle. There are also memberships for students, for 16 to 22-year-olds, for NHS and emergency services staff, and through some employers. Teenagers of 14 and 15 can join, but only train with a parent or guardian in the club.

<h2 id="prices">Prices</h2>

Village Gym has no national price list: each club sets its own monthly price and joining fee, and the website shows the price once you choose a club. With a Results membership (12 months) you pay a lower joining fee, according to the membership terms.

Want to try a club first? Buy a day pass online. An adult day pass at Village Gym Cheadle cost £25 in November 2026, including the pool, sauna, steam room and whirlpool. You use it within 7 days of buying.

Ask your own club about:

- the monthly price for Peak and Off Peak
- the joining fee
- a student or 16–22 discount

<p class="klein">Prices without temporary offers.</p>

<h2 id="included">What's included</h2>

- Gym floor with Technogym equipment
- More than 100 group classes a week
- Heated indoor pool, sauna, steam room and spa pool
- 25% off food and drink in the Village Pub & Grill, and 20% off hotel stays
- Squash courts at some clubs, such as Cheadle, Hyde and Bury
- Some clubs offer extras at an additional cost, such as Reformer Pilates in Glasgow

Most clubs open at 5am on weekdays and close at 10 or 11pm. At weekends they usually open between 6 and 8am.

<h2 id="cancel">Cancelling and freezing</h2>

- **Results (12 months):** to stop when your 12 months are up, give one full month's notice. Cancelling your direct debit early does not end the agreement: the remaining months are still due.
- **Flexible:** you commit to at least 3 months, and after that you give 90 days' notice.
- **How:** by email to Member Services. Cancelling your direct debit is not the same as cancelling.
- **Freeze:** from 1 to 12 months, at £10 a month. Ask before the 20th of the month before. A freeze extends your 12-month term.
- **Joining fee:** not refunded, even if you cancel in the first days.

<h2 id="other-clubs">Training at other clubs</h2>

Your membership is for one home club. You can visit other Village clubs with an upgraded membership or by paying a guest fee. If you train more often at another club than at your own, Village can move your membership to that club, possibly at a higher price.
