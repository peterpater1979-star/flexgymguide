You pay monthly or choose a Saver plan: 9 or 12 months paid up front, or in 3 instalments, for a lower price per month. Monthly memberships have a joining fee; Saver plans don't. Prices differ per gym and are usually higher in London.

<h2 id="students">Student membership</h2>

Students can choose a student Saver plan for 6, 9 or 12 months, paid up front and without a joining fee. It includes classes and 24/7 access, and you can use 2 gyms: one near your university and one back home. Some gyms can't be chosen as a second gym.

<p class="klein">Check the price for your gym on The Gym Group website.</p>

<h2 id="facilities">Facilities</h2>

The gyms focus on training: there's no pool, sauna or café. What you do get, taking London Angel as an example:

| Area | What's there |
| --- | --- |
| **Cardio** | Treadmills, bikes, cross trainers, Concept2 rowers and climbing machines |
| **Free weights** | Dumbbells from 2 to 50 kg, adjustable benches and Olympic lifting platforms with racks |
| **Machines** | Plate-loaded stations, a Smith machine, leg press, chest and shoulder press and cable machines |
| **HIIT zone** | Kettlebells, battle ropes, ski ergs and an assault bike |

Every gym has changing rooms with showers and lockers. Changing rooms close between 1 and 3 a.m. Wi-Fi is free.

<p class="klein">The equipment differs per gym. Check the page of your gym on The Gym Group website.</p>

<h2 id="classes">Classes</h2>

Classes are included in every membership, also in Off-peak. Depending on the gym, you can choose from HYROX training, strength and conditioning, cardio, mind and body, and Fundamentals classes for beginners. Every gym has its own timetable on the website, and you book a class in the app.

<h2 id="access">Access, app and opening hours</h2>

You get in with your own PIN or the QR code in the app. In the app you can also see how busy your gym is, book classes and track your workouts.

Most gyms are open 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. A few gyms close at night, and with Off-peak you only train at quieter times. Members under 18 complete an online induction before their PIN works.

<h2 id="personal-training">Personal training</h2>

Many gyms have personal trainers you can hire for extra coaching. Prices are set per trainer; ask at your gym or check its page on the website.

<h2 id="cancelling">Contract and cancelling</h2>

<div class="kolommen">

<div class="kolom">

### Monthly plans

No contract and no minimum term. Cancel at least four working days before your next payment date. In the first 14 days you can cancel and get a refund, minus the cost of any visits.

</div>

<div class="kolom">

### Saver plans

You pay for 9 or 12 months up front. After the first 14 days you can't cancel early or get a refund for unused months, and you can't freeze a Saver plan.

</div>

</div>

## Frequently asked questions

<details open>
<summary>Does The Gym Group have a contract?</summary>

Not on monthly plans. Saver plans run for 9 or 12 months and are paid up front.

</details>

<details>
<summary>How do I cancel my The Gym Group membership?</summary>

Cancel a monthly membership at least four working days before your next payment date. Within 14 days of joining you can cancel and get a refund, minus the cost of any visits.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Can I use every gym?</summary>

Only with Ultimate. Off-peak and Standard give access to your home gym.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Are classes free at The Gym Group?</summary>

Yes, classes are included in every membership, including Off-peak. You book them in the app.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Does The Gym Group have a student membership?</summary>

Yes, student Saver plans for 6, 9 or 12 months, paid up front without a joining fee. You can use 2 gyms, for example near your university and back home.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Is The Gym Group open 24/7?</summary>

Most gyms are, with Standard or Ultimate. Off-peak only gives access at quieter hours. Check the opening hours of your gym on the website.

</details>
