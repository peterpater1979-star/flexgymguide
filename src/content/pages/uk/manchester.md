Manchester has a big choice on ClassPass, from HIIT at Barry's and F45 to reformer Pilates around Deansgate, the Northern Quarter and Ancoats. For a gym without a contract there are The Gym Group, with 8 gyms in and around the city, and PureGym, and David Lloyd, Virgin Active, Anytime Fitness and Village Gym are here too. Here's what each costs and how flexible it is. Don't want a contract? Read [Gyms without a contract in Manchester](/uk/gyms-without-contract-manchester/).

<h3>Village Gym</h3>

Village Gym has four health clubs around Manchester: in Ashton Moss, Bury, Cheadle and Hyde. Each has a gym floor, an indoor pool, a sauna and more than 100 classes a week, and Cheadle, Hyde and Bury also have squash courts. A day pass costs £25 at Cheadle. You choose Peak or Off Peak, with a 12-month agreement or a Flexible term of at least 3 months.

## ClassPass in Manchester

### Where

A good choice, mostly in the city centre around Deansgate, Spinningfields, the Northern Quarter and Ancoats, and also in Salford, Stockport and Oldham. Here is a small selection. For HIIT there are Barry's, TRIB3 and F45, and for indoor cycling there is RYDE. For reformer Pilates there are Bondi Pilates, The Reformer Studio, Formula MCR, MMOVE and Kore Studios, and for treadmill and sculpt classes there is RE:SCULPT. You can also do hot classes at Deansgate Haus, yoga at Yoga Republic and bodyweight training at Manchester Calisthenics.

[See the studios in Manchester on ClassPass.](https://classpass.com/search/manchester/fitness-classes/3vG4r9qNkJK)

In Manchester the standard plan costs £49 a month for 36 credits, and you can try it first with 14 days free and 10 workouts. How credits work: [ClassPass credits explained](/guides/classpass-credits/).
