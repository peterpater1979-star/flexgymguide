## ClassPass in Manchester

With ClassPass you get monthly credits to book classes and gym visits at studios across the city. Ideal if you like variety and don't want to commit to one place.

### Where

A good choice, mostly in the city centre around Deansgate, Spinningfields, the Northern Quarter and Ancoats, and also in Salford, Stockport and Oldham. Here is a small selection. For HIIT there are Barry's, TRIB3 and F45, and for indoor cycling there is RYDE. For reformer Pilates there are Bondi Pilates, The Reformer Studio, Formula MCR, MMOVE and Kore Studios, and for treadmill and sculpt classes there is RE:SCULPT. You can also do hot classes at Deansgate Haus, yoga at Yoga Republic and bodyweight training at Manchester Calisthenics.

[See the studios in Manchester on ClassPass.](https://classpass.com/search/manchester/fitness-classes/3vG4r9qNkJK)

### What you get

- Monthly credits for classes and gym visits
- Standard plan in Manchester: 36 credits for £49 a month
- 14 days free with 10 workouts to try it out
- Studios in the city centre, the Northern Quarter, Chorlton and Salford
- Use it in more than 2,500 other cities in 31 countries

### Good for

- People who like to try different workouts
- Training one to three times a week
- Students and frequent travellers

### Watch out for

- Credits per class vary by studio and time of day
- Unused credits roll over for one month. If you still don't use them, they expire

## The Gym Group in Manchester

The Gym Group has 8 gyms in and around Manchester, including Ancoats, Oxford Road, Portland Street, Fallowfield and Old Trafford. Monthly memberships have no contract, and most gyms are open 24/7.

### What you get

- A gym floor with classes included
- 24/7 access at most gyms with Standard or Ultimate
- Access to other gyms with Ultimate

### Good for

- Training three times a week or more on a budget
- Students and anyone who wants no contract

### Watch out for

- Off-peak only gives access at quieter hours
- 24/7 access is not available at every gym

## PureGym in Manchester

PureGym is a budget gym chain with over 450 gyms in the UK, including 9 gyms in Manchester. Most are open 24/7. Every plan is without a contract, prices start at £18.99 a month for Off-Peak, and classes are included at most gyms.

### Good for

- Training three times a week or more for little money
- Not being tied to a contract

### Keep in mind

- The price differs per gym
- Off-Peak only gives access outside peak hours
- Joining fee from £10

## Frequently asked questions

<details open>
<summary>Is ClassPass cheaper than a gym membership in Manchester?</summary>

It depends on how often you train and what you want to do. If you mostly use the gym floor, a low-cost gym is usually cheaper. If you want to mix studios and classes, a fitness pass is usually the better fit.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Can I use ClassPass outside Manchester?</summary>

Yes. You can use your credits in other UK cities and abroad, wherever ClassPass is available.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Does The Gym Group have a contract?</summary>

Not on monthly plans. Saver plans run for 9 or 12 months in exchange for a lower price.
