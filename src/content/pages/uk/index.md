Working out without a long contract at one gym comes down to two options: a fitness pass like ClassPass, or a gym chain with flexible terms. Here's what suits you, and in which cities.

<h2 id="opties">Four ways to train flexibly</h2>

<div class="kolommen">

<div class="kolom kaart aanbiederkaart">

<div class="fotokop foto-classpass"><span>Fitness pass</span></div>

### ClassPass

One membership with credits for yoga, pilates, boxing, spin and gym visits at studios across the city, and abroad too.

- Good if you like variety
- Good if you train one to three times a week
- Works in 30 other countries

<div class="kolom kaart aanbiederkaart">

<div class="fotokop foto-fitness-first-uk"><span>Gym chain</span></div>

### Fitness First

A membership at one chain with a gym floor and classes. In the UK for example Fitness First, with 25 clubs, 17 of them in London.

- Good if you train three times a week or more
- Good if you want one fixed place
- Usually UK only

<div class="fotokop foto-gymketen"><span>Low-cost gym</span></div>

### The Gym Group

Low-cost gyms without a contract, most of them open 24/7. Over 260 gyms across the UK, with classes included.

<div class="knoppen">
<a class="knop knop-rand" href="/uk/the-gym-group/">All about The Gym Group</a>

<div class="kolom kaart aanbiederkaart">

<div class="fotokop foto-puregym"><span>Budget gym chain</span></div>

### PureGym

Budget gym chain with over 450 gyms in the UK, most open 24/7. No contract on any plan, from £18.99 a month, with classes at most gyms.

- Good if you want to train cheaply and often
- No contract: cancel anytime
- Price differs per gym

## Frequently asked questions

<details open>
<summary>How much does ClassPass cost in the UK?</summary>

It depends on the number of credits per month. Check the current prices with ClassPass.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Is ClassPass cheaper than a gym?</summary>

If you go to the gym three times a week or more, a regular gym is usually cheaper. If you like switching between studios, a fitness pass suits you better. More in [Gym pass or gym membership?](/guides/gym-pass-or-membership/)

</details>

<details>
<summary>Can I use ClassPass on holiday?</summary>

Yes. Your credits work in every country where ClassPass is active, at that country's prices.

</details>
