You cancel a Nuffield Health gym membership in writing to your home gym, by email or with a form at the gym. You give a calendar month's notice, and your membership always ends at the end of a calendar month.

<h2 id="summary">In short</h2>

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Where** | In writing to your home gym: by email or with a form at the gym |
| **Notice** | A calendar month. Notice received on the 1st ends that month; later, it ends at the end of the next month |
| **12-month commitment** | Leave early only for set reasons, such as pregnancy, illness, redundancy or moving away |
| **Cooling-off** | 14 days with a full refund if you joined online |
| **Freeze** | Free for set reasons, otherwise a paid freeze of up to 3 months |

<h2 id="steps">How to cancel</h2>

1. **Check your payment option:** standard monthly, or a discounted 12-month commitment.
2. **Email your home gym** or fill in the form at the gym. Send it on the 1st of the month if you can, so your membership ends at the end of that month.
3. **Keep your direct debit running** until your membership ends. One more payment is usually taken.
4. **Keep the confirmation** and check your end date.

<h2 id="when">Good to know</h2>

- Notice on 10 May means your membership ends on 30 June, so the timing matters.
- Within a 12-month commitment you need a reason and sometimes proof, such as a doctor's letter or proof of your new address.
- Freezing is not possible during your notice period.

## Frequently asked questions

<summary>How do I cancel Nuffield Health?</summary>

In writing to your home gym, by email or with a form at the gym, with a calendar month's notice.

</details>

<details>
<summary>When does my membership end?</summary>

At the end of a calendar month. Notice that arrives on the 1st ends that month; notice later in the month ends at the end of the next month.

<details>
