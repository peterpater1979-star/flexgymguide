Snap Fitness is a chain of small 24/7 gyms with more than 100 clubs in the UK, mostly in smaller towns and suburbs. Each club is a franchise and sets its own prices. Classes are included, and with a full membership you can also train at Snap Fitness clubs across the UK and abroad.

<h2 id="cancel">Cancelling and freezing</h2>

- **Cancel:** by email to your home club, with 30 days' notice from the day they receive your email.
- **Fixed term:** the 30 days' notice only applies after your first term, for example 12 months. Leaving earlier costs a fee set out in your membership terms.
- **Freeze:** up to 3 months a year according to Snap's FAQs, at up to [PRICE] a month depending on the club. Some clubs have their own rules, so check with yours. You can't cancel while your membership is frozen.

## Frequently asked questions

<details open>
<summary>How do I cancel Snap Fitness?</summary>

Email your home club. On a rolling membership you give 30 days' notice, starting from the day they receive your email. On a fixed term, notice only applies after the first term.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Can I use other Snap Fitness clubs?</summary>

Yes, with a full membership you can train at Snap Fitness clubs across the UK and worldwide. A Home Gym Only membership is for your own club only.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Is Snap Fitness open 24/7?</summary>

Yes. Members get in at any time with their access card or the app. Staff are there at set hours only.

</details>
