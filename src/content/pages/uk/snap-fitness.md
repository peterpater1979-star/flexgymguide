<h2 id="memberships">Memberships</h2>

Every Snap Fitness gym sets its own prices. You see the options for your gym when you join on the Snap Fitness website, or you ask the staff.

- **Monthly:** the usual membership, with access to other Snap Fitness gyms.
- **Term:** some gyms offer a fixed term. It renews automatically after the term, and stopping early costs an early cancellation fee of £75.
- **Pre-paid:** paid up front and not refundable. It is only valid at your home gym.
- **Students:** some gyms have a rolling monthly student membership; you need a student ID.
- **Access card:** you get an access card or app key for 24/7 entry. Cards are not refundable, and a replacement costs extra.

<p class="klein">We don't list temporary offers or free trials.</p>

<h2 id="classes">Classes and opening hours</h2>

Group classes are included at most gyms. Some gyms also have extras such as reformer Pilates or a sauna. You can train 24 hours a day with your access card. Staff are there at set times, which differ per gym.

<h2 id="cancel">Freezing and cancelling</h2>

- **Cancel:** email your home gym. Your membership ends on the last day of the month that is at least 30 days after your notice. You pay until that date.
- **Cooling-off:** you can cancel within 14 days of joining. You then pay only for the days you used, at the day pass rate.
- **Term membership:** stopping early costs £75.
- **Freeze:** at least one month and up to three months a year. Tell your gym in writing at least five days before your next payment. You pay £3 for each frozen month, and you can't cancel during a freeze.
- **Another gym:** if you train more at another Snap Fitness gym than at your own, after 60 days your membership moves to that gym, at that gym's price.

<p class="klein">Source: Snap Fitness UK membership terms (July 2026). Terms can differ per gym.</p>

<h2 id="age">Minimum age</h2>

You usually need to be 16, but this differs per gym.
