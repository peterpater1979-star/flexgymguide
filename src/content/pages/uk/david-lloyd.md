David Lloyd is a chain of large health clubs with more than 100 clubs in the UK and clubs in eight other European countries. Every club has an indoor pool and group classes, and most also have an outdoor pool, tennis courts and a spa. Families are welcome: children can join on a parent's membership, and most clubs have a crèche and a Kids Club. Each club sets its own prices.

<h2 id="memberships">Memberships</h2>

| Membership | What you get |
| --- | --- |
| **Platinum** | Gym, pools, classes and racquet sports at any time, plus Blaze classes, the Spa Retreat at clubs that have one, a fresh towel every visit, a monthly guest pass at most clubs and access to selected other clubs |
| **Club Plus** | Gym, pools and classes at any time, at your own club |
| **Club** | Gym, pools and classes at off-peak times: 11am to 4pm on weekdays and after 2pm at weekends |

You choose each membership as **Standard**, with an initial term of 12 calendar months, or **Flexible**, with an initial term of 3 calendar months. A few clubs, such as Notting Hill and Colliers Wood, don't offer Flexible. You can join on your own, as a couple, or add children aged up to 18 to an adult membership.

<h2 id="prices">Prices</h2>

David Lloyd has no national price list: each club sets its own monthly price, and the website doesn't show prices. You get them when you enquire online or visit the club. On top of the monthly fee you pay a one-off administration fee when you join.

Ask your own club about:

- the monthly price for Platinum, Club Plus and Club
- the difference between Standard and Flexible
- the joining or administration fee
- the price for a couple or with children

<p class="klein">Prices without temporary offers.</p>

<h2 id="included">What's included</h2>

- Gym floor and hundreds of group classes a week
- Indoor pool at every club, and an outdoor pool at most clubs
- Tennis, padel, pickleball, squash or badminton at many clubs, with Platinum
- Sauna and steam room, and a Spa Retreat at some clubs
- Crèche and Kids Club at most clubs
- 10% off in the bar and restaurant with Platinum

<h2 id="cancel">Cancelling and freezing</h2>

- **Standard:** to stop after the first 12 months, give one calendar month's notice before the last month starts. If you stay on, you give three calendar months' notice.
- **Flexible:** after the first 3 months, you give one calendar month's notice.
- **How:** in writing, via the app, by email, by post or at the club.
- **Freeze:** once a year for 2 to 9 months, at 25% of your monthly fee. With a medical reason you pay nothing. A freeze in your first term makes that term longer.
- **Early exit:** possible with proof, for example after a medical condition, losing your job or moving more than 10 miles from a club.

<h2 id="other-clubs">Training at other clubs</h2>

Club Plus and Club are for your home club. With Platinum you can use selected other clubs. At least half of your visits each month should be at your home club; if not, David Lloyd can move your membership to the club you use most, possibly at a higher price.

## Frequently asked questions

<details open>
<summary>How much is David Lloyd?</summary>

It depends on the club, the membership and whether you join alone, as a couple or with children. David Lloyd doesn't show prices online: you get them when you enquire or visit the club.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Does David Lloyd have a contract?</summary>

Yes. A Standard membership has an initial term of 12 months, a Flexible membership 3 months. After that you give three months' notice (Standard) or one month (Flexible).

</details>

<details>
<summary>Can I freeze my David Lloyd membership?</summary>

Yes, once a year for 2 to 9 months. You pay 25% of your monthly fee, or nothing with a medical reason.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Can I use other David Lloyd clubs?</summary>

With Platinum you can use selected other clubs. Club Plus and Club are for your home club only.

</details>
