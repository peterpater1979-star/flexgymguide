<h2 id="memberships">Memberships</h2>

- **Monthly (direct debit):** rolling, no fixed term. Unlimited 24/7 access and all classes.
- **Paid In Advance:** you pay up front for a set period. No refund during that period.
- **Off Peak:** weekdays from 10pm to 4pm, and all day at weekends.
- **Staffed Access:** only during staffed hours.

Every membership starts with a 7-day cooling-off period: cancel within 7 days by email and you get your fees back.

<h2 id="cancel">Cancelling and freezing</h2>

- **Cancel:** by email to your home gym only, not by phone or social media. Your request must arrive by the 1st of the month. Depending on the date, you pay one or two more monthly payments.
- **Paid In Advance:** runs until the end of the period you paid for.
- **Freeze:** possible under the Jetts hold policy. Ask your gym about the length and cost. You can't cancel while your membership is frozen.

## Frequently asked questions

<details open>
<summary>Does Jetts have a contract?</summary>

No. The monthly membership is rolling, without a lock-in contract. You cancel by email to your gym before the 1st of the month.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Is Jetts open 24/7?</summary>

Yes. Members have 24-hour access every day. Staff are there at set hours only.

</details>

<details>
