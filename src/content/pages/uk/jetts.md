Jetts is a 24/7 gym chain from Australia, with clubs in Australia, New Zealand, Thailand and a small number in the UK. Each UK gym is run by a franchise owner. Jetts has no lock-in contracts, and classes are included.

<h2 id="memberships">Membership</h2>

- **No lock-in contract:** you pay per month and aren't tied to a fixed term.
- **Access card:** you pay a one-time fee for your access card.
- **Classes:** included in the membership.

Ask your gym about the monthly price, the access card fee and the notice period.

<p class="klein">We don't list temporary offers or free trials.</p>

<h2 id="cancel">Cancelling</h2>

Jetts has no lock-in contracts, so you can stop your monthly membership. Ask your gym how much notice you need to give and how to cancel, and get a written confirmation.
