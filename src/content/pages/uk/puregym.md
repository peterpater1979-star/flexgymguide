PureGym is a budget gym chain with over 450 gyms across the UK, most of them open 24/7. Every plan is without a contract, so you can cancel anytime, and classes are included at most gyms.

<h2 id="summary">At a glance</h2>

| | PureGym |
| --- | --- |
| **Type** | Budget gym chain |
| **Gyms** | Over 450 in the UK |
| **Price** | From £18.99 a month (Off-Peak); varies by gym |
| **Contract** | <span class="ja" aria-hidden="true">✓</span> No contract, cancel anytime |
| **Joining fee** | From £10 |
| **Classes** | <span class="ja" aria-hidden="true">✓</span> Included at most gyms |
| **Opening hours** | 24/7 at most gyms |

<h2 id="plans">The plans</h2>

| Plan | What you get |
| --- | --- |
| **Off-Peak** | Your home gym outside peak hours; the cheapest plan, from £18.99 a month |
| **Core** | Your home gym, any time it's open |
| **Plus** | More gyms and extras; book classes 14 days ahead instead of 8 |

What each plan costs depends on the gym. You'll see the exact price when you pick a gym on the PureGym site.

<h2 id="classpass">PureGym or ClassPass?</h2>

Do you go to the same gym three times a week or more and want to pay as little as possible? Then PureGym is often the cheaper choice. Want to switch between yoga, pilates, boxing and spinning in different studios, or do you work out one to three times a week? Then ClassPass often suits you better.
