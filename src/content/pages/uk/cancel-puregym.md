You cancel a PureGym monthly membership yourself, in the Members' Area of the PureGym app or website. Do it at least 4 working days before your next payment date. There's no contract, so your membership simply ends the day before that next payment.

<h2 id="summary">In short</h2>

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Where** | Members' Area in the PureGym app or on the website (Online Cancellations) |
| **Deadline** | At least 4 working days before your next payment date |
| **Contract** | None on monthly memberships |
| **Membership ends** | The day before your next payment is due |
| **Cooling-off** | 14 days, for all memberships |
| **Fixed term (6, 9 or 12 months)** | Ends automatically; leaving early gives 50% back of the remaining period |

<h2 id="steps">How to cancel</h2>

1. **Log in to the Members' Area** in the PureGym app or on the website.
2. **Use Online Cancellations** to cancel your direct debit, at least 4 working days before your payment date.
3. **Keep the confirmation.** Your membership runs until the day before your next payment is due, then stops.

You can also cancel the direct debit with your bank, but allow 4 working days for the bank to do it.

<h2 id="when">Good to know</h2>

- **Cooling-off.** For all memberships you have 14 days after joining to change your mind. Email member.services@puregym.com. You get your money back, minus an amount for any use of the gym if you already started.
- **Fixed-term memberships** (6, 9 or 12 months) end automatically, with no auto-renewal. Leave early by emailing Member Services: you can ask for 50% of the fees for the rest of the term back.
- **Price rise.** PureGym gives at least 30 days' notice of a higher monthly price. You can cancel online during that time.
- **Day passes** can't be refunded.

<h2 id="freeze">Freeze instead of cancelling?</h2>

Depending on your membership, you may be able to freeze it, for free or at a cost. What applies to you is in the joining email PureGym sent you.
