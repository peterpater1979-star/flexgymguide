Nuffield Health runs more than 100 Fitness & Wellbeing gyms across the UK. Most have a gym floor and group classes, and many also have a pool, a sauna and a steam room. Every club sets its own price, and with most memberships you can also train at other Nuffield Health gyms.

<h2 id="prices">Prices</h2>

Nuffield Health has no single national price. Each club shows its own monthly price on its page, based on a 12-month membership. Clubs in central London usually cost more than clubs elsewhere in the country.

Ask your own club about:

- the monthly price and the term
- the joining fee
- which other Nuffield Health gyms you can use with your membership

<p class="klein">We don't list temporary offers or discount codes.</p>

<h2 id="cancel">Cancelling and freezing</h2>

- **Notice:** you need to give one calendar month's notice to cancel.
- **How:** in writing to your home gym, by email or with a cancellation form at the gym.
- **During the commitment period:** you can only cancel early for set reasons, such as serious illness or injury, pregnancy, redundancy, moving more than 5 miles from a Nuffield Health gym, or your home gym closing. You may need to show proof.
- **Freeze:** you can freeze your membership for up to 12 months, also in your first 3 months, for set reasons such as illness, injury, pregnancy or redundancy. You fill in a form at your home gym.

<p class="klein">Source: the Nuffield Health cancel and freeze page and its fair and honest contract (November 2026).</p>

## Frequently asked questions

<details open>
<summary>How much is Nuffield Health?</summary>

It depends on the club. Each club shows its monthly price on its own page, based on a 12-month membership. Ask your club about the joining fee and other terms.

</details>

<details>
<summary>How do I cancel my Nuffield Health membership?</summary>

Send your home gym a written request, by email or with a form at the gym. You need to give one calendar month's notice. During the commitment period you can only cancel early for set reasons.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Do all Nuffield Health gyms have a pool?</summary>

No. Many do, but facilities differ per club. Check your club's page before you join.

</details>
