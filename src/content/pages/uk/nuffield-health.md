<h2 id="memberships">Memberships</h2>

- **Connections+:** all Nuffield Health gyms during opening hours.
- **Connections:** all gyms except Barbican, City, Islington and Moorgate.
- **Premier:** the gyms within your group, or your home gym only.
- **Off-Peak:** the gyms in your group, at off-peak hours.

You choose how to pay: standard monthly (you commit to one full calendar month), quarterly in advance, or a discounted option with a 12-month commitment, paid monthly or upfront. There are joint and family memberships, and discounts for students, young adults aged 16 and 17, and some employers. An activation fee may apply, as shown on your membership form.

Use your home gym at least half of the time. Visiting a gym outside your group costs a guest fee at half the normal rate.

<h2 id="cancel">Cancelling and freezing</h2>

- **Cancel:** in writing to your home gym, by email or with a form at the gym. You give a calendar month's notice. If your notice arrives on the 1st, your membership ends on the last day of that month; later in the month, it ends at the end of the next month. Keep your direct debit running until then.
- **12-month commitment:** you can only leave early for set reasons, such as pregnancy, serious illness or injury, redundancy, or moving more than 5 miles from any Nuffield Health gym.
- **Online sign-up:** a 14-day cooling-off period with a full refund.
- **Freeze:** free for 1 to 12 full calendar months in case of pregnancy, serious illness or injury, redundancy or an office move. For other reasons a paid freeze of up to 3 months is possible at [PRICE] a month, paid upfront. A request received by the 15th starts on the 1st of the next month.

## Frequently asked questions

<details open>
<summary>How do I cancel Nuffield Health?</summary>

In writing to your home gym, by email or with a form at the gym. You give a calendar month's notice, and your membership ends at the end of a calendar month.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Can I freeze my Nuffield Health membership?</summary>

Yes. A freeze is free for pregnancy, serious illness or injury, redundancy or an office move. Otherwise you can pay for a freeze of up to 3 months. You can't use the gym while your membership is frozen.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Can I use other Nuffield Health gyms?</summary>

Yes, depending on your membership. Connections+ covers all gyms, Connections all except four in central London, and Premier and Off-Peak the gyms in your group.

</details>
