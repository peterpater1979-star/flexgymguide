<h2 id="areas">By area</h2>

A few examples per area. There are more studios than the ones listed here: you'll find the full list on ClassPass.

### New Town and the West End

Tribe New Town on Northumberland Street, Luma Pilates in the New Town, Mena Reformer Pilates on Saint Colme Street and Pointe Ahead Pilates on Atholl Place. Tallie Lagree Fitness Studio on Greenside Place is the only Lagree studio in Scotland, and The WORX on Conference Square combines ski, bike and rowing machines.

### Leith

Tribe Leith Walk, Tidal Yoga on Leith Walk, Fitness Soul on Tennant Street, Low Tide BJJ for martial arts and Adapt Strength Gym on Tower Street. Synergy Group Fitness and The Club Edinburgh are on West Bowling Green Street, and Persevere Fitness is near London Road.

### Stockbridge and Comely Bank

Tribe Stockbridge for indoor cycling, F45 in Tanfield and Yoga's Got Hot in Comely Bank for hot, warm and room temperature yoga.

### South Edinburgh and Fountainbridge

Tribe Quartermile and Tribe Fountainbridge for yoga and reformer Pilates, Studio Sollo in Fountainbridge, Lila Yoga and Pilates Project in Bruntsfield, and gym visits at Nuffield Health Fountain Park, LIFT Gyms and Energy Gym.

### Around Edinburgh

CORE. The Studio and Love Fitness Midlothian in Dalkeith, and GRAFT Training in Loanhead.

<h2 id="types">By type of workout</h2>

A selection of studios for each type of workout. You'll find all studios on ClassPass.

- **Reformer Pilates and Lagree:** among others, Tallie Lagree Fitness Studio, Tribe, Mena Reformer Pilates, Luma Pilates, Studio Sollo, Pointe Ahead Pilates and Pilates Project
- **Yoga:** among others, Tribe, Tidal Yoga, Yoga's Got Hot, Lila Yoga and The Yoga Shala
- **Indoor cycling and rowing:** among others, Tribe Stockbridge and The WORX
- **Strength and HIIT:** among others, F45, CrossFit MTS, Synergy Group Fitness, The PT Hub and Adapt Strength Gym
- **Martial arts:** among others, Low Tide BJJ and Yee's Hung Ga Kung Fu
- **Outdoor:** among others, TRX Edinburgh Outdoor Fitness and Edinburgh Bootcamp
- **Gyms:** among others, Nuffield Health at Omni, Fountain Park and New Mart Road, Village Gym, Energy Gym and Spartan Gym

<h2 id="tips">Tips</h2>

- Popular studios in the New Town book up fast, especially early mornings and after work. Book a few days ahead.
- Credits per class vary by studio and time of day. Off-peak classes are often cheaper.
- Your plan also works in Glasgow, the rest of the UK and abroad, wherever ClassPass is available.
