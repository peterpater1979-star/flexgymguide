## ClassPass in London

With ClassPass you get monthly credits to book classes and gym visits at studios across the city. Ideal if you like variety and don't want to commit to one place.

### Where

A huge choice, spread across the whole city, from HIIT at Barry's and 1Rebel to reformer Pilates, boxing, yoga and private gyms. See ClassPass in London for studios by area and type of workout.

[See the studios in London by area and type of workout.](/uk/classpass-london/)

### What you get

- Monthly credits for classes and gym visits
- Standard plan in London: 52 credits for £69 a month
- 14 days free with 10 workouts to try it out
- Studios all over London, from yoga to boxing
- Use it in more than 2,500 other cities in 31 countries

### Good for

- People who like to try different workouts
- Training one to three times a week
- Expats and frequent travellers

### Watch out for

- Credits per class vary by studio and time of day
- Unused credits roll over for one month. If you still don't use them, they expire

## Fitness First in London

Fitness First has 25 clubs, 17 of them in London. A membership gives you the gym floor, group classes and access to other clubs at your membership tier. Not sure yet? Try a club first with a guest pass.

### What you get

- Access to every club at your membership tier or below
- Group classes across the network
- Personal trainers on hand for coaching

### Good for

- Training three times a week or more
- Living or working in London

### Watch out for

- A single-club membership gives access to one club only
- A cheaper tier does not get you into more expensive clubs
- Leaving before your minimum term ends means paying the rest of the term, or a settlement fee for the discount you had

## PureGym in London

PureGym is a budget gym chain with over 450 gyms in the UK, including 90 gyms in London. Most are open 24/7. Every plan is without a contract, prices start at £18.99 a month for Off-Peak, and classes are included at most gyms.

### Good for

- Training three times a week or more for little money
- Not being tied to a contract

### Keep in mind

- The price differs per gym
- Off-Peak only gives access outside peak hours
- Joining fee from £10

## Frequently asked questions

<details open>
<summary>Is ClassPass cheaper than a gym membership in London?</summary>

It depends on how often you train and what you want to do. If you mostly use the gym floor, compare the monthly membership fee with your number of visits. If you want to mix studios and classes, a fitness pass is usually the better fit.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Can I use ClassPass outside London?</summary>

Yes. You can use your credits in other UK cities and abroad, wherever ClassPass is available.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Can I use any Fitness First club?</summary>

That depends on your membership. You can use any club at your membership tier or below. A single-club membership gives access to one club only.

</details>
