Don’t want to be tied to one gym for a year? In the UK you can also train with a membership you cancel any time, or only pay when you go. These are your options.

| | Cancelling | Price | Good for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **ClassPass** | Anytime, before your renewal date | For example 36 credits for £49 a month; varies by city | Variety at studios in your city |
| **PureGym** | At least 4 working days before your next payment | From £18.99 a month (Off-Peak), plus a joining fee from £10 | Training often for little money, mostly 24/7 |

<h2 id="classpass">ClassPass: cancel anytime</h2>

With ClassPass you get credits every month for classes and gym time at studios in your city. You cancel anytime before your renewal date.

<h2 id="letop">What to watch out for</h2>

- **Joining fees.** Many gyms charge a one-off fee, also without a contract.
- **Notice.** “No contract” doesn’t always mean you can stop the same day: check how far ahead you need to cancel.
- **How often you go.** If you train three times a week or more at the same place, a cheap gym usually works out cheaper per visit than a pass. If you want variety, a pass suits you better.
