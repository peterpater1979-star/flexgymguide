You cancel Snap Fitness by email to your home club. On a rolling membership you give 30 days' notice. A fixed term, for example 12 months, runs until the end of that term unless you pay the early exit fee in your agreement.

<h2 id="summary">In short</h2>

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Where** | By email to your home club |
| **Notice** | 30 days from the day the club receives your email |
| **Fixed term** | Runs until the end of the term; leaving earlier costs a fee |
| **Freeze** | Up to 3 months a year, at a small monthly fee depending on the club |

<h2 id="steps">How to cancel</h2>

1. **Check your agreement:** rolling monthly or a fixed term, and when your term ends.
2. **Email your home club** with your name, membership number and the date you want to leave.
3. **Keep the reply** with the date your membership ends.
4. **Stop your direct debit** only after your last payment.

<h2 id="when">Good to know</h2>

- Each club is a franchise, so details can differ. Check your own agreement.
- You can't cancel while your membership is frozen. Unfreeze first.
