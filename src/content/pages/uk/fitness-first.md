<h2 id="prices">Membership and prices</h2>

Your membership tier decides which clubs you can use: every club at your tier or below. A Single Club membership gives access to one club only.

An example: at Fitness First London Liverpool Street, a membership cost this in October 2026:

| Membership | Per month |
| --- | --- |
| Single Club | £70 |
| Classic (multi-club access) | £90 |

<p class="klein">Check the price for your club on the Fitness First website.</p>

<h2 id="what-you-get">What you get</h2>

- **Multi-club access:** every club at your membership tier or below
- **Classes:** group classes across the network
- **Coaching:** personal trainers on hand

<h2 id="cancelling">Contract and cancelling</h2>

<div class="kolommen">

<div class="kolom">

### During your minimum term

Give one calendar month's notice to end your membership at the end of your minimum term. Leaving earlier means paying the rest of the term, or a settlement fee for the discount you had.

</div>

<div class="kolom">

### After your minimum term

Cancel any time with one month's notice. Changed your mind straight away? You can cancel within 7 days of joining at no cost.

</div>

</div>

How to cancel online, what leaving early costs and how freezing works: [Cancel Fitness First](/uk/cancel-fitness-first/).

## Frequently asked questions

<details open>
<summary>How much does Fitness First cost?</summary>

It depends on the club and your membership tier. At London Liverpool Street a membership cost £70 a month (Single Club) or £90 (Classic, multi-club access) in October 2026.

</details>

<details>
<summary>How do I cancel my Fitness First membership?</summary>

Give one calendar month's notice to end at your minimum term. After that, you can cancel any time with one month's notice. Within 7 days of joining, you can cancel at no cost. More in [Cancel Fitness First](/uk/cancel-fitness-first/).

</details>

<details>
<summary>Can I use every Fitness First club?</summary>

You can use every club at your membership tier or below. A Single Club membership gives access to one club only.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Can I try Fitness First before joining?</summary>

Yes, with a guest pass you can visit a club first.

</details>
