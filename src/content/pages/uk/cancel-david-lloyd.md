You cancel David Lloyd in writing: via the app, by email, by post or at the club. With a Standard membership you're tied to the first 12 months; with Flexible, to the first 3 months. After that, the notice period is three calendar months (Standard) or one calendar month (Flexible).

<h2 id="summary">In short</h2>

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Where** | In writing: via the app, by email, by post or at the club |
| **Standard** | Initial 12 months. Stop then with one month's notice before the last month starts; later three months' notice |
| **Flexible** | Initial 3 months, then one calendar month's notice |
| **Freeze** | Once a year for 2 to 9 months, at 25% of your fee; free with a medical reason |
| **Early exit** | With proof, for example a medical condition, losing your job or moving more than 10 miles from a club |

<h2 id="steps">How to cancel</h2>

1. **Check your membership:** Standard or Flexible, and when your initial term ends.
2. **Send your cancellation in writing**, via the app, by email, by post or at the club.
3. **Count the notice period** and keep the confirmation.

<h2 id="when">Good to know</h2>

- Want to stop after the first 12 months of a Standard membership? Give notice before the last month starts, or you're on three months' notice.
- A freeze in your first term makes that term longer.
- Cancelling your direct debit with your bank is not the same as cancelling your membership. Cancel with the gym first, then stop the payment.
