## ClassPass in Leeds

### Where

A good mix of boutique studios and gyms, mostly in the city centre, Chapel Allerton, Kirkstall and Horsforth, and also in Pudsey and the wider area. Here is a small selection. For indoor cycling there is RYDE, and for reformer Pilates there are The Wellness Bar, Urth Pilates and Project:Reform. You can do yoga at Yoga Kula, WellNest and Shadwell Studio, CrossFit at FORM and trampoline and cardio classes at ((BOUNCE)). HiiCLUB on Briggate offers workouts. For gym visits there are Nuffield Health, Anytime Fitness and The Edge at the University of Leeds, which has a pool and a climbing wall.

[See the studios in Leeds on ClassPass.](https://classpass.com/search/leeds/fitness-classes/6DyNrfEcKjm)

In Leeds the standard plan costs £49 a month for 36 credits, and you can try it first with 14 days free and 10 workouts. How credits work: [ClassPass credits explained](/guides/classpass-credits/).
