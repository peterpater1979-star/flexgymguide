Freedom Leisure runs leisure centres for local councils, and the membership terms differ per area. The centre pages don't show how to cancel, so contact your home centre and cancel in writing.

<h2 id="summary">In short</h2>

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Where** | Your home centre; cancel in writing, by email or letter |
| **Notice** | See your membership agreement or ask your centre |
| **Annual membership** | Usually runs until the end of the year you paid for |

<h2 id="steps">How to cancel</h2>

1. **Check your agreement** for the notice period and whether you pay monthly or yearly.
2. **Email or write to your home centre** with your name, membership number and the date you want to leave.
3. **Keep the confirmation** with your end date.
4. **Stop your direct debit** only after your last payment.

<h2 id="when">Good to know</h2>

- Cancelling in writing, by email or letter, always gives you proof of the date. Keep a copy.
- Cancelling your direct debit with your bank is not the same as cancelling your membership.

## Frequently asked questions

<details open>
<summary>How do I cancel Freedom Leisure?</summary>

Contact your home centre and cancel in writing, by email or letter. Check the notice period in your agreement.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Does the same apply at every centre?</summary>

No. Memberships and terms are set per area, for example Brighton and Hove or Wealden.

</details>
