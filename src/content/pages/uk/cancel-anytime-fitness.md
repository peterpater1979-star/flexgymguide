You cancel Anytime Fitness with the online cancellation form on your club's page, or at the club. Every club is a franchise, so the notice period depends on your club. A 3, 6 or 12-month agreement runs until the end of its term.

<h2 id="summary">In short</h2>

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Where** | Online cancellation form on your club's page, or at the club |
| **Fixed term** | 3, 6 or 12 months: runs until the end of the term |
| **Rolling monthly** | Notice period set by your club; one calendar month in Manchester Central |
| **Freeze** | Ask your club; rules and cost depend on the club |

<h2 id="steps">How to cancel</h2>

1. **Check your agreement:** fixed term or rolling monthly, and what notice your club asks for.
2. **Fill in the cancellation form** on your club's page, or cancel at the club.
3. **Keep the confirmation** and check the date your membership ends.

<h2 id="when">Good to know</h2>

- Cancelling your direct debit with your bank is not the same as cancelling your membership. Cancel with the gym first, then stop the payment.
- Cancelling in writing, by email or letter, always gives you proof of the date. Keep a copy and the confirmation.
