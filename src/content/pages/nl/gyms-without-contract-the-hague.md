<h2 id="classpass">ClassPass: cancel any time</h2>

With ClassPass you get credits every month for classes and gym visits at studios across The Hague. You are not tied down: you cancel whenever you want and keep training until the end of your month.

- **Plans in The Hague:** the standard plan is 40 credits for €59 a month. There are smaller and bigger plans in the app. See [ClassPass credits explained](/guides/classpass-credits/).
- **Try it first:** 14 days free with 10 workouts. Read [Trying ClassPass for free](/guides/classpass-free-trial/).
- **Travelling a lot?** Your plan also works in other cities. Read [ClassPass while travelling](/guides/classpass-while-travelling/).

<h2 id="trainmore">Trainmore Flex: cancel monthly</h2>

Trainmore is a premium gym chain with group classes and clubs in the city centre. In The Hague there are clubs at Dagelijkse Groenmarkt (Regular), Laan van NOI and De Savornin Lohmanplein (both Black Label), among others. Besides a contract of 1 or 2 years there is Flex, which you cancel monthly. Flex costs more per 4 weeks, but you are not tied down. The price depends on the label of your club. This is what prices looked like in October 2026, per 4 weeks:

| Term | Regular: Dagelijkse Groenmarkt (Home) | Black Label: Laan van NOI (Home+) | Black Label: Savornin (Home+) | City+ |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Flex | €79 | €96 | €99 | €106 |
| 1 year | €59 | €76 | €79 | €86 |
| 2 years | €52 | €69 | €72 | €79 |

At a Regular club you only train at your own club. With Home+ you can also train at the other clubs that cost the same or less: with Home+ at Savornin that includes Laan van NOI and the Regular clubs. With City+ you can train at all clubs in The Hague; it cost the same at all three clubs.

**Student?** A student membership costs €45 per 4 weeks at Dagelijkse Groenmarkt, €62 at Laan van NOI and €65 at Savornin, with a 1-year contract and no startup costs. The discount per workout doesn't apply there.

**A discount when you train more.** With a 1- or 2-year contract you get €1 off per workout, up to €28 per 4 weeks; not with Flex. At Dagelijkse Groenmarkt, for example, a 1-year contract then costs €47 instead of €59. How it works: see [Gyms without a contract in the Netherlands](/nl/gyms-without-contract/#trainmore).

With Flex you don't get the member discount at Trainmore's partner brands, but you do get the training plan in the app. With a 2-year contract there are no startup costs. You cancel in the app; see [Cancelling Trainmore](/nl/cancel-trainmore/).

<h2 id="basic-fit">Basic-Fit Flex: no minimum term</h2>

Basic-Fit has Flex with no minimum term: €9.99 per 4 weeks more than the yearly contract, so from €34.98 per 4 weeks, plus a one-off €19.99 joining fee. You cancel with 4 weeks' notice. The differences between Comfort, Premium and Ultimate are in [Gyms without a contract in the Netherlands](/nl/gyms-without-contract/#basic-fit). Cancelling: see [Cancelling Basic-Fit](/nl/cancel-basic-fit/).

<h2 id="sportcity">SportCity Flex: cancel monthly</h2>

SportCity has clubs all over the Netherlands, including The Hague. You choose between City One (one club) and City Plus (121 clubs in the Netherlands, and bring someone along five times a month). Both come as Flex, 3 months, 1 year or 2 years, with all live group classes included and a 14-day cooling-off period.

This is what prices looked like in October 2026 at SportCity Den Haag Hollands Spoor, per 4 weeks:

| Term | City One | City Plus |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Flex | €37.99 | €39.99 |
| 3 months | €35.99 | €37.99 |
| 1 year | €32.99 | €33.99 |
| 2 years | €29.99 | €30.99 |

With Flex you pay a €9.99 joining fee. Other clubs can be more expensive. Want to try first? You can ask the club for a day pass.

<p class="klein">Source: sportcity.nl/lidmaatschap and the sign-up page for Den Haag Hollands Spoor (October 2026). Always check the current price with SportCity. Cancelling: see <a href="/nl/cancel-sportcity/">Cancelling SportCity</a>.</p>

## Frequently asked questions

<details open>
<summary>Can I join a gym in The Hague without a contract?</summary>

Yes. With ClassPass you cancel any time, Trainmore, Basic-Fit and SportCity have a Flex membership without a long term, and many studios sell drop-in classes.

</details>

<details>
<summary>I am posted to The Hague for a year or two. What should I choose?</summary>

Pick something you can stop when your posting ends. ClassPass has no notice period. Trainmore Flex and SportCity Flex can be cancelled monthly, and Basic-Fit Flex has 4 weeks' notice. Cancel in time before you leave.

</details>

<details>
<summary>I am only in The Hague for a few months. What should I choose?</summary>

Pick something you can cancel quickly. ClassPass has no notice period, so you can stop at the end of any month. With a Flex gym membership, check the notice period and cancel in time before you leave.

</details>
