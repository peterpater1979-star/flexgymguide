<h2 id="classpass">ClassPass: cancel any time</h2>

With ClassPass you get credits every month for classes and gym visits at studios across Haarlem. You are not tied down: you cancel whenever you want and keep training until the end of your month. Which studios there are, from reformer Pilates to kickboxing, is on [ClassPass in Haarlem](/nl/classpass-haarlem/) (in Dutch).

- **Plans in Haarlem:** the standard plan is 47 credits for €69 a month, the same as in Amsterdam. There are smaller and bigger plans in the app. See [ClassPass credits explained](/guides/classpass-credits/).
- **Try it first:** 14 days free with 10 workouts. Read [Trying ClassPass for free](/guides/classpass-free-trial/).
- **Travelling a lot?** Your plan also works in other cities. Read [ClassPass while travelling](/guides/classpass-while-travelling/).

<h2 id="trainmore">Trainmore Flex: cancel monthly</h2>

Trainmore is a premium gym chain with group classes and clubs in the city centre. It has 1 club in Haarlem. Besides a contract of 1 or 2 years there is Flex, which you cancel monthly. Flex costs more per 4 weeks, but you are not tied down. This is what prices looked like in October 2026 at Trainmore Haarlem Hortusplein (Black Label), with Home+ (your own club plus all Regular clubs), per 4 weeks:

| Term | Price |
| --- | --- |
| Flex | €92 |
| 1 year | €72 |
| 2 years | €65 |

**A discount when you train more.** With a 1- or 2-year contract you get €1 off per workout, up to €28 per 4 weeks; not with Flex. At Hortusplein, for example, a 1-year contract then costs €60 instead of €72. How it works: see [Gyms without a contract in the Netherlands](/nl/gyms-without-contract/#trainmore).

With Flex you don't get the member discount at Trainmore's partner brands, but you do get the training plan in the app. With a 2-year contract there are no startup costs. You cancel in the app; see [Cancelling Trainmore](/nl/cancel-trainmore/).

<h2 id="basic-fit">Basic-Fit Flex: no minimum term</h2>

Basic-Fit has Flex with no minimum term: €9.99 per 4 weeks more than the yearly contract, so from €34.98 per 4 weeks, plus a one-off €19.99 joining fee. You cancel with 4 weeks' notice. The differences between Comfort, Premium and Ultimate are in [Gyms without a contract in the Netherlands](/nl/gyms-without-contract/#basic-fit). Cancelling: see [Cancelling Basic-Fit](/nl/cancel-basic-fit/).

## Frequently asked questions

<details open>
<summary>Can I join a gym in Haarlem without a contract?</summary>

Yes. With ClassPass you cancel any time, Trainmore and Basic-Fit have a Flex membership without a long term, and many studios sell drop-in classes.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Can I use ClassPass from Haarlem in Amsterdam too?</summary>

Yes. With the same plan you book classes at studios in Amsterdam and other cities. See [ClassPass in Amsterdam](/nl/classpass-amsterdam/) (in Dutch).

</details>

<details>
<summary>I am only in Haarlem for a few months. What should I choose?</summary>

Pick something you can cancel quickly. ClassPass has no notice period, so you can stop at the end of any month. With a Flex gym membership, check the notice period and cancel in time before you leave.

</details>
