New in the Netherlands for work or study, or not sure where you will live next year? Most Dutch gym chains have a Flex membership that you cancel monthly or every 4 weeks, and with a gym pass you don't need a membership at all. This guide explains the options and what to watch for before you sign up.

<h2 id="short">In short</h2>

| | Cancelling | Price | Good for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **ClassPass** | Any time, no notice period | Plans with monthly credits, standard plan for example €59 a month | Variety at studios and gyms |
| **Trainmore Flex** | Monthly | Depends on the club label (Black, Red, Regular) | Premium gym with group classes |
| **Basic-Fit Flex** | 4 weeks' notice | From €34.98 per 4 weeks, plus a €19.99 joining fee | Cheap and frequent training, many clubs open 24/7 |
| **SportCity Flex** | Monthly | Depends on the club | Gym with all live group classes |
| **BigGym Flex** | Every 4 weeks | For example €45 per 4 weeks in Utrecht (Unlimited Flex) | Large gyms with strength, cardio and classes |
| **Drop-in class or class card** | Not needed | Varies per studio | Now and then at a favourite studio |

<p class="klein">Prices from October and November 2026, from our pages about each provider. Prices can differ per club. Always check the current price with the provider.</p>

<h2 id="classpass">ClassPass: cancel any time</h2>

With ClassPass you get credits every month for classes and gym visits at studios in your city. You cancel any time, with no notice period, and your plan also works in other cities and countries. Read [How ClassPass works](/guides/how-classpass-works/).

<h2 id="trainmore">Trainmore Flex: cancel monthly</h2>

Trainmore has 53 clubs in the Netherlands, labelled Black, Red or Regular; the label sets the price. With Flex you cancel monthly; a 1-year contract is €20 per 4 weeks cheaper, 2 years €27.

**A discount when you train more.** With a 1 or 2-year contract you get €1 off per workout, at most one a day and €28 per 4 weeks; not with Flex, and not with student memberships. You check in and out with the QR code in the Trainmore app and train for at least 30 minutes; a group class on its own doesn't count. Train three times a week and that saves about €12 per 4 weeks.

<h2 id="basic-fit">Basic-Fit Flex: no minimum term</h2>

Basic-Fit is a budget chain with many clubs that are open day and night. Besides a 52-week contract there is Flex, with no minimum term. Flex costs €9.99 per 4 weeks more than the yearly contract, and welcome offers for yearly contracts don't apply.

| Flex | Per 4 weeks | What you get |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Comfort | €34.98 | All clubs in the Netherlands |
| Premium | €39.98 | All clubs in 6 countries, one guest a week, massage chairs |
| Ultimate | €44.98 | All clubs in 6 countries, unlimited guests, sports water, free pausing |

All Flex memberships have a one-off joining fee of €19.99. Group classes are included at clubs that offer them. You cancel through My Basic-Fit, the app or the kiosk in the club, with 4 weeks' notice.

<p class="klein">Source: basic-fit.com/nl-nl/prijs (October 2026). Always check the current price with Basic-Fit. Cancelling: see <a href="/nl/cancel-basic-fit/">Cancelling Basic-Fit</a>.</p>

<h2 id="sportcity">SportCity Flex: cancel monthly</h2>

SportCity has Flex, which you cancel monthly, next to contracts of 3 months, 1 or 2 years. All live group classes are included, and the price differs a lot per club. Cancelling: see [Cancelling SportCity](/nl/cancel-sportcity/).

<h2 id="big-gym">BigGym Flex: cancel every 4 weeks</h2>

BigGym has 16 large gyms in the Netherlands, with strength, cardio and group classes. With Unlimited Flex you cancel every 4 weeks in the BigGym app; in Utrecht it cost €45 per 4 weeks in November 2026, and €30 to €35 with a 1 to 3-year contract. A day pass is also possible.

<h2 id="drop-in">Drop-in classes and class cards</h2>

Many yoga, pilates and boxing studios sell single classes or a card for a number of classes. You only pay when you go, which is handy if you have one favourite studio. Check the studio's website; prices and validity differ.

<h2 id="tips">Tips if you are new in the Netherlands</h2>

- **Websites in Dutch.** Most gym websites have an English version, often behind a language switch at the top. Sign-up pages are sometimes only in Dutch.
- **Per 4 weeks, not per month.** Many Dutch gyms charge every 4 weeks, so you pay 13 times a year.
- **Direct debit.** Gyms usually collect payment by direct debit, so have your bank account number (IBAN) ready.
- **Notice period.** "Cancel monthly" can mean you still pay one more period. Read the terms before you leave the country.
- **How often you go.** If you train three times a week or more at the same place, a fixed contract is often cheaper. Read [Gym pass or membership?](/guides/gym-pass-or-membership/)

## Frequently asked questions

<details open>
<summary>Can I join a gym in the Netherlands without a contract?</summary>

Yes. ClassPass, Trainmore Flex, Basic-Fit Flex, SportCity Flex and BigGym Flex all work without a yearly contract, and many studios sell drop-in classes.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Can I combine ClassPass with a gym membership?</summary>

Yes. Many people train at a gym and book a class through ClassPass now and then, for something their gym does not offer.

</details>
