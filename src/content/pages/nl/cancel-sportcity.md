You cancel SportCity in the app, by emailing your club or in the club itself. The notice period is one month. If your membership has a term of one or two years, cancel at least one month before that term ends.

<h2 id="short">In short</h2>

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Where** | In the app (Account, Membership), by email to your club, or in the club |
| **Notice period** | One month |
| **With a term** | Cancel at least one month before the end of the term |
| **After the term** | Your membership turns into Flex, which you can cancel monthly |
| **Flex** | Cancel any time, with one month's notice |
| **Just joined?** | 14 days to withdraw or stop |

<h2 id="steps">How to cancel</h2>

1. **Open the SportCity app** and go to Account, then Membership. You cancel there.
2. **Prefer email?** Email your club and include your member number. You find it in the app under Account and Membership. Your club's email address is in the club overview on sportcity.nl.
3. **Or drop by** your club and tell the front desk.
4. **Keep the confirmation** and check the end date.

Watch out for cancellation services from other companies: they don't work with SportCity and may charge you.

<h2 id="term">When can you stop?</h2>

- **Membership with a term.** If you cancel at least one month before the end of the term, it stops on the end date. If you don't, it continues and you lose your discount: it turns into a Flex membership.
- **Flex.** No fixed term: you cancel any time, with one month's notice. Flex does cost more than a membership with a term.
- **Regret after joining?** Within 14 days of joining you can withdraw with the withdrawal form, which you email as a PDF to your home club. If you have already trained, SportCity can charge a pro rata amount. SportCity also has a satisfaction guarantee: within 14 days you may stop if it isn't for you.

Moving, a medical reason or freezing your membership? SportCity's cancellation page doesn't cover these. Ask your club or check the terms and conditions.

## Frequently asked questions

<details open>
<summary>How long is the notice period at SportCity?</summary>

One month. With a membership with a term, cancel at least one month before the end of that term.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Can I cancel SportCity in the app?</summary>

Yes, in the app under Account and Membership. You can also email your club or drop by.

</details>

<details>
<summary>What happens if I don't cancel in time?</summary>

Your membership continues as a Flex membership, without your discount. You can then cancel any time with one month's notice.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Can I stop if I have just joined?</summary>

Yes. Within 14 days of joining you can withdraw. If you have already trained, SportCity can charge a pro rata amount.

</details>
