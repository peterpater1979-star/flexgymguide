<h2 id="classpass">ClassPass: cancel any time</h2>

With ClassPass you get credits every month for classes and gym visits at studios across Amsterdam. Many studios also teach in English. You are not tied down: you cancel whenever you want and keep training until the end of your month.

- **Plans in Amsterdam:** from 11 credits for €19 to 140 credits for €199 a month. The standard plan is 47 credits for €69. See [ClassPass credits explained](/guides/classpass-credits/).
- **Try it first:** 14 days free with 10 workouts. Read [Trying ClassPass for free](/guides/classpass-free-trial/).
- **Travelling a lot?** Your plan also works in other cities. Read [ClassPass while travelling](/guides/classpass-while-travelling/).

<h2 id="trainmore">Trainmore Flex: cancel monthly</h2>

Trainmore is a premium gym chain with group classes and clubs in the city centre. It has 31 clubs in Amsterdam. Besides a contract of 1 or 2 years there is Flex, which you cancel monthly. Flex costs more per 4 weeks, but you are not tied down. The price depends on the club's label. This is what prices looked like in October 2026, per 4 weeks:

| Term | Regular (Bos en Lommer) | Black Label (Koningin Wilhelminaplein) | Red Label (Amstelveenseweg) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Flex | €74 | €104 | €116 |
| 1 year | €54 | €84 | €96 |
| 2 years | €47 | €77 | €89 |

**A discount when you train more.** With a 1- or 2-year contract you get €1 off per workout, up to €28 per 4 weeks; not with Flex. At Bos en Lommer, for example, a 1-year contract then costs €42 instead of €54. How it works: see [Gyms without a contract in the Netherlands](/nl/gyms-without-contract/#trainmore).

At a Regular club you train only at your own club. A Red Label membership (Premium) also gives access to all Black Label and Regular clubs. With Flex you pay €29 one-off for the starter package. Students pay €40 per 4 weeks at Bos en Lommer, with a 1-year contract. With Flex you don't get the member discount at Trainmore's partner brands, but you do get the training plan in the app. You cancel in the app; see [Cancelling Trainmore](/nl/cancel-trainmore/).

<h2 id="basic-fit">Basic-Fit Flex: no minimum term</h2>

Basic-Fit has Flex with no minimum term: €9.99 per 4 weeks more than the yearly contract, so from €34.98 per 4 weeks, plus a one-off €19.99 joining fee. You cancel with 4 weeks' notice. The differences between Comfort, Premium and Ultimate are in [Gyms without a contract in the Netherlands](/nl/gyms-without-contract/#basic-fit). Cancelling: see [Cancelling Basic-Fit](/nl/cancel-basic-fit/).

<h2 id="sportcity">SportCity Flex: cancel monthly</h2>

SportCity has clubs all over the Netherlands, including Amsterdam. You choose between City One (one club) and City Plus (121 clubs in the Netherlands, and bring someone along five times a month). Both come as Flex, 3 months, 1 year or 2 years, with all live group classes included and a 14-day cooling-off period.

This is what prices looked like in October 2026 at SportCity Amsterdam Lijnbaansgracht, per 4 weeks:

| Term | City One | City Plus |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Flex | €37.99 | €39.99 |
| 3 months | €35.99 | €37.99 |
| 1 year | €32.99 | €33.99 |
| 2 years | €29.99 | €30.99 |

With Flex you pay a €9.99 joining fee. Prices differ a lot per club: at Amsterdam Weteringdwarsstraat, a few minutes away, City One started at €52.99 per 4 weeks. Want to try first? You can ask the club for a day pass.

<p class="klein">Source: sportcity.nl/lidmaatschap, the Weteringdwarsstraat club page and the sign-up page for Lijnbaansgracht (October 2026). Always check the current price with SportCity. Cancelling: see <a href="/nl/cancel-sportcity/">Cancelling SportCity</a>.</p>

<h2 id="saints-stars">Saints & Stars: Flex or pay per class</h2>

Saints & Stars is a boutique studio with four locations in Amsterdam, offering HIIT, boxing, cycling, strength, yoga and reformer Pilates. With Flex you are not tied down: Gold costs €169 and Premium €229 per 4 weeks, with 4 weeks' notice. Don't want a membership at all? Buy credits: from €20 for one class to €15 per class for 20 classes, valid for 12 months.

<p class="klein">Source: saints-stars.com (November 2026). More in <a href="/nl/saints-stars/">Alles over Saints & Stars</a> (in Dutch).</p>

<h2 id="vondelgym">Vondelgym: per 4 weeks, or with a pass</h2>

Vondelgym has three gyms in Amsterdam, in West, East and South (Zuidas), with a gym floor and group classes such as (kick)boxing, strength, yoga and running. You pay per 4 weeks, for at least two periods, and cancel with one 4-week period of notice. Regular costs €84.50, gym only €61.50 and students pay €46.50, plus a one-off €15 joining fee. Without a membership you can buy a day pass (€19.50), week pass (€62) or month pass (€138).

<p class="klein">Source: vondelgym.nl (November 2026). More in <a href="/nl/vondelgym/">Alles over Vondelgym</a> (in Dutch).</p>

<h2 id="big-gym">BigGym Flex: cancel every 4 weeks</h2>

BigGym has a large gym in Amsterdam, with weights, cardio, a HYROX zone and group classes, and you can train at all 16 BigGyms in the Netherlands. Unlimited Flex costs €45 per 4 weeks and you cancel every 4 weeks, in the BigGym app. With a 1 to 3 year contract you pay €30 to €35. You also pay a one-off €10 activation fee. Want to try first? A day pass costs €10.

<p class="klein">Source: biggym.com (November 2026). More in <a href="/nl/big-gym/">Alles over BigGym</a> (in Dutch).</p>

## Frequently asked questions

<details open>
<summary>Can I join a gym in Amsterdam without a contract?</summary>

Yes. With ClassPass you cancel any time, Trainmore, Basic-Fit and SportCity have a Flex membership without a long term, and many studios sell drop-in classes.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Where can I pay per class in Amsterdam?</summary>

Saints & Stars and Vondelgym also let you train without a membership, with single classes or a pass. With ClassPass you book single classes at studios all over the city without being tied down. See [ClassPass in Amsterdam](/nl/classpass-amsterdam/) (in Dutch).

</details>

<details>
<summary>I am only in Amsterdam for a few months. What should I choose?</summary>

Pick something you can cancel quickly. ClassPass has no notice period, so you can stop at the end of any month. With a Flex gym membership, check the notice period and cancel in time before you leave.

</details>
