Just moved to Amsterdam, here for a few months, or not sure where you will live next year? You don't have to sign a one-year gym contract. These are the options to work out month by month, or only when you go.

<h2 id="short">In short</h2>

| | Cancelling | Price | Good for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **ClassPass** | Any time, no notice period | From €19 a month (11 credits) | Variety at studios and gyms all over the city |
| **Trainmore Flex** | Monthly | €74 to €116 per 4 weeks, depending on the club | Premium gym with group classes, for frequent training |
| **Basic-Fit Flex** | No minimum term, 4 weeks' notice | From €34.98 per 4 weeks, plus €19.99 joining fee | Cheap and frequent training, open 24/7 |
| **SportCity Flex** | Monthly | €37.99 per 4 weeks at Lijnbaansgracht (City One), plus €4.99 joining fee; varies per club | Gym with group classes, day pass available |
| **Drop-in class or class card** | Not needed | Varies per studio | Now and then at a favourite studio |

<p class="klein">Prices from October 2026. ClassPass: location Amsterdam. Trainmore: clubs Bos en Lommer, Koningin Wilhelminaplein and Amstelveenseweg, before discount. Basic-Fit: national prices. SportCity: club Lijnbaansgracht. Always check the current price with the provider.</p>

<h2 id="classpass">ClassPass: cancel any time</h2>

With ClassPass you get credits every month for classes and gym visits at studios across Amsterdam. Many studios also teach in English. You are not tied down: you cancel whenever you want and keep training until the end of your month.

- **Plans in Amsterdam:** from 11 credits for €19 to 140 credits for €199 a month. The standard plan is 47 credits for €69. See [ClassPass credits explained](/guides/classpass-credits/).
- **Try it first:** 14 days free with 10 workouts. Read [Trying ClassPass for free](/guides/classpass-free-trial/).
- **Pausing:** when you cancel, ClassPass often offers a free pause. Read [Cancelling or pausing ClassPass](/guides/cancel-or-pause-classpass/).
- **Travelling a lot?** Your plan also works in other cities. Read [ClassPass while travelling](/guides/classpass-while-travelling/).

<h2 id="trainmore">Trainmore Flex: cancel monthly</h2>

Trainmore is a premium gym chain with group classes and clubs in the city centre. It has 31 clubs in Amsterdam. Besides a contract of 1 or 2 years there is Flex, which you cancel monthly. Flex costs more per 4 weeks, but you are not tied down. The price depends on the club's label. This is what prices looked like in October 2026, per 4 weeks:

| Term | Regular (Bos en Lommer) | Black Label (Koningin Wilhelminaplein) | Red Label (Amstelveenseweg) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Flex | €74 | €104 | €116 |
| 1 year | €54 | €84 | €96 |
| 2 years | €47 | see website | €89 |

**A discount when you train more.** At Trainmore you pay less the more often you go: you get €1 off per workout, at most one a day, with Flex and with a contract. This works at every Trainmore club, whatever the label or contract: train three times a week and that saves about €12 per 4 weeks. At Bos en Lommer, for example, Flex then costs €62 instead of €74. Student memberships don't get this discount.

At a Regular club you train only at your own club. A Red Label membership (Premium) also gives access to all Black Label and Regular clubs. With Flex you pay €29 one-off for the starter package; with a 1 or 2-year contract you often get 50% off for the first months. Students pay €40 per 4 weeks at Bos en Lommer, with a 1-year contract. With Flex you don't get the member discount at Trainmore's partner brands, but you do get the training plan in the app. You cancel in the app.

<h2 id="basic-fit">Basic-Fit Flex: no minimum term</h2>

Basic-Fit is a budget chain with clubs that are open day and night. Besides a 52-week contract there is Flex, with no minimum term. Flex costs €9.99 per 4 weeks more than the yearly contract, and welcome offers for yearly contracts don't apply.

| Flex | Per 4 weeks | What you get |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Comfort | €34.98 | All clubs in the Netherlands |
| Premium | €39.98 | All clubs in Europe, one guest a week, massage chairs |
| Ultimate | €44.98 | All clubs in Europe, unlimited guests, sports water, free pausing |

All Flex memberships have a one-off joining fee of €19.99. Group classes are included at clubs that offer them. You cancel through My Basic-Fit, the app or the kiosk in the club, with 4 weeks' notice.

<p class="klein">Source: <a href="https://www.basic-fit.com/nl-nl/prijs">basic-fit.com/nl-nl/prijs</a> (October 2026). Always check the current price with Basic-Fit.</p>

<h2 id="sportcity">SportCity Flex: cancel monthly</h2>

SportCity has clubs all over the Netherlands, including Amsterdam. You choose between City One (one club) and City Plus (more than 120 clubs in the Netherlands, and bring someone along five times a month). Both come as Flex, 3 months, 1 year or 2 years, with all live group classes included and a 14-day cooling-off period.

This is what prices looked like in October 2026 at SportCity Amsterdam Lijnbaansgracht, per 4 weeks:

| Term | City One | City Plus |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Flex | €37.99 | €39.99 |
| 3 months | €35.99 | €37.99 |
| 1 year | €32.99 | €33.99 |
| 2 years | €29.99 | €30.99 |

With Flex you pay a €4.99 joining fee instead of the usual €9.99. Prices differ a lot per club: at Amsterdam Weteringdwarsstraat, a few minutes away, City One started at €52.99 per 4 weeks. Want to try first? You can ask the club for a day pass.

<p class="klein">Source: <a href="https://www.sportcity.nl/lidmaatschap">sportcity.nl/lidmaatschap</a>, the Weteringdwarsstraat club page and the sign-up page for Lijnbaansgracht (October 2026). Always check the current price with SportCity.</p>

<h2 id="drop-in">Drop-in classes and class cards</h2>

Many yoga, pilates and boxing studios sell single classes or a card for a number of classes. You only pay when you go, which is handy if you have one favourite studio. Check the studio's website; prices and validity differ.

<h2 id="tips">Tips if you are new in the Netherlands</h2>

- **Websites in Dutch.** Most gym websites have an English version, often behind a language switch at the top. Sign-up pages are sometimes only in Dutch.
- **Per 4 weeks, not per month.** Many Dutch gyms charge every 4 weeks, so you pay 13 times a year.
- **Direct debit.** Gyms usually collect payment by direct debit, so have your bank account number (IBAN) ready.
- **Notice period.** "Cancel monthly" can mean you still pay one more period. Read the terms before you leave the country.
- **How often you go.** If you train three times a week or more at the same place, a fixed contract is often cheaper. Read [Gym pass or membership?](/guides/gym-pass-or-membership/)

## Frequently asked questions

<details open>
<summary>Can I join a gym in Amsterdam without a contract?</summary>

Yes. With ClassPass you cancel any time, Trainmore, Basic-Fit and SportCity have a Flex membership without a long term, and many studios sell drop-in classes.

</details>

<details>
<summary>What is the cheapest way to work out without a contract?</summary>

That depends on how often you go. For a few classes a month, a small ClassPass plan or a drop-in class is cheapest. If you often train at one place, a monthly gym membership is usually cheaper per visit.

</details>

<details>
<summary>I am only in Amsterdam for a few months. What should I choose?</summary>

Pick something you can cancel quickly. ClassPass has no notice period, so you can stop at the end of any month. With a Flex gym membership, check the notice period and cancel in time before you leave.

</details>
