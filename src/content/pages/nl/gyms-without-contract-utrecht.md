<h2 id="classpass">ClassPass: cancel any time</h2>

With ClassPass you get credits every month for classes and gym visits at studios across Utrecht. You are not tied down: you cancel whenever you want and keep training until the end of your month.

- **Plans in Utrecht:** the standard plan is 40 credits for €59 a month. There are smaller and bigger plans in the app. See [ClassPass credits explained](/guides/classpass-credits/).
- **Try it first:** 14 days free with 10 workouts. Read [Trying ClassPass for free](/guides/classpass-free-trial/).
- **Travelling a lot?** Your plan also works in other cities. Read [ClassPass while travelling](/guides/classpass-while-travelling/).

<h2 id="trainmore">Trainmore Flex: cancel monthly</h2>

Trainmore is a premium gym chain with group classes and clubs in the city centre. It has 3 clubs in Utrecht, all Black Label. Besides a contract of 1 or 2 years there is Flex, which you cancel monthly. Flex costs more per 4 weeks, but you are not tied down. This is what prices looked like in October 2026 at Trainmore Utrecht Muntgebouw, with Home+ (your own club plus all Regular clubs), per 4 weeks:

| Term | Price |
| --- | --- |
| Flex | €99 |
| 1 year | €79 |
| 2 years | €72 |

**A discount when you train more.** With a 1- or 2-year contract you get €1 off per workout, up to €28 per 4 weeks; not with Flex. At Muntgebouw, for example, a 1-year contract then costs €67 instead of €79. How it works: see [Gyms without a contract in the Netherlands](/nl/gyms-without-contract/#trainmore).

With City+ you can train at all clubs in Utrecht. With Flex you don't get the member discount at Trainmore's partner brands, but you do get the training plan in the app. With a 2-year contract there are no startup costs. You cancel in the app; see [Cancelling Trainmore](/nl/cancel-trainmore/).

<h2 id="basic-fit">Basic-Fit Flex: no minimum term</h2>

Basic-Fit has Flex with no minimum term: €9.99 per 4 weeks more than the yearly contract, so from €34.98 per 4 weeks, plus a one-off €19.99 joining fee. You cancel with 4 weeks' notice. The differences between Comfort, Premium and Ultimate are in [Gyms without a contract in the Netherlands](/nl/gyms-without-contract/#basic-fit). Cancelling: see [Cancelling Basic-Fit](/nl/cancel-basic-fit/).

<h2 id="sportcity">SportCity Flex: cancel monthly</h2>

SportCity has clubs all over the Netherlands, including Utrecht. You choose between City One (one club) and City Plus (121 clubs in the Netherlands, and bring someone along five times a month). Both come as Flex, 3 months, 1 year or 2 years, with all live group classes included and a 14-day cooling-off period.

This is what City Plus prices looked like in October 2026 at SportCity Utrecht Wonderwoods, per 4 weeks:

| Term | City Plus |
| --- | --- |
| Flex | €69.99 |
| 3 months | €66.99 |
| 1 year | €57.99 |
| 2 years | €54.99 |

With Flex you pay a €14.99 joining fee. Prices differ a lot per club: at Amsterdam Lijnbaansgracht, City Plus Flex was €39.99 per 4 weeks. City One is cheaper; check your club's price on the SportCity website. Want to try first? You can ask the club for a day pass.

<p class="klein">Source: sportcity.nl/lidmaatschap and the sign-up pages for Utrecht Wonderwoods and Amsterdam Lijnbaansgracht (October 2026). Always check the current price with SportCity. Cancelling: see <a href="/nl/cancel-sportcity/">Cancelling SportCity</a>.</p>

<h2 id="big-gym">BigGym Flex: cancel every 4 weeks</h2>

BigGym has a large gym in Utrecht, with weights, cardio, a HYROX zone and group classes, and you can train at all 16 BigGyms in the Netherlands. Unlimited Flex costs €45 per 4 weeks and you cancel every 4 weeks, in the BigGym app. With a 1 to 3 year contract you pay €30 to €35. You also pay a one-off €10 activation fee. Want to try first? A day pass costs €10.

<p class="klein">Source: biggym.com (November 2026). More in <a href="/nl/big-gym/">Alles over BigGym</a> (in Dutch).</p>

## Frequently asked questions

<details open>
<summary>Can I join a gym in Utrecht without a contract?</summary>

Yes. With ClassPass you cancel any time, Trainmore, Basic-Fit and SportCity have a Flex membership without a long term, and many studios sell drop-in classes.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Why is Trainmore Flex more expensive in Utrecht?</summary>

All Trainmore clubs in Utrecht are Black Label, the most expensive label. At the Muntgebouw, Flex cost €99 per 4 weeks in October 2026. For something cheaper without a yearly contract, look at BigGym Flex, SportCity Flex or Basic-Fit Flex.

</details>

<details>
<summary>I am only in Utrecht for a few months. What should I choose?</summary>

Pick something you can cancel quickly. ClassPass has no notice period, so you can stop at the end of any month. With a Flex gym membership, check the notice period and cancel in time before you leave.

</details>
