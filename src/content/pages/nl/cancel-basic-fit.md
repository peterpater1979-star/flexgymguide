You cancel Basic-Fit for free via My Basic-Fit, the app or the orange kiosk in the club. The notice period is 4 weeks. If you are still in the first year of a yearly membership, that year has to run out first.

<h2 id="short">In short</h2>

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Where** | My Basic-Fit (website or app), the kiosk in the club, or by email to customer service |
| **Notice period** | 4 weeks |
| **Yearly membership** | First the minimum term of 52 weeks; after that you can cancel any time with 4 weeks' notice |
| **Flex** | Cancel any time, with 4 weeks' notice |
| **Cost** | Free via My Basic-Fit and the kiosk |

<h2 id="steps">How to cancel</h2>

1. **Log in to My Basic-Fit** on the website or in the app, or go to the orange kiosk in your club.
2. **Go to your membership** and cancel there. You also see the end date of your contract.
3. **Keep the confirmation.** Payments only stop after the notice period or at the end of your minimum term.

Prefer email? Include your full name, your member number and the address of your home club. Watch out for cancellation services from other companies: they don't work with Basic-Fit and may charge you, while cancelling itself is free.

<h2 id="term">Yearly or Flex: when can you stop?</h2>

- **Yearly membership.** You are tied in for the first year (52 weeks). If you don't cancel at least 4 weeks before the end, it continues for an indefinite period. From then on you can cancel any time, with 4 weeks' notice.
- **Flex.** No minimum term: you cancel any time, with 4 weeks' notice. Flex costs € 9.99 per 4 weeks more than a yearly membership.
- **Changed your mind after joining online?** You have 14 days to withdraw if you signed up online. This doesn't apply if you joined in a club, or via the app or kiosk.

Changed your mind about cancelling? Until the end date of your contract you can undo it in My Basic-Fit.

<h2 id="pause">Rather take a break?</h2>

- **Freezing.** With Comfort or Premium you can freeze your membership, up to 4 weeks at a time and twice a year. With Ultimate, pausing for free is included. Your term moves up accordingly.
- **Medical.** With a valid medical certificate for at least a month you can suspend your membership, for € 5 admin costs.
- **Moving.** You can keep training at another club. If there is no club within 5 km of your new address, you may cancel early with 4 weeks' notice. You need proof of your new address.

<p class="klein">Source: <a href="https://www.basic-fit.com/nl-nl/opzeggen">basic-fit.com/nl-nl/opzeggen</a> and the Basic-Fit terms and conditions for the Netherlands (version 9 September 2026). Always check the current terms with Basic-Fit.</p>

<h2 id="alternatives">What now? Flexible alternatives</h2>

Leaving Basic-Fit because you want more variety, or because you don't want to be tied down? These options have no long contract:

- **ClassPass:** credits every month for classes and gyms at studios in your city, cancel any time. Read [How ClassPass works](/guides/how-classpass-works/) or [Trying ClassPass for free](/guides/classpass-free-trial/).
- **Month-to-month at another gym:** see the overviews for [Amsterdam](/nl/gyms-without-contract-amsterdam/), [Rotterdam](/nl/gyms-without-contract-rotterdam/), [The Hague](/nl/gyms-without-contract-the-hague/) and [Utrecht](/nl/gyms-without-contract-utrecht/).
- **Not sure what suits you?** Read [Gym pass or membership?](/guides/gym-pass-or-membership/)

## Frequently asked questions

<details open>
<summary>How long is the notice period at Basic-Fit?</summary>

4 weeks. With a yearly membership you also have to finish the minimum term of 52 weeks first.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Can I cancel Basic-Fit monthly?</summary>

With Flex, yes, and with a yearly membership once the first year is over. You then pay for 4 more weeks.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Does cancelling Basic-Fit cost money?</summary>

No, cancelling via My Basic-Fit and the kiosk in the club is free.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Can I undo my cancellation?</summary>

Yes, in My Basic-Fit, until the end date of your contract.

</details>
