You cancel Trainmore in the Trainmore app or through the online contact form. The notice period is one month. With a 1- or 2-year contract, you stop at the end of that term. After that, your membership continues and you can cancel at any time, again with one month's notice.

<h2 id="short">In short</h2>

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Where** | Online only: in the Trainmore app or through the contact form on the website |
| **Notice period** | One month |
| **1 or 2-year contract** | Cancel for the end of the term; after that at any time, with one month's notice |
| **Flex** | Cancel monthly |
| **Cooling-off period** | 14 days after signing up, free of charge |
| **Stopping early** | Only by paying the rest of your contract |

<h2 id="steps">How to cancel</h2>

1. **Open the Trainmore app** and go to your membership. You can also see the end date of your contract there.
2. **Cancel in the app.** If that doesn't work, use the contact form on the Trainmore website. You can't cancel at the desk in the club.
3. **Keep the confirmation.** You keep paying until the end of your notice period or term.

If you don't cancel, you keep paying, even if you no longer go. Leaving the Netherlands? Cancel in time, because moving abroad doesn't end your contract by itself.

<h2 id="term">Contract or Flex: when can you stop?</h2>

- **1-year contract.** You cancel for the end of the year, with one month's notice. If you do nothing, it turns into a membership without an end date, which you can cancel at any time with one month's notice.
- **2-year contract.** After at least one year you can, under conditions, stop or switch to a 1-year contract. You then pay the difference between the lower price of your longer contract and the price you would otherwise have paid, plus any promotions such as free months.
- **Flex.** Cancel monthly. Flex costs more per 4 weeks, but you're not tied down.
- **Stopping earlier within your contract** is only possible by paying the remaining months in one go.
- **Changed your mind after signing up?** Within 14 days you can cancel free of charge. If you have already trained in that time, you pay one period at the Flex rate, or the price of day passes if that is cheaper. Promotions then lapse.

<h2 id="freeze">Rather take a break?</h2>

At Trainmore you can only freeze your membership for medical reasons, by arrangement and with proof. A break lasts at least 1 and at most 6 months, and never more than 6 months in 12 months. The frozen time is added to the end of your contract. You can't freeze during your notice period.

<h2 id="price">Price increases</h2>

If Trainmore raises its prices, you hear about it 30 days in advance by email or in the app. You can then cancel free of charge within 4 weeks. This doesn't apply to the yearly indexation based on inflation (the CPI of Statistics Netherlands).

<p class="klein">Source: Trainmore terms and conditions (last updated 18 June 2026). Always check the current terms with Trainmore.</p>

<h2 id="alternatives">What next? Flexible alternatives</h2>

Stopping because you don't want to be tied down anymore, or because you want more variety? These options have no long contract:

- **Trainmore Flex:** want to stay but not be tied down? After your contract you can switch to Flex.
- **ClassPass:** credits every month for classes and gyms at studios in your city, cancel any time. Read [How ClassPass works](/guides/how-classpass-works/) or [Trying ClassPass for free](/guides/classpass-free-trial/).
- **Monthly gyms in your city:** see the overviews for [Amsterdam](/nl/gyms-without-contract-amsterdam/), [Rotterdam](/nl/gyms-without-contract-rotterdam/), [The Hague](/nl/gyms-without-contract-the-hague/), [Utrecht](/nl/gyms-without-contract-utrecht/), [Eindhoven](/nl/gyms-without-contract-eindhoven/), [Groningen](/nl/gyms-without-contract-groningen/) and [Haarlem](/nl/gyms-without-contract-haarlem/).

## Frequently asked questions

<details open>
<summary>What is the notice period at Trainmore?</summary>

One month. With a 1- or 2-year contract, you also cancel for the end of the term.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Can I cancel Trainmore at the club?</summary>

No. You can only cancel online: in the Trainmore app or through the contact form on the website.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Can I cancel Trainmore early?</summary>

Only if you pay the rest of your contract. With a 2-year contract you can stop after the first year under conditions; you then pay the price difference and any free months.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Can I freeze my Trainmore membership?</summary>

Only for medical reasons, with proof, for 1 to 6 months. The break is added to the end of your contract.

</details>
