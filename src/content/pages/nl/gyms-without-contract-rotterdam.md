<h2 id="classpass">ClassPass: cancel any time</h2>

With ClassPass you get credits every month for classes and gym visits at studios across Rotterdam. You are not tied down: you cancel whenever you want and keep training until the end of your month.

- **Plans in Rotterdam:** the standard plan is 40 credits for €59 a month. There are smaller and bigger plans in the app. See [ClassPass credits explained](/guides/classpass-credits/).
- **Try it first:** 14 days free with 10 workouts. Read [Trying ClassPass for free](/guides/classpass-free-trial/).
- **Pausing:** when you cancel, ClassPass often offers a free pause. Read [Cancelling or pausing ClassPass](/guides/cancel-or-pause-classpass/).
- **Travelling a lot?** Your plan also works in other cities. Read [ClassPass while travelling](/guides/classpass-while-travelling/).

<h2 id="trainmore">Trainmore Flex: cancel monthly</h2>

Trainmore is a premium gym chain with group classes and clubs in the city centre. It has 7 clubs in Rotterdam. Besides a contract of 1 or 2 years there is Flex, which you cancel monthly. Flex costs more per 4 weeks, but you are not tied down. The clubs in Rotterdam are Black Label or Red Label, and prices also differ between clubs with the same label. This is what prices looked like in October 2026 at two Black Label clubs, with Home+ (your own club plus all Regular clubs), per 4 weeks:

| Term | Coolsingel | Meent |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Flex | €89 | €95 |
| 1 year | €69 | €75 |
| 2 years | €62 | €68 |

With a 1 or 2-year contract you get 50% off for the first months; how many depends on the club. At Coolsingel it was 4 months for 1 year and 6 for 2 years, at Meent 5 and 7.

**Red Label costs more.** A Red Label club has one membership, Premium: your own club, all Regular clubs and the Black Label clubs too. At Trainmore Rotterdam Blaak that was €112 per 4 weeks with Flex in October 2026, €92 for 1 year and €85 for 2 years. At Trainmore Rotterdam Wijnhaven, also Red Label, it was €110, €90 and €83.

**Student?** At Coolsingel a student membership costs €55 per 4 weeks, with a 1-year contract and no startup costs. The discount per workout doesn't apply there.

**A discount when you train more.** At Trainmore you pay less the more often you go: you get €1 off per workout, at most one a day and €28 per 4 weeks, only with a 1- or 2-year contract, not with Flex. You check in and out with the QR code in the Trainmore app and train for at least 30 minutes; a group class on its own doesn't count. Every visit counts on its own, so you don't need a fixed schedule. This works at every Trainmore club: train three times a week and that saves about €12 per 4 weeks. At Coolsingel, for example, a 1-year contract then costs €57 instead of €69. Student memberships don't get it either.

With City+ you can train at all Black Label clubs in Rotterdam. It costs €86 per 4 weeks for 1 year and €79 for 2 years; there is no Flex with City+. With Flex you don't get the member discount at Trainmore's partner brands, but you do get the training plan in the app. With a 2-year contract there are no startup costs. You cancel in the app; see [Cancelling Trainmore](/nl/cancel-trainmore/).

<h2 id="basic-fit">Basic-Fit Flex: no minimum term</h2>

Basic-Fit is a budget chain with clubs that are open day and night. Besides a 52-week contract there is Flex, with no minimum term. Flex costs €9.99 per 4 weeks more than the yearly contract, and welcome offers for yearly contracts don't apply.

| Flex | Per 4 weeks | What you get |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Comfort | €34.98 | All clubs in the Netherlands |
| Premium | €39.98 | All clubs in Europe, one guest a week, massage chairs |
| Ultimate | €44.98 | All clubs in Europe, unlimited guests, sports water, free pausing |

All Flex memberships have a one-off joining fee of €19.99. Group classes are included at clubs that offer them. You cancel through My Basic-Fit, the app or the kiosk in the club, with 4 weeks' notice.

<p class="klein">Source: <a href="https://www.basic-fit.com/nl-nl/prijs">basic-fit.com/nl-nl/prijs</a> (October 2026). Always check the current price with Basic-Fit. Cancelling: see <a href="/nl/cancel-basic-fit/">Cancelling Basic-Fit</a>.</p>

<h2 id="sportcity">SportCity Flex: cancel monthly</h2>

SportCity has clubs all over the Netherlands, including Rotterdam. You choose between City One (one club) and City Plus (85 clubs in the Netherlands, and bring someone along five times a month). Both come as Flex, 3 months, 1 year or 2 years, with all live group classes included and a 14-day cooling-off period.

This is what City One prices looked like in October 2026 at SportCity Rotterdam Conradstraat, per 4 weeks:

| Term | City One |
| --- | --- |
| Flex | €37.99 |
| 3 months | €35.99 |
| 1 year | €32.99 |
| 2 years | €29.99 |

With Flex you pay a €4.99 joining fee instead of the usual €9.99. City Plus costs a bit more, and other clubs can be more expensive too. Want to try first? You can ask the club for a day pass.

<p class="klein">Source: <a href="https://www.sportcity.nl/lidmaatschap">sportcity.nl/lidmaatschap</a> and the sign-up page for Rotterdam Conradstraat (October 2026). Always check the current price with SportCity. Cancelling: see <a href="/nl/cancel-sportcity/">Cancelling SportCity</a>.</p>

<h2 id="drop-in">Drop-in classes and class cards</h2>

Many yoga, pilates and boxing studios sell single classes or a card for a number of classes. You only pay when you go, which is handy if you have one favourite studio. Check the studio's website; prices and validity differ.

<h2 id="tips">Tips if you are new in the Netherlands</h2>

- **Websites in Dutch.** Most gym websites have an English version, often behind a language switch at the top. Sign-up pages are sometimes only in Dutch.
- **Per 4 weeks, not per month.** Many Dutch gyms charge every 4 weeks, so you pay 13 times a year.
- **Direct debit.** Gyms usually collect payment by direct debit, so have your bank account number (IBAN) ready.
- **Notice period.** "Cancel monthly" can mean you still pay one more period. Read the terms before you leave the country.
- **How often you go.** If you train three times a week or more at the same place, a fixed contract is often cheaper. Read [Gym pass or membership?](/guides/gym-pass-or-membership/)

## Frequently asked questions

<details open>
<summary>Can I join a gym in Rotterdam without a contract?</summary>

Yes. With ClassPass you cancel any time, Trainmore, Basic-Fit and SportCity have a Flex membership without a long term, and many studios sell drop-in classes.

</details>

<details>
<summary>What is the cheapest way to work out without a contract?</summary>

That depends on how often you go. For a few classes a month, a small ClassPass plan or a drop-in class is cheapest. If you often train at one place, a monthly gym membership is usually cheaper per visit.

</details>

<details>
<summary>I am only in Rotterdam for a few months. What should I choose?</summary>

Pick something you can cancel quickly. ClassPass has no notice period, so you can stop at the end of any month. With a Flex gym membership, check the notice period and cancel in time before you leave.

</details>
