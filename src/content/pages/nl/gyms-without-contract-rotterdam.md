<h2 id="classpass">ClassPass: cancel any time</h2>

With ClassPass you get credits every month for classes and gym visits at studios across Rotterdam. You are not tied down: you cancel whenever you want and keep training until the end of your month.

- **Plans in Rotterdam:** the standard plan is 40 credits for €59 a month. There are smaller and bigger plans in the app. See [ClassPass credits explained](/guides/classpass-credits/).
- **Try it first:** 14 days free with 10 workouts. Read [Trying ClassPass for free](/guides/classpass-free-trial/).
- **Travelling a lot?** Your plan also works in other cities. Read [ClassPass while travelling](/guides/classpass-while-travelling/).

<h2 id="trainmore">Trainmore Flex: cancel monthly</h2>

Trainmore is a premium gym chain with group classes and clubs in the city centre. It has 7 clubs in Rotterdam. Besides a contract of 1 or 2 years there is Flex, which you cancel monthly. Flex costs more per 4 weeks, but you are not tied down. The clubs in Rotterdam are Black Label or Red Label, and prices also differ between clubs with the same label. This is what prices looked like in October 2026 at two Black Label clubs, with Home+ (your own club plus all Regular clubs), per 4 weeks:

| Term | Coolsingel | Meent |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Flex | €89 | €95 |
| 1 year | €69 | €75 |
| 2 years | €62 | €68 |

**Red Label costs more.** A Red Label club has one membership, Premium: your own club, all Regular clubs and the Black Label clubs too. At Trainmore Rotterdam Blaak that was €112 per 4 weeks with Flex in October 2026, €92 for 1 year and €85 for 2 years. At Trainmore Rotterdam Wijnhaven, also Red Label, it was €110, €90 and €83.

**Student?** At Coolsingel a student membership costs €55 per 4 weeks, with a 1-year contract and no startup costs. The discount per workout doesn't apply there.

**A discount when you train more.** With a 1- or 2-year contract you get €1 off per workout, up to €28 per 4 weeks; not with Flex. At Coolsingel, for example, a 1-year contract then costs €57 instead of €69. How it works: see [Gyms without a contract in the Netherlands](/nl/gyms-without-contract/#trainmore).

With City+ you can train at all Black Label clubs in Rotterdam. It costs €86 per 4 weeks for 1 year and €79 for 2 years; there is no Flex with City+. With Flex you don't get the member discount at Trainmore's partner brands, but you do get the training plan in the app. With a 2-year contract there are no startup costs. You cancel in the app; see [Cancelling Trainmore](/nl/cancel-trainmore/).

<h2 id="basic-fit">Basic-Fit Flex: no minimum term</h2>

Basic-Fit has Flex with no minimum term: €9.99 per 4 weeks more than the yearly contract, so from €34.98 per 4 weeks, plus a one-off €19.99 joining fee. You cancel with 4 weeks' notice. The differences between Comfort, Premium and Ultimate are in [Gyms without a contract in the Netherlands](/nl/gyms-without-contract/#basic-fit). Cancelling: see [Cancelling Basic-Fit](/nl/cancel-basic-fit/).

<h2 id="sportcity">SportCity Flex: cancel monthly</h2>

SportCity has clubs all over the Netherlands, including Rotterdam. You choose between City One (one club) and City Plus (121 clubs in the Netherlands, and bring someone along five times a month). Both come as Flex, 3 months, 1 year or 2 years, with all live group classes included and a 14-day cooling-off period.

This is what City One prices looked like in October 2026 at SportCity Rotterdam Conradstraat, per 4 weeks:

| Term | City One |
| --- | --- |
| Flex | €37.99 |
| 3 months | €35.99 |
| 1 year | €32.99 |
| 2 years | €29.99 |

With Flex you pay a €9.99 joining fee. City Plus costs a bit more, and other clubs can be more expensive too. Want to try first? You can ask the club for a day pass.

<p class="klein">Source: sportcity.nl/lidmaatschap and the sign-up page for Rotterdam Conradstraat (October 2026). Always check the current price with SportCity. Cancelling: see <a href="/nl/cancel-sportcity/">Cancelling SportCity</a>.</p>

<h2 id="big-gym">BigGym Flex: cancel every 4 weeks</h2>

BigGym has a large gym in Rotterdam, with weights, cardio, a HYROX zone and group classes, and you can train at all 16 BigGyms in the Netherlands. Unlimited Flex costs €45 per 4 weeks and you cancel every 4 weeks, in the BigGym app. With a 1 to 3 year contract you pay €30 to €35. You also pay a one-off €10 activation fee. Want to try first? A day pass costs €10.

<p class="klein">Source: biggym.com (November 2026). More in <a href="/nl/big-gym/">Alles over BigGym</a> (in Dutch).</p>

## Frequently asked questions

<details open>
<summary>Can I join a gym in Rotterdam without a contract?</summary>

Yes. With ClassPass you cancel any time, Trainmore, Basic-Fit and SportCity have a Flex membership without a long term, and many studios sell drop-in classes.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Which gym in Rotterdam can I cancel every 4 weeks?</summary>

BigGym Unlimited Flex can be cancelled every 4 weeks in the app. Trainmore Flex and SportCity Flex can be cancelled monthly, and Basic-Fit Flex has 4 weeks' notice. ClassPass can be cancelled any time.

</details>

<details>
<summary>I am only in Rotterdam for a few months. What should I choose?</summary>

Pick something you can cancel quickly. ClassPass has no notice period, so you can stop at the end of any month. With a Flex gym membership, check the notice period and cancel in time before you leave.

</details>
